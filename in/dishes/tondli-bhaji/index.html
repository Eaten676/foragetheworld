Tondli bhaji

Tondli bhaji is made with wild ivy gourd, a favourite in West coast and Deccan and South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make tondli bhaji?

Ivy gourd slices cooked with peanuts, coconut and goda masala.

Wild ingredients

  • Ivy gourd (Coccinia grandis): in season March to November
