Crowberry

Empetrum nigrum
Also called Kråkbär

Crowberry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to September across Sweden. Low heather-like evergreen mat with needle leaves and shiny black berries, on moors and mountains.

When is crowberry in season in Sweden?

Crowberry is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does crowberry grow in Sweden?

Crowberry grows across Sweden. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle), Norrland (north).

How do you recognise crowberry?

Low heather-like evergreen mat with needle leaves and shiny black berries, on moors and mountains.

How do you harvest crowberry?

Strip the black berries from the mats with a berry comb or by hand.

How do you eat crowberry?

Juice and jelly, mixed with sweeter berries.

How do you store crowberry?

Juice or freeze.

What is crowberry good for?

Vitamin C.
