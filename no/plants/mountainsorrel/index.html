Mountain sorrel

Oxyria digyna
Also called Fjellsyre

Mountain sorrel is a wild green you can forage from June to August across Norway. Kidney-shaped, juicy leaves along mountain streams and screes, with reddish flower spikes.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; moderate amounts.

When is mountain sorrel in season in Norway?

Mountain sorrel is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mountain sorrel grow in Norway?

Mountain sorrel grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise mountain sorrel?

Kidney-shaped, juicy leaves along mountain streams and screes, with reddish flower spikes.

How do you harvest mountain sorrel?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat mountain sorrel?

Raw, lemony, in salads and with fish.

How do you store mountain sorrel?

Best fresh.

What is mountain sorrel good for?

Vitamin C.
