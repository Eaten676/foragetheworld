Rowan berries

Sorbus aucuparia
Also called Vogelbeere, Eberesche

Rowan berries is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October across Germany. Tree with feathery leaves of many toothed leaflets and big clusters of orange-red berries.

Caution. Never eat raw; always cook (raw berries upset the stomach).

When is rowan berries in season in Germany?

Rowan berries is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does rowan berries grow in Germany?

Rowan berries grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise rowan berries?

Tree with feathery leaves of many toothed leaflets and big clusters of orange-red berries.

How do you harvest rowan berries?

Cut whole clusters when bright orange-red; freezing them overnight reduces the bitterness.

How do you eat rowan berries?

Cooked jelly with apple, for game.

How do you store rowan berries?

Freeze before cooking to reduce bitterness.

What is rowan berries good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with rowan berries?

  • Rowan jelly (Vogelbeergelee): Rowan with apple, set into jelly for game.
