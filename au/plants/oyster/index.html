Sydney rock oysters

Saccostrea glomerata
Also called Sydney rock oysters

Sydney rock oysters is a wild protein you can forage from May to September in Subtropical east coast and Temperate south-east. Craggy oysters on estuary rocks.

Caution. Wild oyster collection is banned or limited in many places; check state rules and closures.

When is sydney rock oysters in season in Australia?

Sydney rock oysters is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sydney rock oysters grow in Australia?

Sydney rock oysters grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Victoria. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east.

How do you recognise sydney rock oysters?

Craggy oysters on estuary rocks.

How do you harvest sydney rock oysters?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat sydney rock oysters?

Raw with finger lime, or grilled.

How do you store sydney rock oysters?

Eat the same day.

What is sydney rock oysters good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with sydney rock oysters?

  • Oysters with lemon aspen: Rock oysters with a lemon aspen dressing.
