Tikhur

Curcuma angustifolia
Also called Tikhur, Jangli aloo, East Indian arrowroot

Tikhur is a root or shoot you can forage from December to February in Central forests and East. Wild turmeric relative of central Indian forests. Starch is washed out of the rhizomes.

When is tikhur in season in India?

Tikhur is usually in season December to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does tikhur grow in India?

Tikhur grows in Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: Central forests, East.

How do you recognise tikhur?

Wild turmeric relative of central Indian forests. Starch is washed out of the rhizomes.

How do you harvest tikhur?

Dig rhizomes in winter after the leaves dry, and replant a few small pieces.

How do you eat tikhur?

Tikhur halwa, barfi, or a cooling drink.

How do you store tikhur?

Grate, wash, settle and dry the starch. Keeps a year.

What is tikhur good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with tikhur?

  • Tikhur cooler: Stir a spoon of tikhur starch into cold water or milk with sugar. A traditional summer cooler in Chhattisgarh.
  • Tikhur barfi: Tikhur starch cooked with milk and sugar until it sets, then cut into squares. Chhattisgarh's festival sweet.
