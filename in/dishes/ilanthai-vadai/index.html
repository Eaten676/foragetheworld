Ilanthai vadai

Ilanthai vadai is made with wild ber, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make ilanthai vadai?

Dried ber pounded with jaggery, chilli and salt, then pressed into sweet-sour sun-dried discs. Tamil Nadu's school-gate snack.

Wild ingredients

  • Ber (Ziziphus mauritiana): in season December to March
