Silverweed

Argentina anserina
Also called Zilverschoon

Silverweed is a root or shoot you can forage from September to November across the Netherlands. Creeping plant of damp paths and shores with feathery leaves, silver underneath, and single yellow flowers.

When is silverweed in season in the Netherlands?

Silverweed is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does silverweed grow in the Netherlands?

Silverweed grows across the Netherlands. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden, West, East, South.

How do you recognise silverweed?

Creeping plant of damp paths and shores with feathery leaves, silver underneath, and single yellow flowers.

How do you harvest silverweed?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat silverweed?

Roots roasted or boiled; once a famine food in Scotland and Ireland.

How do you store silverweed?

Store roots.
