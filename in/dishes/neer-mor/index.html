Neer mor (spiced buttermilk)

Neer mor (spiced buttermilk) is made with wild curry leaves, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make neer mor (spiced buttermilk)?

Whisk curd with water, crushed curry leaves, ginger, green chilli and salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Curry leaves (Murraya koenigii): in season all year round
