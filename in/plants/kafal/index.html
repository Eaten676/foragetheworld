Kafal

Myrica esculenta
Also called Kafal, Kaphal

Kafal is a wild fruit you can forage from April to June in Himalaya and Northeast. Mid-hill tree of Uttarakhand and Himachal. Small dark red berries.

When is kafal in season in India?

Kafal is usually in season April to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kafal grow in India?

Kafal grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya, Northeast.

How do you recognise kafal?

Mid-hill tree of Uttarakhand and Himachal. Small dark red berries.

How do you harvest kafal?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat kafal?

Fresh with salt and chilli.

How do you store kafal?

Eat fresh. It spoils within days.

What is kafal good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with kafal?

  • Himalayan berry juice: Crush ripe wild berries with a little water, strain, then add honey and a pinch of salt.
  • Himalayan berry jam: Wild hill berries cooked with sugar into jam.
