Bhurat

Cenchrus biflorus
Also called Bhurat, Bharut

Bhurat is a wild seed or grain you can forage from September to October in Thar and dry northwest. Thar desert grass with spiny burs. Its seeds are rich in fat and protein, and were a lifeline in famine years.

Caution. The burs are very spiny. Use gloves and a sieve to separate the seeds.

When is bhurat in season in India?

Bhurat is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bhurat grow in India?

Bhurat grows in Gujarat, Rajasthan. Regions: Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise bhurat?

Thar desert grass with spiny burs. Its seeds are rich in fat and protein, and were a lifeline in famine years.

How do you harvest bhurat?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat bhurat?

Seeds ground and mixed with bajra flour for rotis.

How do you store bhurat?

Dry and store the cleaned seeds.

What is bhurat good for?

Calories, Protein, Fat.

What can you cook with bhurat?

  • Bhurat roti: Ground bhurat seeds mixed into bajra flour and patted into thick rotis. Desert famine fare.
