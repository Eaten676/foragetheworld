Stinging nettle

Urtica incisa
Also called Scrub nettle

Stinging nettle is a wild green you can forage from August to November in Temperate south-east, Tasmania and Subtropical east coast. Native nettle of damp gullies with toothed leaves and stinging hairs.

When is stinging nettle in season in Australia?

Stinging nettle is usually in season August to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does stinging nettle grow in Australia?

Stinging nettle grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria. Regions: Temperate south-east, Tasmania, Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise stinging nettle?

Native nettle of damp gullies with toothed leaves and stinging hairs.

How do you harvest stinging nettle?

Wear gloves and snip the top four to six leaves of young plants before they flower. Cut back old patches in early summer for a second crop of tender tips.

How do you eat stinging nettle?

Soup, pesto, tea.

How do you store stinging nettle?

Blanch and freeze.

What is stinging nettle good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.
