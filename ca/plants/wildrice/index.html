Wild rice

Zizania palustris
Also called Manoomin, Riz sauvage

Wild rice is a wild seed or grain you can forage from August to September in Ontario and Prairies. Tall aquatic grass in northern lakes; seeds harvested from canoes by knocking.

Caution. Harvesting needs a licence in Minnesota, and much is on tribal waters where only Native harvesters may gather.

When is wild rice in season in Canada?

Wild rice is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild rice grow in Canada?

Wild rice grows in Alberta, Manitoba, Ontario, Saskatchewan. Regions: Ontario, Prairies.

How do you recognise wild rice?

Tall aquatic grass in northern lakes; seeds harvested from canoes by knocking.

How do you harvest wild rice?

Harvesting needs a licence; ricers bend stalks over a canoe and knock ripe grains loose with sticks.

How do you eat wild rice?

Pilaf, soup, stuffing.

How do you store wild rice?

Parch, dry and store.

What is wild rice good for?

Calories, Protein.

What can you cook with wild rice?

  • Wild rice and mushroom pilaf: Manoomin with mushrooms and herbs, from Ontario and Manitoba lakes.
  • Porcini and wild rice stuffing: Wild rice with porcini, onion and herbs.
