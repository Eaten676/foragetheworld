Kachnar ki sabzi

Kachnar ki sabzi is made with wild kachnar buds, a favourite in Northern plains and Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kachnar ki sabzi?

Blanched kachnar buds cooked with onion, curd and garam masala.

Wild ingredients

  • Kachnar buds (Bauhinia variegata): in season February to April
