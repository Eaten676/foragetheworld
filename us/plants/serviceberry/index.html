Serviceberry

Amelanchier
Also called Serviceberry, Saskatoon, Juneberry

Serviceberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July across the United States. Shrub or small tree with white spring blossom and purple berries with a little crown at the tip, like a blueberry.

When is serviceberry in season in the United States?

Serviceberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does serviceberry grow in the United States?

Serviceberry grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise serviceberry?

Shrub or small tree with white spring blossom and purple berries with a little crown at the tip, like a blueberry.

How do you harvest serviceberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat serviceberry?

Fresh, pie, jam, pemmican.

How do you store serviceberry?

Freeze or dry.

What is serviceberry good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with serviceberry?

  • Serviceberry crumble: Serviceberries under an oat crumble.
