Shevgyachya shenganchi amti

Shevgyachya shenganchi amti is made with wild moringa, tamarind, a favourite in West coast and Deccan.

How do you make shevgyachya shenganchi amti?

Maharashtrian drumstick amti: toor dal with goda masala, kokum or tamarind and a touch of jaggery.

Wild ingredients

  • Moringa (Moringa oleifera): in season all year round
  • Tamarind (Tamarindus indica): in season January to April
