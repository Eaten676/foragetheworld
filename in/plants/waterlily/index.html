Water lily seeds

Nymphaea nouchali
Also called Shapla, Kumudini, Ambal, Bheti

Water lily seeds is a wild seed or grain you can forage from October to January in East, South India, Northeast and Central forests. Blue and white water lilies of village ponds. The fruit sinks and rots, freeing tiny seeds that pop like makhana.

Caution. Only from clean ponds.

When is water lily seeds in season in India?

Water lily seeds is usually in season October to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does water lily seeds grow in India?

Water lily seeds grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: East, South India, Northeast, Central forests.

How do you recognise water lily seeds?

Blue and white water lilies of village ponds. The fruit sinks and rots, freeing tiny seeds that pop like makhana.

How do you harvest water lily seeds?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat water lily seeds?

Seeds popped in a hot pan (shapla khoi). Young stalks are cooked as a vegetable.

How do you store water lily seeds?

Dry the seeds and store them airtight.

What is water lily seeds good for?

Calories, Protein.

What can you cook with water lily seeds?

  • Shapla khoi: Water lily seeds popped in a hot pan like tiny popcorn, then salted.
