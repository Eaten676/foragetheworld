Chanterelle

Cantharellus cibarius
Also called Chanterelle, Girolle, Cantarnaid

Chanterelle is a wild mushroom you can forage from July to October across Ireland. Egg-yolk yellow, funnel-shaped mushroom smelling of apricots, with blunt, forking ridges (not true gills) running down the stem. Grows from soil in mossy woods.

Caution. Jack-o'-lantern (poisonous) grows on wood in clusters with true gills. In the Nordic countries the deadly webcap is brownish-orange with gills. Ridges, not gills.

When is chanterelle in season in Ireland?

Chanterelle is usually in season July to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chanterelle grow in Ireland?

Chanterelle grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise chanterelle?

Egg-yolk yellow, funnel-shaped mushroom smelling of apricots, with blunt, forking ridges (not true gills) running down the stem. Grows from soil in mossy woods.

How do you harvest chanterelle?

Cut or twist at the base, brush off soil and needles on the spot, and leave small ones to grow.

How do you eat chanterelle?

Fried in butter, in omelettes, cream sauces and risotto.

How do you store chanterelle?

Dry-fry and freeze, or dry.

What can be confused with chanterelle?

Jack-o'-lantern mushroom (Omphalotus olearius), rated poisonous. Chanterelles grow singly from soil; jack-o'-lanterns grow in tight clusters from wood or buried roots. Jack-o'-lanterns have true, sharp-edged gills; chanterelles have blunt, forking ridges. Chanterelle flesh is white inside; jack-o'-lantern flesh is orange. Deadly webcap (Cortinarius rubellus), rated deadly. Deadly webcap is rusty orange-brown with true gills and a cobwebby veil when young. It damages the kidneys days later. Never pick orange-brown gilled mushrooms.

What is chanterelle good for?

Protein, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with chanterelle?

  • Chanterelles on toast: Chanterelles fried in butter with thyme on sourdough.
