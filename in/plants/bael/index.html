Bael

Aegle marmelos
Also called Bael, Bilva, Vilvam, Bel

Bael is a wild fruit you can forage from March to June in Northern plains, Central forests, East, West coast and Deccan, South India and Thar and dry northwest. Thorny temple tree with hard, round, woody fruit. Fragrant orange pulp inside.

When is bael in season in India?

Bael is usually in season March to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bael grow in India?

Bael grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Northern plains, Central forests, East, West coast and Deccan, South India, Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise bael?

Thorny temple tree with hard, round, woody fruit. Fragrant orange pulp inside.

How do you harvest bael?

Wait for fruit to turn yellow and drop, or cut it with its stalk.

How do you eat bael?

Bael sharbat, or the pulp eaten fresh.

How do you store bael?

Slice and sun-dry the pulp, or make a thick syrup.

What can be confused with bael?

Nux vomica (kuchla) (Strychnos nux-vomica), rated deadly. Nux vomica fruit is smooth and orange with a thin, soft skin. Bael and wood apple have hard, woody shells you have to crack. Its seeds are flat, grey, velvety discs like buttons, and contain strychnine. Bael and wood apple trees have thorns. Nux vomica has glossy leaves with three strong veins.

What is bael good for?

Calories, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with bael?

  • Bael sharbat: Crack the shell, soak the pulp in water, mash, and strain out the seeds and fibres. Sweeten with jaggery and add a pinch of black salt.
