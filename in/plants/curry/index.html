Curry leaves

Murraya koenigii
Also called Kadi patta, Karibevu, Kariveppilai, Kadhi limdo

Curry leaves is a wild green you can forage all year round in South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Northeast and Himalaya. Small tree with glossy, strongly aromatic paired leaflets. Grows wild across India's foothills and scrub, and in almost every courtyard.

When is curry leaves in season in India?

Curry leaves is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does curry leaves grow in India?

Curry leaves grows across India. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Northeast, Himalaya.

How do you recognise curry leaves?

Small tree with glossy, strongly aromatic paired leaflets. Grows wild across India's foothills and scrub, and in almost every courtyard.

How do you harvest curry leaves?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat curry leaves?

Tempering, chutney powder, rice.

How do you store curry leaves?

Shade-dry, then roast into a podi with dal and chilli. Keeps 2–3 months.

What can be confused with curry leaves?

Orange jasmine (Murraya paniculata), rated not food. Crush a leaf. Curry leaves have their strong, unmistakable smell, while orange jasmine barely smells. Orange jasmine is a garden hedge with fragrant white flowers and red berries. Its leaflets are glossier and rounder. A curry leaf has 11–21 smaller, pointed leaflets.

What is curry leaves good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with curry leaves?

  • Neer mor (spiced buttermilk): Whisk curd with water, crushed curry leaves, ginger, green chilli and salt.
  • Karibevu podi: Dry-roasted curry leaves with urad dal, chilli and tamarind, ground to a powder for rice and dosa.
  • Puliyogare: Rice tossed in a thick tamarind paste with sesame, peanuts and curry leaves.
