Camas pit bake

Camas pit bake is made with wild camas, a favourite in British Columbia.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make camas pit bake?

Camas bulbs slow-cooked until sweet, after Coast Salish and Nlaka'pamux tradition.

Wild ingredients

  • Camas (Camassia quamash): in season July to October
