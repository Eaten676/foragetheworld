Ghain

Elaeagnus umbellata
Also called Ghain, Kankoli

Ghain is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August in Himalaya. Silvery-leaved thorny shrub with small speckled red berries.

When is ghain in season in India?

Ghain is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does ghain grow in India?

Ghain grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise ghain?

Silvery-leaved thorny shrub with small speckled red berries.

How do you harvest ghain?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat ghain?

Fresh, or jam.

How do you store ghain?

Jam or juice.

What is ghain good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with ghain?

  • Himalayan berry juice: Crush ripe wild berries with a little water, strain, then add honey and a pinch of salt.
  • Himalayan berry jam: Wild hill berries cooked with sugar into jam.
