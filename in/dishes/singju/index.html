Singju

Singju is made with wild tree bean, culantro, fishwort, a favourite in Northeast.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make singju?

Manipuri salad of sliced tree bean, wild herbs and cabbage, dressed with roasted chilli and perilla.

Wild ingredients

  • Tree bean (Parkia timoriana): in season November to March
  • Culantro (Eryngium foetidum): in season all year round
  • Fishwort (Houttuynia cordata): in season March to July
