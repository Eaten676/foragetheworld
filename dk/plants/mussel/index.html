Blue mussels

Mytilus edulis
Also called Blåmusling

Blue mussels is a wild protein you can forage from January to December across Denmark. Blue-black shells in clusters on rocks and piers at low tide.

Caution. Check the local shellfish toxin warnings before every harvest. Only cold months; discard any that don't close.

When is blue mussels in season in Denmark?

Blue mussels is usually in season January to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blue mussels grow in Denmark?

Blue mussels grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise blue mussels?

Blue-black shells in clusters on rocks and piers at low tide.

How do you harvest blue mussels?

Twist mussels off rocks at low tide, taking only large ones, and check local toxin warnings first.

How do you eat blue mussels?

Steamed with wine, garlic and herbs.

How do you store blue mussels?

Eat the same day.

What is blue mussels good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with blue mussels?

  • Mussels in white wine (muslinger): Limfjord mussels steamed with wine, shallots and parsley.
