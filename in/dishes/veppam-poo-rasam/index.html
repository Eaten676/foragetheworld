Veppam poo rasam

Veppam poo rasam is made with wild neem flowers, tamarind, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make veppam poo rasam?

Fried neem flowers in a tamarind rasam. Bitter, sour and soothing.

Wild ingredients

  • Neem flowers (Azadirachta indica): in season March to April
  • Tamarind (Tamarindus indica): in season January to April
