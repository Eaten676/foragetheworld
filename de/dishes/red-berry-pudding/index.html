Red berry pudding (Rote Grütze)

Red berry pudding (Rote Grütze) is made with wild raspberry, blackcurrant, a favourite in North German lowlands and coast.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make red berry pudding (rote grütze)?

Summer berries thickened with starch, with vanilla sauce.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild raspberry (Rubus idaeus): in season July to August
  • Wild blackcurrant (Ribes nigrum): in season July to August
