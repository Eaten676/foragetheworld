Good King Henry

Blitum bonus-henricus
Also called Guter Heinrich, Épinard sauvage, Buon Enrico

Good King Henry is a wild green you can forage from April to July in Alps and Jura. Arrow-shaped, slightly mealy leaves around mountain huts and pastures; flower spikes like spinach.

Caution. Contains oxalates; cook it.

When is Good King Henry in season in Switzerland?

Good King Henry is usually in season April to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does Good King Henry grow in Switzerland?

Good King Henry grows in Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Appenzell Innerrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Bern, Fribourg, Glarus, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Nidwalden, Obwalden, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Ticino, Uri, Valais, Vaud. Regions: Alps, Jura.

How do you recognise Good King Henry?

Arrow-shaped, slightly mealy leaves around mountain huts and pastures; flower spikes like spinach.

How do you harvest Good King Henry?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat Good King Henry?

Cook like spinach; shoots like asparagus.

How do you store Good King Henry?

Blanch and freeze.

What is Good King Henry good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with Good King Henry?

  • Good King Henry with polenta: Alpine wild spinach wilted with butter, served on polenta.
