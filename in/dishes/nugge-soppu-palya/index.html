Nugge soppu palya

Nugge soppu palya is made with wild moringa, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nugge soppu palya?

Moringa leaves stir-fried with mustard, urad dal, onion and grated coconut.

Wild ingredients

  • Moringa (Moringa oleifera): in season all year round
