Wild strawberry

Fragaria vesca
Also called Wild strawberry, Sú talún fiáin

Wild strawberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July across Ireland. Tiny plants on woodland edges and banks with leaves in threes; small, intensely sweet berries.

When is wild strawberry in season in Ireland?

Wild strawberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild strawberry grow in Ireland?

Wild strawberry grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise wild strawberry?

Tiny plants on woodland edges and banks with leaves in threes; small, intensely sweet berries.

How do you harvest wild strawberry?

Pick the tiny berries when fully red and soft; leave plants and runners undisturbed.

How do you eat wild strawberry?

Fresh, or threaded on a grass stalk.

How do you store wild strawberry?

Best fresh.

What is wild strawberry good for?

Vitamin C.
