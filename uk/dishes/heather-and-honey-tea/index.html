Heather and honey tea

Heather and honey tea is made with wild heather flowers, a favourite in Scotland.

How do you make heather and honey tea?

Dried heather flowers steeped with honey.

Wild ingredients

  • Heather flowers (Calluna vulgaris): in season August to September
