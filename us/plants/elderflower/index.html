Elderflower

Sambucus canadensis
Also called Elder blossom

Elderflower is a edible flower or tea you can forage from May to July in Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, California and Pacific Northwest. Flat creamy-white umbrellas of tiny flowers with a musky-sweet scent in early summer.

Caution. Water hemlock and poison hemlock also have white umbels; elder is a woody shrub.

When is elderflower in season in the United States?

Elderflower is usually in season May to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does elderflower grow in the United States?

Elderflower grows in Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, California, Pacific Northwest.

How do you recognise elderflower?

Flat creamy-white umbrellas of tiny flowers with a musky-sweet scent in early summer.

How do you harvest elderflower?

Cut fully open, creamy flower heads on a sunny morning; smell them and use the same day. Leave plenty for berries.

How do you eat elderflower?

Cordial, fritters, champagne.

How do you store elderflower?

Make cordial or dry flowers.

What can be confused with elderflower?

Poison hemlock (Conium maculatum), rated deadly. Elder is a woody shrub. Poison hemlock is a soft, hollow-stemmed plant. Hemlock stems are smooth with purple blotches. Crushed hemlock smells mousy and unpleasant.

What is elderflower good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with elderflower?

  • Elderflower cordial: Steep flower heads overnight with lemon, strain and dissolve in sugar syrup. Dilute with sparkling water.
