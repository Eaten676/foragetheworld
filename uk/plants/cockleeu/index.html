Cockles

Cerastoderma edule
Also called Cockles

Cockles is a wild protein you can forage from September to April across the UK. Ribbed, round shells just under the surface of sandy and muddy beaches; rake gently at low tide.

Caution. Check your shellfish water classification and closures first, purge in clean seawater, and always cook.

When is cockles in season in the UK?

Cockles is usually in season September to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cockles grow in the UK?

Cockles grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise cockles?

Ribbed, round shells just under the surface of sandy and muddy beaches; rake gently at low tide.

How do you harvest cockles?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat cockles?

Steamed, in paella and pasta, or with laverbread and bacon.

How do you store cockles?

Cook and freeze meat.

What is cockles good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with cockles?

  • Laverbread, cockles and bacon: The Welsh breakfast of cockles, laverbread and bacon.
