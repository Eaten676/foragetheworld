Jackfruit

Artocarpus heterophyllus
Also called Kathal, Halasu, Pala, Phanas

Jackfruit is a sap or starch you can forage from March to July in South India, West coast and Deccan, East and Northeast. Huge spiky fruit hanging off trunks. The biggest calorie source you can forage. Seeds add protein.

Caution. Trees almost always have an owner, so ask first.

When is jackfruit in season in India?

Jackfruit is usually in season March to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does jackfruit grow in India?

Jackfruit grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Northeast.

How do you recognise jackfruit?

Huge spiky fruit hanging off trunks. The biggest calorie source you can forage. Seeds add protein.

How do you harvest jackfruit?

Ask the owner first. Cut the stalk with a knife oiled against the sticky latex, and lower heavy fruit with a rope.

How do you eat jackfruit?

Unripe, cooked as a vegetable or in biryani. Ripe, eaten as fruit. Seeds boiled or roasted.

How do you store jackfruit?

Seeds keep for months coated in mud or dry sand. Make chips, papad, or dry the raw slices.

What is jackfruit good for?

Calories, Vitamin C, Protein.

What can you cook with jackfruit?

  • Jackfruit chips: Firm unripe bulbs sliced thin and fried in coconut oil with salt.
  • Roasted jackfruit seeds: Seeds roasted in hot sand or a pan, then peeled and salted. Nutty and filling.
  • Kathal biryani: Raw jackfruit chunks layered with rice and whole spices. It eats like meat.
  • Chakka varatti: Ripe jackfruit cooked down with jaggery and ghee into a dark jam. Kerala.
