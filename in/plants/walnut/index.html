Wild nut · Sep–Oct

Walnut

Juglans regia
Also called Akhrot, Doon

Walnut is a wild nut you can forage from September to October in Himalaya. Big Himalayan trees, often semi-wild around villages. Ask before picking.

When is walnut in season in India?

Walnut is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does walnut grow in India?

Walnut grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise walnut?

Big Himalayan trees, often semi-wild around villages. Ask before picking.

How do you harvest walnut?

Collect fallen nuts and peel the husk with gloves, because it stains badly.

How do you eat walnut?

Raw, in chutney, in rotis.

How do you store walnut?

Dry in the husk-free shell. Keeps a year.

What is walnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.

What can you cook with walnut?

  • Akhrot chutney: Walnuts ground with curd, green chilli, mint and salt. A Kashmiri favourite.
