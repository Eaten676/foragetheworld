Sea grape

Coccoloba uvifera
Also called Sea grape

Sea grape is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in Southeast. Florida beach shrub with huge round leathery leaves and grape-like bunches of purple fruit.

Caution. Protected on Florida dunes: pick only from plants away from the beach, and never trim them on public shoreline.

When is sea grape in season in the United States?

Sea grape is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea grape grow in the United States?

Sea grape grows in Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia. Regions: Southeast.

How do you recognise sea grape?

Florida beach shrub with huge round leathery leaves and grape-like bunches of purple fruit.

How do you harvest sea grape?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat sea grape?

Fresh, or cooked into jelly and wine.

How do you store sea grape?

Jelly.

What is sea grape good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with sea grape?

  • Sea grape jelly: Florida sea grapes cooked and strained into jelly.
