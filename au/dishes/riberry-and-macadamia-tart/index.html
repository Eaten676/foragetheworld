Riberry and macadamia tart

Riberry and macadamia tart is made with wild riberry, macadamia, a favourite in Subtropical east coast.

How do you make riberry and macadamia tart?

Tart of riberries over a macadamia frangipane.

Wild ingredients

  • Riberry (Syzygium luehmannii): in season December to February
  • Macadamia (Macadamia integrifolia): in season March to August
