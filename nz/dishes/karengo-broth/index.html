Karengo broth

Karengo broth is made with wild karengo, a favourite in East Coast and Hawke's Bay and Otago and Southland.

Open in Forage New ZealandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make karengo broth?

Seaweed simmered into a savoury broth with fish.

Wild ingredients

  • Karengo (Pyropia): in season July to November
