Roasted makhana

Roasted makhana is made with wild makhana, a favourite in East and Northern plains.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make roasted makhana?

Fox nuts tossed in ghee with salt, pepper and chaat masala until crunchy.

Wild ingredients

  • Makhana (Euryale ferox): in season August to October
