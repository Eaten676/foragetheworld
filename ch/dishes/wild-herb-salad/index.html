Wild herb salad (Wildkräutersalat)

Wild herb salad (Wildkräutersalat) is made with wild ground elder, chickweed, common sorrel, dandelion.

Open in Forage SwitzerlandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make wild herb salad (wildkräutersalat)?

Mixed wild greens with flowers and a mustard dressing.

Wild ingredients

  • Ground elder (Aegopodium podagraria): in season March to June
  • Chickweed (Stellaria media): in season March to November
  • Common sorrel (Rumex acetosa): in season April to August
  • Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale): in season March to October
