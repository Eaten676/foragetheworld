Elderflower

Sambucus nigra
Also called Hylleblomst

Elderflower is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to July in Southern Norway, Western Norway and Eastern Norway. Flat creamy-white umbrellas of tiny flowers with a musky-sweet scent on a woody shrub in early summer.

Caution. Hemlock and hemlock water-dropwort also have white umbels, but they are soft plants, not shrubs.

When is elderflower in season in Norway?

Elderflower is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does elderflower grow in Norway?

Elderflower grows in Agder, Akershus, Buskerud, Innlandet, Møre og Romsdal, Oslo, Rogaland, Telemark, Vestfold, Vestland, Østfold. Regions: Southern Norway, Western Norway, Eastern Norway.

How do you recognise elderflower?

Flat creamy-white umbrellas of tiny flowers with a musky-sweet scent on a woody shrub in early summer.

How do you harvest elderflower?

Cut fully open, creamy flower heads on a sunny morning; smell them and use the same day. Leave plenty for berries.

How do you eat elderflower?

Cordial, fritters, sparkling elderflower.

How do you store elderflower?

Make cordial or dry.

What can be confused with elderflower?

Hemlock (Conium maculatum), rated deadly. Elder is a woody shrub; hemlock is a soft plant with hollow stems. Hemlock stems are smooth with purple blotches and smell mousy. Ground elder has leaves in threes on a triangular, unspotted stem.

What is elderflower good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with elderflower?

  • Elderflower cordial (hylleblomstsaft): Flowers steeped with lemon and citric acid, then sweetened.
