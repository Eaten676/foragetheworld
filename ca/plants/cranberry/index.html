Wild cranberry

Vaccinium macrocarpon
Also called American cranberry, Canneberge

Wild cranberry is a wild fruit you can forage from September to October in Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Creeping vine in bogs with tiny leathery leaves and red berries that bounce.

When is wild cranberry in season in Canada?

Wild cranberry is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild cranberry grow in Canada?

Wild cranberry grows in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise wild cranberry?

Creeping vine in bogs with tiny leathery leaves and red berries that bounce.

How do you harvest wild cranberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat wild cranberry?

Sauce, juice, dried.

How do you store wild cranberry?

Freeze whole.

What is wild cranberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild cranberry?

  • Cranberry sauce: Wild cranberries simmered with maple syrup.
