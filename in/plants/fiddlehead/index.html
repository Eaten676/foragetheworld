Fiddlehead fern

Diplazium esculentum
Also called Lingdu, Dhekia, Ningro

Fiddlehead fern is a root or shoot you can forage from March to August in Himalaya, Northeast and East. Coiled young fronds by streams and in damp forest edges.

Caution. Never eat raw. Boil and discard the water first.

When is fiddlehead fern in season in India?

Fiddlehead fern is usually in season March to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does fiddlehead fern grow in India?

Fiddlehead fern grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Ladakh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Himalaya, Northeast, East.

How do you recognise fiddlehead fern?

Coiled young fronds by streams and in damp forest edges.

How do you harvest fiddlehead fern?

Snap off only tightly coiled tops about 15 cm long, taking one or two per crown so the fern survives.

How do you eat fiddlehead fern?

Stir-fry or pickle after boiling.

How do you store fiddlehead fern?

Pickle in mustard oil, or blanch and dry.

What can be confused with fiddlehead fern?

Bracken fern (Pteridium aquilinum), rated harmful. Edible lingdu grows in clumps in wet ground near streams. Bracken grows as single stalks across dry, open hillsides. Lingdu stems have a U-shaped groove on the inside, like celery. Bracken stems are round and fuzzy when young. A bracken frond splits into three branches near the top. A lingdu frond is a single feather shape.

What is fiddlehead fern good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with fiddlehead fern?

  • Lingdu pickle: Boiled fiddleheads in mustard oil with lemon, chilli and salt.
  • Lingdu bhaji: Fiddleheads boiled, then stir-fried with garlic, cumin and chilli.
