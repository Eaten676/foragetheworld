Laver

Porphyra umbilicalis
Also called Laver

Laver is a wild green you can forage from October to April in Wales, South West England, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Thin, glossy purple-black sheets of seaweed clinging to rocks.

When is laver in season in the UK?

Laver is usually in season October to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does laver grow in the UK?

Laver grows in Northern Ireland, Scotland, South West England, Wales. Regions: Wales, South West England, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise laver?

Thin, glossy purple-black sheets of seaweed clinging to rocks.

How do you harvest laver?

Peel sheets off rocks at low tide, taking a little from many rocks.

How do you eat laver?

Laverbread: cooked for hours into a dark purée, fried with oats.

How do you store laver?

Dry.

What is laver good for?

Iron & calcium, Protein, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with laver?

  • Laverbread with cockles and bacon: The Welsh breakfast of laverbread, cockles and bacon.
  • Laverbread, cockles and bacon: The Welsh breakfast of cockles, laverbread and bacon.
