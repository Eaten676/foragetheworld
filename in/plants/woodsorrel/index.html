Wood sorrel

Oxalis corniculata
Also called Amrul, Huli soppu, Puliyarai

Wood sorrel is a wild green you can forage from June to February across India. Tiny clover-like heart-shaped leaflets with small yellow flowers. Lemony taste.

Caution. Contains oxalic acid. Eat in small amounts.

When is wood sorrel in season in India?

Wood sorrel is usually in season June to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wood sorrel grow in India?

Wood sorrel grows across India. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, Thar and dry northwest, Central forests, East, Northeast, Northern plains, Himalaya.

How do you recognise wood sorrel?

Tiny clover-like heart-shaped leaflets with small yellow flowers. Lemony taste.

How do you harvest wood sorrel?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat wood sorrel?

Chutney or a handful in dal for sourness.

How do you store wood sorrel?

Use fresh.

What is wood sorrel good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wood sorrel?

  • Huli soppu chutney: Wood sorrel ground with coconut, chilli and a little jaggery. Small portions only.
