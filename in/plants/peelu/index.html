Peelu

Salvadora oleoides
Also called Peelu, Jaal, Mitha jaal

Peelu is a wild fruit you can forage from May to June in Thar and dry northwest, West coast and Deccan and Northern plains. Gnarled desert tree on saline soil, with sweet, tiny yellow-red berries in early summer.

When is peelu in season in India?

Peelu is usually in season May to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does peelu grow in India?

Peelu grows in Bihar, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand. Regions: Thar and dry northwest, West coast and Deccan, Northern plains.

How do you recognise peelu?

Gnarled desert tree on saline soil, with sweet, tiny yellow-red berries in early summer.

How do you harvest peelu?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat peelu?

Fresh, by the handful.

How do you store peelu?

Sun-dry.

What is peelu good for?

Calories.
