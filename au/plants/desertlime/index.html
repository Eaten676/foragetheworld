Desert lime

Citrus glauca
Also called Desert lime

Desert lime is a wild fruit you can forage from November to January in Arid centre and Subtropical east coast. Spiny inland shrub with grey-green leaves and small round green limes.

When is desert lime in season in Australia?

Desert lime is usually in season November to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does desert lime grow in Australia?

Desert lime grows in New South Wales, Northern Territory, Queensland, South Australia, Western Australia. Regions: Arid centre, Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise desert lime?

Spiny inland shrub with grey-green leaves and small round green limes.

How do you harvest desert lime?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat desert lime?

Whole in marmalade, cordial, sauces.

How do you store desert lime?

Freeze.

What is desert lime good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with desert lime?

  • Desert lime cordial: Whole desert limes cooked into a tangy cordial.
