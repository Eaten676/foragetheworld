Spruce tips

Picea abies
Also called Granskudd

Spruce tips is a wild green you can forage from May to June across Norway. Bright green new growth bursting from papery brown casings on spruce branches in late spring. Citrus-resin flavour.

Caution. Never confuse with yew (flat soft needles, red berries), which is deadly.

When is spruce tips in season in Norway?

Spruce tips is usually in season May to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does spruce tips grow in Norway?

Spruce tips grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise spruce tips?

Bright green new growth bursting from papery brown casings on spruce branches in late spring. Citrus-resin flavour.

How do you harvest spruce tips?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat spruce tips?

Syrup ('spruce honey'), salt, tea.

How do you store spruce tips?

Syrup; freeze.

What can be confused with spruce tips?

Yew (Taxus baccata), rated deadly. Spruce needles are stiff, square in section and grow all round the twig. Yew needles are flat, soft and dark, in two rows, with a paler underside. Yew has red cup-shaped berries.

What is spruce tips good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with spruce tips?

  • Spruce tip syrup (granskuddsirup): Young spruce shoots simmered with sugar into a citrusy syrup.
