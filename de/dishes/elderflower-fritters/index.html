Elderflower fritters (Hollerküchle)

Elderflower fritters (Hollerküchle) is made with wild elderflower, a favourite in South and the Alps.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower fritters (hollerküchle)?

Flower heads in pancake batter, fried and sugared. A Bavarian and Swabian favourite.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
