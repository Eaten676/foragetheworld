Rose hip syrup (rozenbottelsiroop)

Rose hip syrup (rozenbottelsiroop) is made with wild beach rose, rose hips, a favourite in Coast, dunes and Wadden.

Open in Forage NetherlandsShare on WhatsApp

How do you make rose hip syrup (rozenbottelsiroop)?

Hips from dune roses cooked into syrup.

Wild ingredients

  • Beach rose (Rosa rugosa): in season August to October
  • Rose hips (Rosa canina): in season September to November
