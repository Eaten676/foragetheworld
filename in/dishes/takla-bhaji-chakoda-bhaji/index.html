Takla bhaji / chakoda bhaji

Takla bhaji / chakoda bhaji is made with wild sickle senna, a favourite in West coast and Deccan and Central forests.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make takla bhaji / chakoda bhaji?

Tender senna leaves cooked dry with onion, garlic and besan.

Wild ingredients

  • Sickle senna (Senna tora): in season June to August
