Vermicelles

Vermicelles is made with wild sweet chestnut, a favourite in Swiss Plateau and Ticino and southern valleys.

Open in Forage SwitzerlandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make vermicelles?

Sweet chestnut purée piped into noodles over meringue and cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa): in season October to November
