Wild cherry

Prunus avium
Also called Søtkirsebær

Wild cherry is a wild fruit you can forage from July in Eastern Norway, Southern Norway and Western Norway. Tall tree with shiny, peeling red-brown bark and white spring blossom; small red to black cherries in early summer.

Caution. Don't crush the stones.

When is wild cherry in season in Norway?

Wild cherry is usually in season July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild cherry grow in Norway?

Wild cherry grows in Agder, Akershus, Buskerud, Innlandet, Møre og Romsdal, Oslo, Rogaland, Telemark, Vestfold, Vestland, Østfold. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway.

How do you recognise wild cherry?

Tall tree with shiny, peeling red-brown bark and white spring blossom; small red to black cherries in early summer.

How do you harvest wild cherry?

Pick ripe cherries before the birds do, from low branches.

How do you eat wild cherry?

Fresh, jam, cherry soup, liqueur.

How do you store wild cherry?

Freeze stoned.

What is wild cherry good for?

Vitamin C.
