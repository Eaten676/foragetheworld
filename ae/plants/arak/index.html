Arak berries

Salvadora persica
Also called Arak, Kabath, Toothbrush tree

Arak berries is a wild fruit you can forage from April to June in Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Gulf coast and Cities, farms and oases. Sprawling shrub of sand plains and coasts with pale, fleshy leaves; its twigs are the miswak toothbrush. Clusters of small berries ripen red to purple.

Caution. Eat only small amounts.

How do you harvest arak berries?

Pick only ripe fruit that comes away easily, and only with the owner's permission: most trees and palms belong to a farm, a family or the municipality.

How do you eat arak berries?

Ripe berries eaten fresh: sweet with a peppery bite.

How do you store arak berries?

Best fresh.
