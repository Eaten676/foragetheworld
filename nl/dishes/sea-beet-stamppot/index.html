Sea beet stamppot

Sea beet stamppot is made with wild sea beet, a favourite in Coast, dunes and Wadden.

Open in Forage NetherlandsShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sea beet stamppot?

Mashed potatoes stirred with wilted sea beet and smoked sausage.

Wild ingredients

  • Sea beet (Beta vulgaris subsp. maritima): in season March to October
