River mint

Mentha australis
Also called River mint

River mint is a edible flower or tea you can forage from September to April in Temperate south-east, Subtropical east coast and Tasmania. Soft spreading mint of creek banks with narrow leaves and a sweet spearmint scent.

When is river mint in season in Australia?

River mint is usually in season September to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does river mint grow in Australia?

River mint grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria. Regions: Temperate south-east, Subtropical east coast, Tasmania.

How do you recognise river mint?

Soft spreading mint of creek banks with narrow leaves and a sweet spearmint scent.

How do you harvest river mint?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat river mint?

Tea, with lamb, in drinks.

How do you store river mint?

Dry.

What can you cook with river mint?

  • River mint tea: Fresh river mint steeped in hot water.
  • Steamed mussels with river mint: Mussels steamed with garlic, white wine and native mint.
