Wapato

Sagittaria latifolia
Also called Wapato, Sagittaire

Wapato is a root or shoot you can forage from October to January across Canada. Arrowhead-shaped leaves in marsh shallows; tubers at the end of runners in the mud.

Caution. Only from clean water; always cook.

When is wapato in season in Canada?

Wapato is usually in season October to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wapato grow in Canada?

Wapato grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise wapato?

Arrowhead-shaped leaves in marsh shallows; tubers at the end of runners in the mud.

How do you harvest wapato?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat wapato?

Roast or boil like potatoes.

How do you store wapato?

Store cool.
