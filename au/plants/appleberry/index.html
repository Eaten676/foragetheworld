Apple berry

Billardiera scandens
Also called Apple berry

Apple berry is a wild fruit you can forage from December to March in Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, Tasmania and South-west. Soft twining vine with yellow bell flowers and green, sausage-shaped berries that fall when ripe.

When is apple berry in season in Australia?

Apple berry is usually in season December to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does apple berry grow in Australia?

Apple berry grows across Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, Tasmania, South-west.

How do you recognise apple berry?

Soft twining vine with yellow bell flowers and green, sausage-shaped berries that fall when ripe.

How do you harvest apple berry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat apple berry?

Fallen, wrinkly ripe fruit tastes of stewed apple and kiwi.

How do you store apple berry?

Best fresh.

What is apple berry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with apple berry?

  • Apple berry crumble: Ripe apple berries under an oat crumble.
