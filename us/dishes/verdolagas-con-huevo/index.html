Verdolagas con huevo

Verdolagas con huevo is made with wild purslane, a favourite in Southwest and California.

How do you make verdolagas con huevo?

Purslane scrambled with egg, onion and chilli, Mexican style.

Wild ingredients

  • Purslane (Portulaca oleracea): in season June to September
