Bush tomato kangaroo

Bush tomato kangaroo is made with wild desert raisin, mountain pepper, a favourite in Arid centre.

How do you make bush tomato kangaroo?

Kangaroo fillet with a bush tomato and pepperberry rub.

Wild ingredients

  • Desert raisin (Solanum centrale): in season April to July
  • Mountain pepper (Tasmannia lanceolata): in season March to May
Seasons and ranges are approximate and shift with weather and altitude. Identify every plant with an experienced local before eating it. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited on each page.
