# Forage the World

Free guides to wild food by country, region and season: what's in season near you this month, how to harvest it, the dangerous lookalikes to avoid, and what to cook.

Countries: United States, Switzerland, Australia, Norway, Denmark, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, New Zealand. India lives at https://eaten676.github.io/forage-india/

Seasons and ranges are approximate. Identify every plant with an experienced local before eating it. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited in each guide.
