Bog cranberry

Vaccinium oxycoccos
Also called Tranebær

Bog cranberry is a wild fruit you can forage from September to October in Jutland. Thread-thin creeping stems over sphagnum bogs with tiny leaves and red speckled berries.

When is bog cranberry in season in Denmark?

Bog cranberry is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bog cranberry grow in Denmark?

Bog cranberry grows in Central Jutland (Midtjylland), North Jutland (Nordjylland), Southern Denmark (Syddanmark). Regions: Jutland.

How do you recognise bog cranberry?

Thread-thin creeping stems over sphagnum bogs with tiny leaves and red speckled berries.

How do you harvest bog cranberry?

Pick red berries from the bog surface in autumn; stay on boardwalks where they exist.

How do you eat bog cranberry?

Sauce, juice, with game.

How do you store bog cranberry?

Freeze.

What is bog cranberry good for?

Vitamin C.
