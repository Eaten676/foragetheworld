Heather flowers

Calluna vulgaris
Also called Heather, Ling, Fraoch

Heather flowers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from August to September across Ireland. Low woody shrub covering moors and heaths with tiny purple bell flowers in late summer.

When is heather flowers in season in Ireland?

Heather flowers is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does heather flowers grow in Ireland?

Heather flowers grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise heather flowers?

Low woody shrub covering moors and heaths with tiny purple bell flowers in late summer.

How do you harvest heather flowers?

Snip flowering tips in late summer.

How do you eat heather flowers?

Flowers as tea and in heather beer.

How do you store heather flowers?

Dry flowers.

What can you cook with heather flowers?

  • Bog myrtle and heather beer: Old-style gruit ale brewed with bog myrtle and heather instead of hops.
