Kurrajong seeds

Brachychiton populneus
Also called Kurrajong seeds

Kurrajong seeds is a wild nut you can forage from March to June in Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east and Arid centre. Tough, shiny-leaved tree of dry country with boat-shaped woody pods full of yellow seeds.

Caution. Pods are lined with irritating hairs: wear gloves and rub the hairs off.

When is kurrajong seeds in season in Australia?

Kurrajong seeds is usually in season March to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kurrajong seeds grow in Australia?

Kurrajong seeds grows across Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, Arid centre.

How do you recognise kurrajong seeds?

Tough, shiny-leaved tree of dry country with boat-shaped woody pods full of yellow seeds.

How do you harvest kurrajong seeds?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat kurrajong seeds?

Seeds roasted and eaten or ground into a coffee-like drink.

How do you store kurrajong seeds?

Store roasted seeds.
