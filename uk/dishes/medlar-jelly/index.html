Medlar jelly

Medlar jelly is made with wild medlar, a favourite in South East England and Midlands and East.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make medlar jelly?

Bletted medlars simmered, strained and set into an amber jelly.

Wild ingredients

  • Medlar (Mespilus germanica): in season October to December
