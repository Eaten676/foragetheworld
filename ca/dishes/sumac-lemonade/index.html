Sumac lemonade

Sumac lemonade is made with wild staghorn sumac, a favourite in Ontario.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sumac lemonade?

Sumac clusters steeped in cold water, strained and sweetened.

Wild ingredients

  • Staghorn sumac (Rhus typhina): in season August to September
