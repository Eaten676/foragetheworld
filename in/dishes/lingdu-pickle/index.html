Lingdu pickle

Lingdu pickle is made with wild fiddlehead fern, a favourite in Himalaya and Northeast.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make lingdu pickle?

Boiled fiddleheads in mustard oil with lemon, chilli and salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Fiddlehead fern (Diplazium esculentum): in season March to August
