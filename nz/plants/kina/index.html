Kina

Evechinus chloroticus
Also called Kina

Kina is a wild protein you can forage from June to October across New Zealand. Spiny green sea urchin on subtidal rocks; orange roe inside.

Caution. Follow MPI daily limits and local rāhui.

When is kina in season in New Zealand?

Kina is usually in season June to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kina grow in New Zealand?

Kina grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise kina?

Spiny green sea urchin on subtidal rocks; orange roe inside.

How do you harvest kina?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat kina?

Roe raw on bread, or in fritters.

How do you store kina?

Eat fresh.

What is kina good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with kina?

  • Kina on toast: Fresh kina roe on buttered bread.
