Old man saltbush

Atriplex nummularia
Also called Saltbush

Old man saltbush is a wild green you can forage all year round in Arid centre, Temperate south-east and South-west. Silvery-grey shrub of dry inland plains with salty, rounded leaves.

When is old man saltbush in season in Australia?

Old man saltbush is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does old man saltbush grow in Australia?

Old man saltbush grows across Australia. Regions: Arid centre, Temperate south-east, South-west.

How do you recognise old man saltbush?

Silvery-grey shrub of dry inland plains with salty, rounded leaves.

How do you harvest old man saltbush?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat old man saltbush?

Leaves as a salty herb, fried as chips, or dried as seasoning on lamb.

How do you store old man saltbush?

Dry and grind.

What is old man saltbush good for?

Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with old man saltbush?

  • Saltbush chips: Saltbush leaves fried crisp in light batter.
  • Saltbush lamb: Lamb rubbed with dried saltbush and pepperberry, roasted.
