Ramp butter

Ramp butter is made with wild ramps, a favourite in Northeast, Southeast and Midwest.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make ramp butter?

Chopped ramp leaves beaten into softened butter.

Wild ingredients

  • Ramps (Allium tricoccum): in season April to May
