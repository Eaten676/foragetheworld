Orache

Atriplex prostrata / A. patula
Also called Melde

Orache is a wild green you can forage from June to September across Norway. Spinach-like plant of beaches, salt marshes and field edges with arrow-shaped, often mealy leaves.

When is orache in season in Norway?

Orache is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does orache grow in Norway?

Orache grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise orache?

Spinach-like plant of beaches, salt marshes and field edges with arrow-shaped, often mealy leaves.

How do you harvest orache?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat orache?

Cooked like spinach; naturally salty near the sea.

How do you store orache?

Blanch and freeze.

What is orache good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C.
