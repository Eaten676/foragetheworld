Oysters

Crassostrea virginica / Magallana gigas
Also called Oysters, Huître

Oysters is a wild protein you can forage from September to April in Atlantic Canada and British Columbia. Rough, irregular shells cemented to rocks and each other on sheltered shores and estuaries.

Caution. Check the shellfish closure map and licence rules; in summer, cook them to avoid Vibrio.

When is oysters in season in Canada?

Oysters is usually in season September to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does oysters grow in Canada?

Oysters grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island. Regions: Atlantic Canada, British Columbia.

How do you recognise oysters?

Rough, irregular shells cemented to rocks and each other on sheltered shores and estuaries.

How do you harvest oysters?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat oysters?

Raw on the half shell, grilled or in stew.

How do you store oysters?

Eat fresh.

What is oysters good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with oysters?

  • Oysters with mignonette: Island oysters shucked and served with shallot vinegar.
