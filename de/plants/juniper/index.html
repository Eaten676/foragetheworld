Juniper berries

Juniperus communis
Also called Wacholder

Juniper berries is a wild fruit you can forage from September to November across Germany. Spiky evergreen shrub of heaths and mountains; blue-black berries take two years to ripen.

Caution. Use as a spice only; avoid in pregnancy.

When is juniper berries in season in Germany?

Juniper berries is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does juniper berries grow in Germany?

Juniper berries grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise juniper berries?

Spiky evergreen shrub of heaths and mountains; blue-black berries take two years to ripen.

How do you harvest juniper berries?

Pick only the blue-black, ripe berries (two years old), leaving green ones to ripen.

How do you eat juniper berries?

A spice for game, sauerkraut and gin.

How do you store juniper berries?

Dry.
