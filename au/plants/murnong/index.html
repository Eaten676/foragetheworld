Yam daisy

Microseris walteri
Also called Murnong, Yam daisy

Yam daisy is a root or shoot you can forage from May to August in Temperate south-east. Dandelion-like daisy with nodding yellow flower buds; small sweet tubers that were a staple of south-eastern Aboriginal people.

Caution. Rare in the wild today; grow it rather than dig wild plants. Similar yellow daisies are weeds such as catsear.

Where does yam daisy grow in Australia?

Yam daisy grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, South Australia, Victoria. Regions: Temperate south-east.

How do you harvest yam daisy?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat yam daisy?
