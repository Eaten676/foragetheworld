Mallow

Malva parviflora
Also called Khubbeiza

Mallow is a wild green you can forage from November to April in Cities, farms and oases, Gulf coast and Desert, dunes and gravel plains. Soft, round, crinkle-edged leaves on long stalks and tiny pale pink flowers on farm edges, gardens and waste ground in winter.

When is mallow in season in the UAE?

Mallow is usually in season November to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mallow grow in the UAE?

Mallow grows across the UAE. Regions: Cities, farms and oases, Gulf coast, Desert, dunes and gravel plains.

How do you recognise mallow?

Soft, round, crinkle-edged leaves on long stalks and tiny pale pink flowers on farm edges, gardens and waste ground in winter.

How do you harvest mallow?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.

How do you eat mallow?

Cooked like spinach with onion, garlic and lemon.

How do you store mallow?

Blanch and freeze.

What is mallow good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with mallow?

  • Khubbeiza (mallow stew): Mallow leaves sautéed with onion, garlic, olive oil and lemon.
