Birch sap

Betula papyrifera
Also called Birch sap

Birch sap is a sap or starch you can forage from March to April in Northeast, Midwest and Alaska. Paper birch; sap rises in early spring just after maple season.

When is birch sap in season in the United States?

Birch sap is usually in season March to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does birch sap grow in the United States?

Birch sap grows in Alaska, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin. Regions: Northeast, Midwest, Alaska.

How do you recognise birch sap?

Paper birch; sap rises in early spring just after maple season.

How do you harvest birch sap?

Drill a small hole a few centimetres deep, collect for a few days only, then plug the hole with a twig.

How do you eat birch sap?

Drink fresh or boil to syrup (100:1).

How do you store birch sap?

Freeze sap.
