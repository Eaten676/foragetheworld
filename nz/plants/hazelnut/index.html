Hazelnut

Corylus avellana
Also called Hazelnut

Hazelnut is a wild nut you can forage from February to April in Canterbury, Otago and Southland and Top of the South. Multi-stemmed shrub with round, toothed leaves; nuts in frilly husks, ripe when they turn brown.

When is hazelnut in season in New Zealand?

Hazelnut is usually in season February to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does hazelnut grow in New Zealand?

Hazelnut grows in Canterbury, Marlborough, Nelson, Otago, Southland, Tasman. Regions: Canterbury, Otago and Southland, Top of the South.

How do you recognise hazelnut?

Multi-stemmed shrub with round, toothed leaves; nuts in frilly husks, ripe when they turn brown.

How do you harvest hazelnut?

Pick nuts as husks turn brown and the nut slips out, before squirrels get them; or gather fallen nuts.

How do you eat hazelnut?

Raw, roasted, in baking.

How do you store hazelnut?

Dry in the shell.

What is hazelnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.
