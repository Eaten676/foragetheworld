Lemon myrtle

Backhousia citriodora
Also called Lemon myrtle

Lemon myrtle is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round in Subtropical east coast and Tropical north. Rainforest tree with glossy, intensely lemon-scented leaves.

When is lemon myrtle in season in Australia?

Lemon myrtle is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lemon myrtle grow in Australia?

Lemon myrtle grows in New South Wales, Northern Territory, Queensland, Western Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Tropical north.

How do you recognise lemon myrtle?

Rainforest tree with glossy, intensely lemon-scented leaves.

How do you harvest lemon myrtle?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat lemon myrtle?

Tea, in shortbread, on fish and chicken.

How do you store lemon myrtle?

Dry leaves.

What can you cook with lemon myrtle?

  • Lemon myrtle iced tea: Lemon myrtle leaves steeped, chilled, with honey.
  • Lemon myrtle shortbread: Buttery shortbread scented with dried lemon myrtle.
  • Barramundi in native ginger leaf: Fish wrapped in native ginger leaves with lemon myrtle and grilled.
