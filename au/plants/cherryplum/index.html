Cherry plum

Prunus cerasifera
Also called Cherry plum

Cherry plum is a wild fruit you can forage from December to January in Temperate south-east, Tasmania and Subtropical east coast. Hedge tree flowering white in early spring; small round yellow or red plums in summer.

When is cherry plum in season in Australia?

Cherry plum is usually in season December to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cherry plum grow in Australia?

Cherry plum grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria. Regions: Temperate south-east, Tasmania, Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise cherry plum?

Hedge tree flowering white in early spring; small round yellow or red plums in summer.

How do you harvest cherry plum?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat cherry plum?

Fresh, jam, plum sauce.

How do you store cherry plum?

Jam.

What is cherry plum good for?

Vitamin C.
