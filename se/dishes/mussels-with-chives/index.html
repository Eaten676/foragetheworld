Mussels with chives

Mussels with chives is made with wild blue mussels, chives, a favourite in Götaland (south).

How do you make mussels with chives?

West-coast mussels with wine and wild chives.

Wild ingredients

  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
  • Wild chives (Allium schoenoprasum): in season June to August
