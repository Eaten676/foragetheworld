Wild banana flower & stem

Musa balbisiana
Also called Kela phool, Bale hoovu, Vazhaipoo, Kol posola

Wild banana flower & stem is a wild green you can forage all year round in Northeast, South India, East and West coast and Deccan. Wild seeded bananas grow along Northeast and Ghats forest streams. The purple bud and inner stem are eaten.

When is wild banana flower & stem in season in India?

Wild banana flower & stem is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild banana flower & stem grow in India?

Wild banana flower & stem grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: Northeast, South India, East, West coast and Deccan.

How do you recognise wild banana flower & stem?

Wild seeded bananas grow along Northeast and Ghats forest streams. The purple bud and inner stem are eaten.

How do you harvest wild banana flower & stem?

Cut the purple bud once the last banana hands have formed.

How do you eat wild banana flower & stem?

Vazhaipoo vadai, thoran, khar, curries.

How do you store wild banana flower & stem?

Use fresh. Keep the stem in buttermilk water to stop browning.

What can you cook with wild banana flower & stem?

  • Vazhaipoo vadai: Chopped banana flower in a coarse chana dal batter with onion and curry leaves, shallow-fried.
