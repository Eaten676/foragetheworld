Birch sap

Betula papyrifera
Also called Birch sap, Eau de bouleau

Birch sap is a sap or starch you can forage from March to April in Quebec, Ontario, Atlantic Canada and The North. Paper birch; sap rises in early spring just after maple season.

When is birch sap in season in Canada?

Birch sap is usually in season March to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does birch sap grow in Canada?

Birch sap grows in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Northwest Territories, Nova Scotia, Nunavut, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Yukon. Regions: Quebec, Ontario, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise birch sap?

Paper birch; sap rises in early spring just after maple season.

How do you harvest birch sap?

Drill a small hole a few centimetres deep, collect for a few days only, then plug the hole with a twig.

How do you eat birch sap?

Drink fresh or boil to syrup (100:1).

How do you store birch sap?

Freeze sap.

What is birch sap good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with birch sap?

  • Birch syrup glazed salmon: Salmon brushed with birch syrup and roasted.
