Dandelion and burdock

Dandelion and burdock is made with wild dandelion, burdock root, a favourite in Northern England.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make dandelion and burdock?

Old northern English soft drink brewed from the roots, with ginger and sugar.

Wild ingredients

  • Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale): in season March to October
  • Burdock root (Arctium lappa): in season April to October
