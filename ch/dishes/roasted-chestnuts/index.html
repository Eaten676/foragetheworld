Roasted chestnuts (Marroni)

Roasted chestnuts (Marroni) is made with wild sweet chestnut, a favourite in Ticino and southern valleys and Swiss Plateau.

Open in Forage SwitzerlandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make roasted chestnuts (marroni)?

Scored chestnuts roasted until the shells split, sold hot in winter streets.

Wild ingredients

  • Sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa): in season October to November
