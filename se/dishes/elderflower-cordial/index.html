Elderflower cordial (flädersaft)

Elderflower cordial (flädersaft) is made with wild elderflower, a favourite in Götaland (south) and Svealand (middle).

Open in Forage SwedenShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower cordial (flädersaft)?

Flowers steeped with lemon and citric acid, then sweetened. Served at Midsummer.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season June to July
