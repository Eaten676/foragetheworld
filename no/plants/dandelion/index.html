Dandelion

Taraxacum officinale
Also called Løvetann

Dandelion is a wild green you can forage from April to September across Norway. Rosette of jagged leaves with a single yellow flower on each hollow, milky stem.

When is dandelion in season in Norway?

Dandelion is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does dandelion grow in Norway?

Dandelion grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise dandelion?

Rosette of jagged leaves with a single yellow flower on each hollow, milky stem.

How do you harvest dandelion?

Pick young leaves from plants not yet in flower; flowers on a sunny morning when fully open; dig roots in autumn with permission.

How do you eat dandelion?

Young leaves in salad, flowers as syrup ('dandelion honey'), roasted root as coffee.

How do you store dandelion?

Flowers into syrup; dry roots.

What is dandelion good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.
