Saguaro fruit syrup

Saguaro fruit syrup is made with wild saguaro fruit, a favourite in Southwest.

How do you make saguaro fruit syrup?

Saguaro pulp boiled down into a dark, fig-like syrup.

Wild ingredients

  • Saguaro fruit (Carnegiea gigantea): in season June to July
