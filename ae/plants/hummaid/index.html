Bladder dock

Rumex vesicarius
Also called Hummaid, Hambaz

Bladder dock is a wild green you can forage from December to March in Hajar Mountains and wadis, Desert, dunes and gravel plains and Gulf coast. Fleshy, pale-green arrow-shaped leaves and pink, papery, winged seed cases. Carpets mountain slopes and sandy roadsides after winter rain and tastes sour, like sorrel.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; eat in moderation.

When is bladder dock in season in the UAE?

Bladder dock is usually in season December to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bladder dock grow in the UAE?

Bladder dock grows across the UAE. Regions: Hajar Mountains and wadis, Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Gulf coast.

How do you recognise bladder dock?

Fleshy, pale-green arrow-shaped leaves and pink, papery, winged seed cases. Carpets mountain slopes and sandy roadsides after winter rain and tastes sour, like sorrel.

How do you harvest bladder dock?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.

How do you eat bladder dock?

Raw in salads with lemon, or cooked like spinach.

How do you store bladder dock?

Best fresh.

What is bladder dock good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with bladder dock?

  • Hummaid salad: Sour bladder dock leaves with tomato, onion and lemon.
