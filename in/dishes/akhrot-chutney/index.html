Akhrot chutney

Akhrot chutney is made with wild walnut, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make akhrot chutney?

Walnuts ground with curd, green chilli, mint and salt. A Kashmiri favourite.

Wild ingredients

  • Walnut (Juglans regia): in season September to October
