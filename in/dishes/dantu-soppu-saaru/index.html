Dantu soppu saaru

Dantu soppu saaru is made with wild amaranth, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make dantu soppu saaru?

Amaranth simmered with dal, tamarind and rasam powder, tempered with garlic.

Wild ingredients

  • Amaranth (Amaranthus viridis): in season June to November
