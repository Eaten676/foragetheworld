Pork and pūhā boil-up

Pork and pūhā boil-up is made with wild pūhā, watercress.

Open in Forage New ZealandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make pork and pūhā boil-up?

Pork bones, potatoes, kūmara and pūhā or watercress simmered with dumplings.

Wild ingredients

  • Pūhā (Sonchus oleraceus): in season all year round
  • Watercress (Nasturtium officinale): in season August to December
