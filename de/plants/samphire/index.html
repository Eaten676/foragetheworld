Marsh samphire

Salicornia europaea
Also called Queller

Marsh samphire is a wild green you can forage from June to August in North German lowlands and coast. Fleshy, jointed, leafless green stems like tiny cacti on tidal mud flats. Salty and crunchy.

Caution. Cut, don't uproot, and check whether picking is allowed on that marsh; many are nature reserves.

When is marsh samphire in season in Germany?

Marsh samphire is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does marsh samphire grow in Germany?

Marsh samphire grows in Bremen, Hamburg, Lower Saxony, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Schleswig-Holstein. Regions: North German lowlands and coast.

How do you recognise marsh samphire?

Fleshy, jointed, leafless green stems like tiny cacti on tidal mud flats. Salty and crunchy.

How do you harvest marsh samphire?

Cut the green tips with scissors at low tide rather than pulling up the plant.

How do you eat marsh samphire?

Steamed with butter alongside fish, or pickled.

How do you store marsh samphire?

Pickle.

What is marsh samphire good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.
