Elderflower and gooseberry fool

Elderflower and gooseberry fool is made with wild gooseberry, elderflower, a favourite in Götaland (south).

Open in Forage SwedenShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower and gooseberry fool?

Stewed gooseberries with elderflower folded into cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild gooseberry (Ribes uva-crispa): in season July to August
  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season June to July
