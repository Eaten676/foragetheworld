Jackfruit chips

Jackfruit chips is made with wild jackfruit, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make jackfruit chips?

Firm unripe bulbs sliced thin and fried in coconut oil with salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Jackfruit (Artocarpus heterophyllus): in season March to July
