Mussels in white wine (muslinger)

Mussels in white wine (muslinger) is made with wild blue mussels, a favourite in Jutland.

Open in Forage DenmarkShare on WhatsApp

How do you make mussels in white wine (muslinger)?

Limfjord mussels steamed with wine, shallots and parsley.

Wild ingredients

  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
