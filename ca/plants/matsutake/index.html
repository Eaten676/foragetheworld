Pine mushroom

Tricholoma murrillianum
Also called Pine mushroom, Matsutake, Matsutaké

Pine mushroom is a wild mushroom you can forage from September to October in British Columbia and Quebec. Firm, white to brown-streaked mushroom with a cottony ring, pushing up under the moss of coastal and lodgepole pine forests. Spicy, cinnamon-like smell.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: deadly white amanitas grow in the same forests. Learn it with an expert, and check for the spicy smell and no cup at the base.

When is pine mushroom in season in Canada?

Pine mushroom is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does pine mushroom grow in Canada?

Pine mushroom grows in British Columbia, Quebec. Regions: British Columbia, Quebec.

How do you recognise pine mushroom?

Firm, white to brown-streaked mushroom with a cottony ring, pushing up under the moss of coastal and lodgepole pine forests. Spicy, cinnamon-like smell.

How do you harvest pine mushroom?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat pine mushroom?

Sliced thin into rice, soup or grilled; prized in Japan.

How do you store pine mushroom?

Wrap in paper and refrigerate; use within days.

What is pine mushroom good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with pine mushroom?

  • Matsutake rice: Thin-sliced pine mushrooms steamed with rice, soy and dashi.
