Sol kadhi

Sol kadhi is made with wild kokum, a favourite in West coast and Deccan and South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sol kadhi?

Soak kokum in warm water, then mix the pink extract with coconut milk, crushed garlic, green chilli and salt. Drink chilled after meals.

Wild ingredients

  • Kokum (Garcinia indica): in season March to May
