Kachnar buds

Bauhinia variegata
Also called Kachnar, Kuler, Koliar

Kachnar buds is a edible flower or tea you can forage from February to April in Northern plains, Himalaya, Central forests, East and Northeast. Orchid-like pink and white flowers on a camel-hoof-leaf tree. Buds are picked before opening.

When is kachnar buds in season in India?

Kachnar buds is usually in season February to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kachnar buds grow in India?

Kachnar buds grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Ladakh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Punjab, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Northern plains, Himalaya, Central forests, East, Northeast.

How do you recognise kachnar buds?

Orchid-like pink and white flowers on a camel-hoof-leaf tree. Buds are picked before opening.

How do you harvest kachnar buds?

Pick plump buds before they open.

How do you eat kachnar buds?

Kachnar ki sabzi, raita, pickle. Young leaves (kuler sago) in Odisha.

How do you store kachnar buds?

Blanch and sun-dry the buds.

What can you cook with kachnar buds?

  • Kachnar ki sabzi: Blanched kachnar buds cooked with onion, curd and garam masala.
