White and red deadnettle

Lamium album
Also called Taubnessel

White and red deadnettle is a wild green you can forage from March to September across Germany. Nettle-like leaves without the sting, square stems and white or pink hooded flowers.

When is white and red deadnettle in season in Germany?

White and red deadnettle is usually in season March to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does white and red deadnettle grow in Germany?

White and red deadnettle grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise white and red deadnettle?

Nettle-like leaves without the sting, square stems and white or pink hooded flowers.

How do you harvest white and red deadnettle?

Pick the top few leaves and flowers; no gloves needed.

How do you eat white and red deadnettle?

Young tops raw or cooked; flowers sucked for nectar.

How do you store white and red deadnettle?

Dry for tea.

What is white and red deadnettle good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.
