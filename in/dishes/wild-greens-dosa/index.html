Wild greens dosa

Wild greens dosa is made with wild amaranth, Malabar spinach, sessile joyweed, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make wild greens dosa?

Ground rice and dal batter with a handful of greens. No fermentation needed.

Wild ingredients

  • Amaranth (Amaranthus viridis): in season June to November
  • Malabar spinach (Basella alba): in season June to November
  • Sessile joyweed (Alternanthera sessilis): in season all year round
