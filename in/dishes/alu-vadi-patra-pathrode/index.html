Alu vadi / patra / pathrode

Alu vadi / patra / pathrode is made with wild colocasia leaves, a favourite in West coast and Deccan and South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make alu vadi / patra / pathrode?

Colocasia leaves layered with spiced gram or rice batter, rolled, steamed, sliced and tempered.

Note. Cook thoroughly with tamarind or kokum.

Wild ingredients

  • Colocasia leaves (Colocasia esculenta): in season June to September
