Common sorrel

Rumex acetosa
Also called Sorrel

Common sorrel is a wild green you can forage from April to August across the UK. Arrow-shaped leaves with downward-pointing lobes in meadows; reddish flower spikes in early summer. Lemony.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; eat in moderation.

When is common sorrel in season in the UK?

Common sorrel is usually in season April to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does common sorrel grow in the UK?

Common sorrel grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise common sorrel?

Arrow-shaped leaves with downward-pointing lobes in meadows; reddish flower spikes in early summer. Lemony.

How do you harvest common sorrel?

Pick young leaves from the base before the plant sends up its flower spike.

How do you eat common sorrel?

Soup, green sauce for fish, salads.

How do you store common sorrel?

Best fresh; purée and freeze.

What is common sorrel good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.
