Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus
Also called Brombeere, Mûre, Mora

Blackberry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to September in Swiss Plateau, Jura and Ticino and southern valleys. Thorny arching brambles with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen red to black.

When is blackberry in season in Switzerland?

Blackberry is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blackberry grow in Switzerland?

Blackberry grows in Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. Regions: Swiss Plateau, Jura, Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you recognise blackberry?

Thorny arching brambles with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen red to black.

How do you harvest blackberry?

Pick glossy, fully black berries that come away easily; wear long sleeves and avoid low berries by dog-walking paths.

How do you eat blackberry?

Fresh, crumble, jam, jelly.

How do you store blackberry?

Freeze on trays, then bag.

What is blackberry good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.
