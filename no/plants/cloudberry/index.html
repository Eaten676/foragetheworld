Cloudberry

Rubus chamaemorus
Also called Multe, Molte

Cloudberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across Norway. Low plant of northern bogs with crinkled, lobed leaves; single amber-orange berries like a raspberry.

When is cloudberry in season in Norway?

Cloudberry is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cloudberry grow in Norway?

Cloudberry grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise cloudberry?

Low plant of northern bogs with crinkled, lobed leaves; single amber-orange berries like a raspberry.

How do you harvest cloudberry?

Pick when amber and soft, with the green husk easily separated; check whether picking is restricted on that land.

How do you eat cloudberry?

Cream, jam with waffles or ice cream.

How do you store cloudberry?

Freeze or jam.

What is cloudberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with cloudberry?

  • Cloudberry cream (multekrem): Cloudberries folded into whipped cream; a Christmas dessert.
