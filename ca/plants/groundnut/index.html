Groundnut

Apios americana
Also called Groundnut, Apios d'Amérique

Groundnut is a root or shoot you can forage from October to May in Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Climbing vine with leaves of 5–7 leaflets and maroon pea flowers; strings of tubers like beads underground.

Caution. Always cook.

When is groundnut in season in Canada?

Groundnut is usually in season October to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does groundnut grow in Canada?

Groundnut grows in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise groundnut?

Climbing vine with leaves of 5–7 leaflets and maroon pea flowers; strings of tubers like beads underground.

How do you harvest groundnut?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat groundnut?

Boiled or roasted like potatoes.

How do you store groundnut?

Store in sand.

What is groundnut good for?

Calories, Protein.
