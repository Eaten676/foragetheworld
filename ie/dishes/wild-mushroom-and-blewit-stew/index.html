Wild mushroom and blewit stew is made with wild wood blewit, oyster mushroom.

How do you make wild mushroom and blewit stew?

Autumn mushrooms braised with onions and stout.

Wild ingredients

  • Wood blewit (Lepista nuda): in season October to December
  • Oyster mushroom (Pleurotus ostreatus): in season October to March
