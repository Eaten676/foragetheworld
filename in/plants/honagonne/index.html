Sessile joyweed

Alternanthera sessilis
Also called Honagonne, Ponnanganni, Matsyakshi

Sessile joyweed is a wild green you can forage all year round in South India, East, Central forests, Northern plains and West coast and Deccan. Low creeping green with small white flower heads in lake beds and damp ground. A favourite of Bengaluru's foragers.

Caution. Lake beds are often polluted. Pick only from clean ground.

When is sessile joyweed in season in India?

Sessile joyweed is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sessile joyweed grow in India?

Sessile joyweed grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, East, Central forests, Northern plains, West coast and Deccan.

How do you recognise sessile joyweed?

Low creeping green with small white flower heads in lake beds and damp ground. A favourite of Bengaluru's foragers.

How do you harvest sessile joyweed?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat sessile joyweed?

Saaru, palya, poriyal, scrambled with eggs.

How do you store sessile joyweed?

Shade-dry and powder.

What is sessile joyweed good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with sessile joyweed?

  • Honagonne saaru: Sessile joyweed in a light dal saaru with garlic tempering.
  • Wild greens dosa: Ground rice and dal batter with a handful of greens. No fermentation needed.
