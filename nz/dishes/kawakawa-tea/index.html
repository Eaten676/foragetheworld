Kawakawa tea

Kawakawa tea is made with wild kawakawa.

Open in Forage New ZealandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kawakawa tea?

Holey kawakawa leaves steeped in hot water, a traditional rongoā drink.

Wild ingredients

  • Kawakawa (Piper excelsum): in season all year round
