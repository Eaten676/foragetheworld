Boab

Adansonia gregorii
Also called Boab

Boab is a wild fruit you can forage from April to September in Tropical north. Bottle-trunked tree of the Kimberley; hard pods hold powdery, sherbet-like pulp around seeds.

When is boab in season in Australia?

Boab is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does boab grow in Australia?

Boab grows in Northern Territory, Queensland, Western Australia. Regions: Tropical north.

How do you recognise boab?

Bottle-trunked tree of the Kimberley; hard pods hold powdery, sherbet-like pulp around seeds.

How do you harvest boab?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat boab?

Pulp as a snack or in drinks; young roots eaten too.

How do you store boab?

Dry pulp.

What is boab good for?

Vitamin C, Calories.

What can you cook with boab?

  • Boab pulp sherbet drink: Boab pulp whisked into water, a Kimberley treat.
