Karvandachi chutney

Karvandachi chutney is made with wild karonda, a favourite in West coast and Deccan.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make karvandachi chutney?

Raw karvanda ground with green chilli, jaggery and salt. A Maharashtrian monsoon side.

Wild ingredients

  • Karonda (Carissa carandas): in season July to September
