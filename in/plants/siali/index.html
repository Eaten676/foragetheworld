Siali seeds

Phanera vahlii
Also called Siali, Mahul, Maljhan

Siali seeds is a wild seed or grain you can forage from March to May in Central forests, East and Himalaya. Giant forest climber whose big leaves are stitched into leaf plates. Its large woody pods hold flat, nutty seeds.

When is siali seeds in season in India?

Siali seeds is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does siali seeds grow in India?

Siali seeds grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Ladakh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Sikkim, Telangana, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Central forests, East, Himalaya.

How do you recognise siali seeds?

Giant forest climber whose big leaves are stitched into leaf plates. Its large woody pods hold flat, nutty seeds.

How do you harvest siali seeds?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat siali seeds?

Seeds roasted and eaten as a snack.

How do you store siali seeds?

Dry the seeds and store them in the shell.

What is siali seeds good for?

Protein, Fat, Calories.

What can you cook with siali seeds?

  • Roasted siali seeds: Siali seeds roasted in their shells, then cracked and eaten warm. A tribal Odisha snack.
