Broadleaf plantain

Plantago major
Also called Plantain, Plantain

Broadleaf plantain is a wild green you can forage from May to October across Canada. Rosette of broad oval leaves with strong parallel veins and a stringy stem, in lawns and paths.

When is broadleaf plantain in season in Canada?

Broadleaf plantain is usually in season May to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does broadleaf plantain grow in Canada?

Broadleaf plantain grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise broadleaf plantain?

Rosette of broad oval leaves with strong parallel veins and a stringy stem, in lawns and paths.

How do you harvest broadleaf plantain?

Pick young, soft leaves from the centre of the rosette, and green flower buds before they open.

How do you eat broadleaf plantain?

Very young leaves raw or steamed; seeds on the spikes can be eaten.

How do you store broadleaf plantain?

Dry leaves.

What is broadleaf plantain good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.
