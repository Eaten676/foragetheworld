Rose hip syrup

Rose hip syrup is made with wild rose hips.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make rose hip syrup?

Wartime vitamin C syrup of rose hips, strained twice to remove the hairs.

Wild ingredients

  • Rose hips (Rosa canina): in season September to November
