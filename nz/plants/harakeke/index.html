Harakeke nectar

Phormium tenax
Also called Harakeke nectar

Harakeke nectar is a edible flower or tea you can forage from October to December across New Zealand. Flax with long sword leaves and tall stalks of red flowers full of nectar.

When is harakeke nectar in season in New Zealand?

Harakeke nectar is usually in season October to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does harakeke nectar grow in New Zealand?

Harakeke nectar grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise harakeke nectar?

Flax with long sword leaves and tall stalks of red flowers full of nectar.

How do you harvest harakeke nectar?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat harakeke nectar?

Nectar sipped or shaken into water as a sweet drink.

How do you store harakeke nectar?

Best fresh.
