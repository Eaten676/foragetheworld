Good King Henry

Blitum bonus-henricus
Also called Guter Heinrich

Good King Henry is a wild green you can forage from April to July in South and the Alps. Arrow-shaped, slightly mealy leaves around mountain huts and pastures; flower spikes like spinach.

Caution. Contains oxalates; cook it.

When is Good King Henry in season in Germany?

Good King Henry is usually in season April to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does Good King Henry grow in Germany?

Good King Henry grows in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria. Regions: South and the Alps.

How do you recognise Good King Henry?

Arrow-shaped, slightly mealy leaves around mountain huts and pastures; flower spikes like spinach.

How do you harvest Good King Henry?

Pick leaves and young flower shoots in spring, leaving the rootstock.

How do you eat Good King Henry?

Cook like spinach; shoots like asparagus.

How do you store Good King Henry?

Blanch and freeze.

What is Good King Henry good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.
