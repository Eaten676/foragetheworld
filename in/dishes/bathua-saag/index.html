Bathua saag

Bathua saag is made with wild bathua, stinging nettle, a favourite in Northern plains.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bathua saag?

Bathua slow-cooked with mustard greens, garlic and makki atta, finished with ghee.

Wild ingredients

  • Bathua (Chenopodium album): in season November to March
  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season February to November
