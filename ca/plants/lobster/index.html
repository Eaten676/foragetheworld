Lobster mushroom

Hypomyces lactifluorum
Also called Lobster mushroom, Champignon homard

Lobster mushroom is a wild mushroom you can forage from July to September in British Columbia, Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Bright orange-red, hard, misshapen mushroom with no real gills: a mould that has taken over a Russula or Lactarius. Seafood smell.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: only firm, bright specimens; discard soft or white-spotted ones.

When is lobster mushroom in season in Canada?

Lobster mushroom is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lobster mushroom grow in Canada?

Lobster mushroom grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: British Columbia, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise lobster mushroom?

Bright orange-red, hard, misshapen mushroom with no real gills: a mould that has taken over a Russula or Lactarius. Seafood smell.

How do you harvest lobster mushroom?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat lobster mushroom?

Sliced and sautéed, in bisques and risotto; holds its colour.

How do you store lobster mushroom?

Dry or sauté and freeze.

What is lobster mushroom good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with lobster mushroom?

  • Lobster mushroom bisque: Lobster mushrooms simmered with shallots and cream, then blended.
