Black locust fritters

Black locust fritters is made with wild black locust flowers, a favourite in Southeast and Midwest.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make black locust fritters?

Flower clusters dipped in light batter, fried and dusted with sugar.

Wild ingredients

  • Black locust flowers (Robinia pseudoacacia): in season May to June
