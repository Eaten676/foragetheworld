Wild yam

Dioscorea
Also called Jangli kand, Kaadu genasu, Kaattu kizhangu

Wild yam is a root or shoot you can forage from October to February in South India, East, Central forests and Northeast. Twining vine with heart-shaped leaves. The tuber is dug in the dry season.

Caution. Many wild yams are toxic raw. Only dig ones a local forager recognises, and slice, soak and boil them well.

When is wild yam in season in India?

Wild yam is usually in season October to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild yam grow in India?

Wild yam grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: South India, East, Central forests, Northeast.

How do you recognise wild yam?

Twining vine with heart-shaped leaves. The tuber is dug in the dry season.

How do you harvest wild yam?

Follow the vine down and dig around the tuber. Traditionally the top of the tuber is left attached so it regrows. Go with someone who knows the safe species.

How do you eat wild yam?

Boiled, then curried or roasted.

How do you store wild yam?

Slice, soak, boil, then sun-dry the chips.

What can be confused with wild yam?

Glory lily (Gloriosa superba), rated deadly. Glory lily leaves end in curling tendril tips. Wild yam leaves are heart-shaped, with no tendrils. Glory lily has spectacular red-and-yellow wavy flowers. Yams have tiny, dull flowers. Glory lily tubers are white and forked into a V or L shape. Yam tubers are rounded or long, and never forked.

What is wild yam good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with wild yam?

  • Wild yam curry: Soaked and boiled yam chunks in a coconut or mustard curry.
