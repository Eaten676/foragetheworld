Bladderwrack

Fucus vesiculosus
Also called Blæretang

Bladderwrack is a wild green you can forage from March to September across Denmark. The common brown seaweed of rocky shores, with paired air bladders along a midrib.

Caution. Very high in iodine: small amounts only, and avoid with thyroid problems.

When is bladderwrack in season in Denmark?

Bladderwrack is usually in season March to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bladderwrack grow in Denmark?

Bladderwrack grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise bladderwrack?

The common brown seaweed of rocky shores, with paired air bladders along a midrib.

How do you harvest bladderwrack?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat bladderwrack?

Young tips in stews, steamed with shellfish, or dried for broth and tea.

How do you store bladderwrack?

Dry.

What is bladderwrack good for?

Iron & calcium.
