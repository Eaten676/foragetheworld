Black trumpet

Craterellus fallax / C. cornucopioides
Also called Black trumpet, Trompette des morts

Black trumpet is a wild mushroom you can forage from July to October in Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada and British Columbia. Thin, hollow, black-grey trumpets with smooth undersides, hiding in moss under oaks and beech.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: learn it in person with an experienced forager before eating, and always cook it.

When is black trumpet in season in Canada?

Black trumpet is usually in season July to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does black trumpet grow in Canada?

Black trumpet grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, British Columbia.

How do you recognise black trumpet?

Thin, hollow, black-grey trumpets with smooth undersides, hiding in moss under oaks and beech.

How do you harvest black trumpet?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat black trumpet?

Sautéed, in risotto and pasta, or dried and powdered.

How do you store black trumpet?

Dry.

What is black trumpet good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.
