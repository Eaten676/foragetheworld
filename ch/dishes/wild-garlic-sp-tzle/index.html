Wild garlic spätzle (Bärlauchspätzli)

Wild garlic spätzle (Bärlauchspätzli) is made with wild garlic, a favourite in Swiss Plateau.

How do you make wild garlic spätzle (bärlauchspätzli)?

Spätzle dough blended with wild garlic, with browned butter and cheese.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild garlic (Allium ursinum): in season March to May
