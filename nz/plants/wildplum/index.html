Wild plums

Prunus cerasifera
Also called Wild plums

Wild plums is a wild fruit you can forage from December to February in Canterbury, Otago and Southland, Top of the South and East Coast and Hawke's Bay. Introduced hedge plums along roads and rivers; small red or yellow fruit.

When is wild plums in season in New Zealand?

Wild plums is usually in season December to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild plums grow in New Zealand?

Wild plums grows in Canterbury, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Marlborough, Nelson, Otago, Southland, Tasman. Regions: Canterbury, Otago and Southland, Top of the South, East Coast and Hawke's Bay.

How do you recognise wild plums?

Introduced hedge plums along roads and rivers; small red or yellow fruit.

How do you harvest wild plums?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat wild plums?

Fresh, jam, sauce.

How do you store wild plums?

Jam.

What is wild plums good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild plums?

  • Wild plum sauce: Plums cooked with spices into a sauce for meat.
