Amla juice

Amla juice is made with wild amla, a favourite in Northern plains, West coast and Deccan and South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make amla juice?

Deseed amla, blend with water, ginger and a little honey or jaggery, then strain. Drink fresh, since vitamin C fades fast.

Wild ingredients

  • Amla (Phyllanthus emblica): in season November to February
