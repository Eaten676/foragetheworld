Kachri

Cucumis callosus
Also called Kachri, Kachar, Kachariya

Kachri is a wild fruit you can forage from October to December in Thar and dry northwest, Northern plains and West coast and Deccan. Wild melon of the Thar, plum-sized and striped. Grows on its own in sandy fields after the rains.

When is kachri in season in India?

Kachri is usually in season October to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kachri grow in India?

Kachri grows in Bihar, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand. Regions: Thar and dry northwest, Northern plains, West coast and Deccan.

How do you recognise kachri?

Wild melon of the Thar, plum-sized and striped. Grows on its own in sandy fields after the rains.

How do you harvest kachri?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat kachri?

Kachri chutney and sabzi. The powder tenderises meat.

How do you store kachri?

Slice and sun-dry, then powder.

What can be confused with kachri?

Bitter apple (Citrullus colocynthis), rated toxic. Bitter apple is fiercely bitter and a strong purgative. Even desert herders avoid it. Its fruits are tennis-ball sized, smooth, green with yellow mottling, and turn yellow as they dry. Its vines and leaves look very like matira, so only pick melons from vines a local has confirmed.

What is kachri good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with kachri?

  • Kachri chutney: Dried kachri ground with garlic, red chilli and salt. Fiery Rajasthani chutney.
  • Panchkuta: The Thar's five-ingredient desert curry, cooked dry with spices and oil.
