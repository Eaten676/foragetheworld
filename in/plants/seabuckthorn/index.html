Seabuckthorn

Hippophae rhamnoides
Also called Tsestalulu, Chharma

Seabuckthorn is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in Himalaya. Spiny shrub of Ladakh, Lahaul and Spiti, covered in bright orange berries.

When is seabuckthorn in season in India?

Seabuckthorn is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does seabuckthorn grow in India?

Seabuckthorn grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise seabuckthorn?

Spiny shrub of Ladakh, Lahaul and Spiti, covered in bright orange berries.

How do you harvest seabuckthorn?

Very thorny. Pick with gloves, or cut a few short fruiting twigs and freeze them to shake the berries off.

How do you eat seabuckthorn?

Juice, jam, tea.

How do you store seabuckthorn?

Freeze the berries, or make juice concentrate.

What is seabuckthorn good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A, Fat.

What can you cook with seabuckthorn?

  • Seabuckthorn juice: Crush berries, strain through cloth, and sweeten with honey. Very sour and full of vitamin C.
