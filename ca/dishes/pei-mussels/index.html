PEI mussels

PEI mussels is made with wild blue mussels, a favourite in Atlantic Canada.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make pei mussels?

Mussels steamed with white wine, garlic and herbs.

Wild ingredients

  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season October to April
