Mesquite pods

Prosopis glandulosa
Also called Honey mesquite

Mesquite pods is a wild seed or grain you can forage from July to September in Southwest, Great Plains and California. Thorny desert tree with feathery leaves and long, straw-coloured pods that taste of caramel.

Caution. Only use dry, sound pods; discard mouldy ones.

When is mesquite pods in season in the United States?

Mesquite pods is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mesquite pods grow in the United States?

Mesquite pods grows in Arizona, California, Colorado, Hawaii, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Mexico, North Dakota, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Texas, Utah. Regions: Southwest, Great Plains, California.

How do you recognise mesquite pods?

Thorny desert tree with feathery leaves and long, straw-coloured pods that taste of caramel.

How do you harvest mesquite pods?

Shake or strip ripe seed heads into a bag when they rattle and fall easily. Winnow out the chaff in a breeze.

How do you eat mesquite pods?

Ground into sweet flour for pancakes and cookies.

How do you store mesquite pods?

Dry pods fully, then grind.

What is mesquite pods good for?

Calories, Protein.

What can you cook with mesquite pods?

  • Mesquite cookies: Sweet mesquite flour shortbread with a caramel taste.
