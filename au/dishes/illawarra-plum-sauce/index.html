Illawarra plum sauce

Illawarra plum sauce is made with wild illawarra plum, a favourite in Subtropical east coast.

How do you make illawarra plum sauce?

Plums cooked with ginger and vinegar for duck and pork.

Wild ingredients

  • Illawarra plum (Podocarpus elatus): in season March to July
