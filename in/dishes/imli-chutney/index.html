Imli chutney

Imli chutney is made with wild tamarind, a favourite in Northern plains, Central forests and West coast and Deccan.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make imli chutney?

Tamarind cooked down with jaggery, dry ginger and roasted cumin.

Wild ingredients

  • Tamarind (Tamarindus indica): in season January to April
