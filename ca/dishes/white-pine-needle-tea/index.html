White pine needle tea

White pine needle tea is made with wild white pine needles, a favourite in Ontario and Quebec.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make white pine needle tea?

Chopped fresh needles steeped (not boiled) for a lemony, vitamin C-rich tea.

Wild ingredients

  • White pine needles (Pinus strobus): in season all year round
