Chaga

Inonotus obliquus
Also called Sprängticka

Chaga is a wild mushroom you can forage all year round across Sweden. Black, cracked, charcoal-like canker on living birch, rusty orange inside.

Caution. High in oxalates: drink occasionally. Only harvest part of it, from living birch, with permission.

When is chaga in season in Sweden?

Chaga is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chaga grow in Sweden?

Chaga grows across Sweden. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle), Norrland (north).

How do you recognise chaga?

Black, cracked, charcoal-like canker on living birch, rusty orange inside.

How do you harvest chaga?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat chaga?

Simmered for hours as a dark tea.

How do you store chaga?

Dry in chunks.

What can you cook with chaga?

  • Chaga tea (sprängticka): Chaga simmered into tea.
