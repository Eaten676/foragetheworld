Bamboo shoots

Bambusa
Also called Bans karil, Kalale, Khorisa, Mesu

Bamboo shoots is a root or shoot you can forage from June to September in Northeast, South India, East and Central forests. Young shoots pushing up at the base of clumps in the monsoon. Fibrous and filling.

Caution. Raw shoots contain cyanide-forming compounds. Peel, slice, boil, and discard the water. Forest bamboo is often protected.

When is bamboo shoots in season in India?

Bamboo shoots is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bamboo shoots grow in India?

Bamboo shoots grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: Northeast, South India, East, Central forests.

How do you recognise bamboo shoots?

Young shoots pushing up at the base of clumps in the monsoon. Fibrous and filling.

How do you harvest bamboo shoots?

Cut shoots 15–30 cm tall at ground level with a sharp knife, taking only one or two per clump, and only from village or private clumps.

How do you eat bamboo shoots?

Curries, pork dishes in the Northeast, kalale palya in Malnad.

How do you store bamboo shoots?

Ferment in jars (khorisa, mesu), or boil and sun-dry.

What can you cook with bamboo shoots?

  • Kalale palya: Boiled bamboo shoots cooked dry with coconut and chilli. A Malnad monsoon dish.
  • Bamboo shoot pork: Pork cooked with fermented bamboo shoot and chilli. A Naga and Assamese classic.
