Moules with dill (blåmusslor)

Moules with dill (blåmusslor) is made with wild blue mussels, a favourite in Götaland (south).

Open in Forage SwedenShare on WhatsApp

How do you make moules with dill (blåmusslor)?

West coast mussels steamed with white wine and dill.

Wild ingredients

  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
