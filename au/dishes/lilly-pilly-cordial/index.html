Lilly pilly cordial

Lilly pilly cordial is made with wild lilly pilly, a favourite in Subtropical east coast and Temperate south-east.

Open in Forage AustraliaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make lilly pilly cordial?

Berries simmered with sugar and lemon, strained.

Wild ingredients

  • Lilly pilly (Syzygium smithii): in season February to June
