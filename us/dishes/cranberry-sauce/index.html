Cranberry sauce

Cranberry sauce is made with wild cranberry, a favourite in Northeast and Midwest.

How do you make cranberry sauce?

Cranberries simmered with sugar and orange until they pop.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild cranberry (Vaccinium macrocarpon): in season September to October
