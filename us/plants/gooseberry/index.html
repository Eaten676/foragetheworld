Wild gooseberry

Ribes cynosbati
Also called Wild gooseberry

Wild gooseberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across the United States. Spiny shrub with lobed leaves; prickly green to purple berries.

When is wild gooseberry in season in the United States?

Wild gooseberry is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild gooseberry grow in the United States?

Wild gooseberry grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise wild gooseberry?

Spiny shrub with lobed leaves; prickly green to purple berries.

How do you harvest wild gooseberry?

Pick when berries soften and colour; wear gloves against the spines.

How do you eat wild gooseberry?

Jam, pie, fool.

How do you store wild gooseberry?

Freeze.

What is wild gooseberry good for?

Vitamin C.
