Sea asparagus salad

Sea asparagus salad is made with wild pickleweed, a favourite in British Columbia and Atlantic Canada.

How do you make sea asparagus salad?

Blanched sea asparagus with sesame oil and vinegar, a BC and Maritime favourite.

Wild ingredients

  • Pickleweed (Salicornia pacifica): in season June to September
