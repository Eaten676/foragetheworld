Witchetty grub

Endoxyla leucomochla
Also called Witchetty grub

Witchetty grub is a wild protein you can forage all year round in Arid centre. Large white moth larva in the roots of the witchetty bush, dug out by Aboriginal women in Central Australia.

Caution. Learn from Aboriginal guides; don't dig plants on others' country.

When is witchetty grub in season in Australia?

Witchetty grub is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does witchetty grub grow in Australia?

Witchetty grub grows in Northern Territory, Queensland, South Australia, Western Australia. Regions: Arid centre.

How do you recognise witchetty grub?

Large white moth larva in the roots of the witchetty bush, dug out by Aboriginal women in Central Australia.

How do you eat witchetty grub?

Raw (tastes of almond) or lightly cooked on coals.

How do you store witchetty grub?

Eat fresh.

What is witchetty grub good for?

Protein, Fat.

What can you cook with witchetty grub?

  • Witchetty grubs on coals: Grubs cooked briefly on hot coals until golden.
