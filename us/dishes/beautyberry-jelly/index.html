Beautyberry jelly

Beautyberry jelly is made with wild American beautyberry, a favourite in Southeast.

How do you make beautyberry jelly?

Beautyberries simmered and strained into a floral pink jelly.

Wild ingredients

  • American beautyberry (Callicarpa americana): in season August to October
