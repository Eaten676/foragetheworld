Blackcurrant cordial (solbærsaft)

Blackcurrant cordial (solbærsaft) is made with wild blackcurrant, a favourite in Eastern Norway.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make blackcurrant cordial (solbærsaft)?

Blackcurrants cooked with sugar and strained, diluted hot in winter.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild blackcurrant (Ribes nigrum): in season July to August
