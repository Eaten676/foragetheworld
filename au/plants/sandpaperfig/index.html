Sandpaper fig

Ficus coronata
Also called Sandpaper fig

Sandpaper fig is a wild fruit you can forage from December to March in Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east and Tropical north. Creekside tree with rough sandpaper leaves and small hairy purple figs.

When is sandpaper fig in season in Australia?

Sandpaper fig is usually in season December to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sandpaper fig grow in Australia?

Sandpaper fig grows across Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, Tropical north.

How do you recognise sandpaper fig?

Creekside tree with rough sandpaper leaves and small hairy purple figs.

How do you harvest sandpaper fig?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat sandpaper fig?

Peel and eat fresh, or jam.

How do you store sandpaper fig?

Jam.

What is sandpaper fig good for?

Vitamin C.
