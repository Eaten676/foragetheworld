Bunya nut

Araucaria bidwillii
Also called Bunya nut

Bunya nut is a wild nut you can forage from January to March in Subtropical east coast. Tall dome-shaped conifer with huge, heavy cones (up to 10 kg) that fall in summer.

Caution. Falling cones can kill: never stand under trees in cone season.

When is bunya nut in season in Australia?

Bunya nut is usually in season January to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bunya nut grow in Australia?

Bunya nut grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise bunya nut?

Tall dome-shaped conifer with huge, heavy cones (up to 10 kg) that fall in summer.

How do you harvest bunya nut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat bunya nut?

Boil or roast the nuts; taste like chestnut.

How do you store bunya nut?

Freeze boiled nuts.

What is bunya nut good for?

Calories, Protein.
