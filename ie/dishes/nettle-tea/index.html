Nettle tea

Nettle tea is made with wild stinging nettle.

Open in Forage IrelandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nettle tea?

Fresh nettle tops steeped in hot water.

Wild ingredients

  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season March to June
