Damson and bullace

Prunus domestica subsp. insititia
Also called Damson, Bullace

Damson and bullace is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October across the UK. Old hedgerow plums, round to oval and purple-black (damson) or yellow-green (bullace); bigger and sweeter than sloes.

When is damson and bullace in season in the UK?

Damson and bullace is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does damson and bullace grow in the UK?

Damson and bullace grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise damson and bullace?

Old hedgerow plums, round to oval and purple-black (damson) or yellow-green (bullace); bigger and sweeter than sloes.

How do you harvest damson and bullace?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat damson and bullace?

Jam, cheese, damson gin and crumbles.

How do you store damson and bullace?

Freeze, or make jam and gin.

What is damson and bullace good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with damson and bullace?

  • Damson cheese: Damson pulp boiled down into a firm fruit paste for cheese boards.
  • Damson gin: Damsons steeped in gin with sugar until Christmas.
