Gorse flowers

Ulex europaeus
Also called Gorse flowers

Gorse flowers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from July to November across New Zealand. Spiny evergreen shrub covered in coconut-scented yellow pea flowers, most of the year.

Caution. Invasive weed: pick the flowers freely, but beware the spines.

When is gorse flowers in season in New Zealand?

Gorse flowers is usually in season July to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does gorse flowers grow in New Zealand?

Gorse flowers grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise gorse flowers?

Spiny evergreen shrub covered in coconut-scented yellow pea flowers, most of the year.

How do you harvest gorse flowers?

Pick open flowers carefully with gloves; scent is strongest on sunny days.

How do you eat gorse flowers?

Gorse flower cordial, wine and syrup.

How do you store gorse flowers?

Cordial.

What can you cook with gorse flowers?

  • Gorse flower cordial: Coconut-scented cordial from gorse blossom.
