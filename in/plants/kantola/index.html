Spiny gourd

Momordica dioica
Also called Kantola, Kakroda, Madahagala, Kartoli

Spiny gourd is a wild green you can forage from July to September in South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests, Northern plains and Northeast. Wild climber with small soft-spiked green fruit, found on hedges in the monsoon.

When is spiny gourd in season in India?

Spiny gourd is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does spiny gourd grow in India?

Spiny gourd grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests, Northern plains, Northeast.

How do you recognise spiny gourd?

Wild climber with small soft-spiked green fruit, found on hedges in the monsoon.

How do you harvest spiny gourd?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat spiny gourd?

Stuffed, fried or as sabzi.

How do you store spiny gourd?

Slice and sun-dry, or pickle.

What is spiny gourd good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with spiny gourd?

  • Kartoli bhaji / kantola sabzi: Sliced spiny gourd stir-fried with onion and spices, or stuffed and fried.
