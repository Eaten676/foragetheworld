Capers

Capparis spinosa
Also called Kabir

Capers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from March to June in Hajar Mountains and wadis and Gulf coast. Sprawling, spiny shrub on rocks, cliffs and old walls in the Hajar Mountains and on some islands, with round fleshy leaves and big white flowers with long purple stamens.

Caution. Never eat them raw from the bush: brine or salt first to remove bitterness.

When is capers in season in the UAE?

Capers is usually in season March to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does capers grow in the UAE?

Capers grows across the UAE. Regions: Hajar Mountains and wadis, Gulf coast.

How do you recognise capers?

Sprawling, spiny shrub on rocks, cliffs and old walls in the Hajar Mountains and on some islands, with round fleshy leaves and big white flowers with long purple stamens.

How do you harvest capers?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat capers?

Pickle the unopened buds and young fruits in salt or brine.

How do you store capers?

Pickle.

What can you cook with capers?

  • Pickled capers: Caper buds salted, then kept in vinegar brine.
