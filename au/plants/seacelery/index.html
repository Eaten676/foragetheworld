Sea celery

Apium prostratum
Also called Sea celery

Sea celery is a wild green you can forage all year round in Tropical north, Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, South-west and Tasmania. Low coastal plant with glossy, celery-scented, divided leaves on rocks and dunes.

Caution. Hemlock is related: crush a leaf and check for a clean celery smell.

When is sea celery in season in Australia?

Sea celery is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea celery grow in Australia?

Sea celery grows across Australia. Regions: Tropical north, Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, South-west, Tasmania.

How do you recognise sea celery?

Low coastal plant with glossy, celery-scented, divided leaves on rocks and dunes.

How do you harvest sea celery?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat sea celery?

A herb for soups, salads and fish.

How do you store sea celery?

Dry.

What is sea celery good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with sea celery?

  • Sea celery salsa verde: Chopped sea celery with capers, lemon and oil.
