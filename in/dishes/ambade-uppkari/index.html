Ambade uppkari

Ambade uppkari is made with wild hog plum, a favourite in South India and West coast and Deccan.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make ambade uppkari?

Hog plums cooked with jaggery, chilli and mustard. A sweet-sour Konkani side.

Wild ingredients

  • Hog plum (Spondias pinnata): in season August to November
