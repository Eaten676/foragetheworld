How do you make macadamia and wattleseed ice cream?

Coffee-like wattleseed custard with toasted macadamias.

Wild ingredients

  • Macadamia (Macadamia integrifolia): in season March to August
  • Wattleseed (Acacia victoriae): in season November to January
