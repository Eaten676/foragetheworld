Water spinach

Ipomoea aquatica
Also called Kalmi saag, Nali bhaji, Vallal keerai

Water spinach is a wild green you can forage from June to November in East, Northeast, South India, Northern plains and Central forests. Hollow-stemmed creeper with arrow-shaped leaves, floating in ponds and ditches.

Caution. It thrives in dirty water and absorbs pollutants. Only pick from clean ponds, and cook it.

When is water spinach in season in India?

Water spinach is usually in season June to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does water spinach grow in India?

Water spinach grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Goa, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: East, Northeast, South India, Northern plains, Central forests.

How do you recognise water spinach?

Hollow-stemmed creeper with arrow-shaped leaves, floating in ponds and ditches.

How do you harvest water spinach?

Snip the tips above the water line from clean ponds only.

How do you eat water spinach?

Stir-fried with garlic, or kalmi saag.

How do you store water spinach?

Use fresh.

What is water spinach good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with water spinach?

  • Kalmi saag: Water spinach stir-fried with nigella, garlic and dry chilli.
