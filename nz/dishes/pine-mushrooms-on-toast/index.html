Pine mushrooms on toast

Pine mushrooms on toast is made with wild saffron milk cap, a favourite in Canterbury and Otago and Southland.

Open in Forage New ZealandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make pine mushrooms on toast?

Saffron milk caps fried in butter on toast.

Wild ingredients

  • Saffron milk cap (Lactarius deliciosus / L. deterrimus): in season March to May
