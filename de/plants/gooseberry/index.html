Wild gooseberry

Ribes uva-crispa
Also called Stachelbeere

Wild gooseberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July across Germany. Spiny shrub with small lobed leaves; hairy green to red berries.

When is wild gooseberry in season in Germany?

Wild gooseberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild gooseberry grow in Germany?

Wild gooseberry grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise wild gooseberry?

Spiny shrub with small lobed leaves; hairy green to red berries.

How do you harvest wild gooseberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat wild gooseberry?

Fool, crumble, jam.

How do you store wild gooseberry?

Freeze.

What is wild gooseberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild gooseberry?

  • Gooseberry meringue cake (Stachelbeerbaiser): Gooseberries under a crisp meringue on shortcrust.
