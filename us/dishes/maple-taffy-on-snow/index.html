Maple taffy on snow

Maple taffy on snow is made with wild maple sap, a favourite in Northeast.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make maple taffy on snow?

Maple syrup boiled to soft-ball stage and poured on clean snow.

Wild ingredients

  • Maple sap (Acer saccharum): in season February to March
