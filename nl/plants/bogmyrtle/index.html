Bog myrtle

Myrica gale
Also called Wilde gagel

Bog myrtle is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to September in East and South. Low shrub of wet bogs with grey-green, resin-scented leaves.

Caution. Avoid in pregnancy; small amounts.

When is bog myrtle in season in the Netherlands?

Bog myrtle is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bog myrtle grow in the Netherlands?

Bog myrtle grows in Drenthe, Flevoland, Friesland, Gelderland, Groningen, Limburg, North Brabant, Overijssel, Zeeland. Regions: East, South.

How do you recognise bog myrtle?

Low shrub of wet bogs with grey-green, resin-scented leaves.

How do you harvest bog myrtle?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat bog myrtle?

Old beer flavouring (gale); leaves as a herb.

How do you store bog myrtle?

Dry.
