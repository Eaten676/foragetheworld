Lime blossom

Tilia cordata
Also called Linde

Lime blossom is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to July across the Netherlands. Big tree with heart-shaped leaves and dangling clusters of pale yellow, honey-scented flowers under a leafy wing.

When is lime blossom in season in the Netherlands?

Lime blossom is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lime blossom grow in the Netherlands?

Lime blossom grows across the Netherlands. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden, West, East, South.

How do you recognise lime blossom?

Big tree with heart-shaped leaves and dangling clusters of pale yellow, honey-scented flowers under a leafy wing.

How do you harvest lime blossom?

Pick flower clusters with their leafy bract when the scent is strongest, early in flowering.

How do you eat lime blossom?

Calming tea; young leaves in salad.

How do you store lime blossom?

Dry with the bract.
