Lemon myrtle iced tea

Lemon myrtle iced tea is made with wild lemon myrtle, a favourite in Subtropical east coast.

How do you make lemon myrtle iced tea?

Lemon myrtle leaves steeped, chilled, with honey.

Wild ingredients

  • Lemon myrtle (Backhousia citriodora): in season all year round
