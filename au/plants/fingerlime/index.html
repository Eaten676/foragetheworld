Finger lime

Citrus australasica
Also called Finger lime, Caviar lime

Finger lime is a wild fruit you can forage from December to May in Subtropical east coast. Thorny rainforest shrub with tiny leaves; finger-shaped fruit full of caviar-like citrus beads.

When is finger lime in season in Australia?

Finger lime is usually in season December to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does finger lime grow in Australia?

Finger lime grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise finger lime?

Thorny rainforest shrub with tiny leaves; finger-shaped fruit full of caviar-like citrus beads.

How do you harvest finger lime?

Pick fruit when it comes away easily and the skin feels slightly soft.

How do you eat finger lime?

Beads on oysters, fish, desserts and drinks.

How do you store finger lime?

Freeze whole fruit.

What is finger lime good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with finger lime?

  • Oysters with finger lime: Fresh oysters topped with finger lime pearls.
  • Finger lime pavlova: Pavlova topped with cream and finger lime caviar.
