Cornelian cherry

Cornus mas
Also called Kornelkirsche, Tierlibaum

Cornelian cherry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to September in South and the Alps and West. Shrub flowering yellow in late winter; glossy red olive-shaped fruit, sour until very ripe.

When is cornelian cherry in season in Germany?

Cornelian cherry is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cornelian cherry grow in Germany?

Cornelian cherry grows in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Hesse, North Rhine-Westphalia, Rhineland-Palatinate, Saarland. Regions: South and the Alps, West.

How do you recognise cornelian cherry?

Shrub flowering yellow in late winter; glossy red olive-shaped fruit, sour until very ripe.

How do you harvest cornelian cherry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat cornelian cherry?

Jam, syrup, liqueur.

How do you store cornelian cherry?

Jam.

What is cornelian cherry good for?

Vitamin C.
