Oysters

Ostrea edulis / Magallana gigas
Also called Oysters, Tio

Oysters is a wild protein you can forage from March to August in Northland and Auckland and Otago and Southland. Rock and Pacific oysters on northern harbour rocks; Bluff oysters are dredged in the far south.

Caution. Follow MPI daily limits and toxic shellfish warnings.

When is oysters in season in New Zealand?

Oysters is usually in season March to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does oysters grow in New Zealand?

Oysters grows in Auckland, Northland, Otago, Southland. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise oysters?

Rock and Pacific oysters on northern harbour rocks; Bluff oysters are dredged in the far south.

How do you harvest oysters?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat oysters?

Raw with lemon, or grilled.

How do you store oysters?

Eat fresh.

What is oysters good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with oysters?

  • Oysters with lemon: Freshly shucked oysters with lemon.
