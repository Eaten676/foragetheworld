Wild custard apple

Annona squamosa
Also called Sitaphal, Seethaphala, Seetha pazham

Wild custard apple is a wild fruit you can forage from August to November in South India, Central forests, West coast and Deccan and East. Gone wild on rocky hills across the Deccan. Knobbly green fruit with white custardy pulp.

Caution. Never eat the seeds. They are toxic.

When is wild custard apple in season in India?

Wild custard apple is usually in season August to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild custard apple grow in India?

Wild custard apple grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: South India, Central forests, West coast and Deccan, East.

How do you recognise wild custard apple?

Gone wild on rocky hills across the Deccan. Knobbly green fruit with white custardy pulp.

How do you harvest wild custard apple?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat wild custard apple?

Ripe pulp fresh.

How do you store wild custard apple?

Freeze the pulp.

What is wild custard apple good for?

Calories, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild custard apple?

  • Sitaphal basundi: Custard apple pulp folded into reduced, sweetened milk.
