Lingonberry

Vaccinium vitis-idaea
Also called Tyttebær

Lingonberry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October across Norway. Low evergreen shrub with glossy leathery leaves and clusters of tart red berries in pine and spruce forest.

When is lingonberry in season in Norway?

Lingonberry is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lingonberry grow in Norway?

Lingonberry grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise lingonberry?

Low evergreen shrub with glossy leathery leaves and clusters of tart red berries in pine and spruce forest.

How do you harvest lingonberry?

Pick ripe red clusters from late August; berries keep well because they are naturally preserved.

How do you eat lingonberry?

Lingonberry jam with meatballs, game and porridge.

How do you store lingonberry?

Stir raw with sugar; keeps for months.

What is lingonberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with lingonberry?

  • Lingonberry jam (tyttebærsyltetøy): Lingonberries stirred raw with sugar, served with meatballs, game and potato.
  • Juniper-cured game sauce: Crushed juniper and lingonberry in a cream sauce for elk or reindeer.
  • Chanterelle sauce for reindeer: Chanterelles in cream with juniper, served with reindeer and lingonberries.
