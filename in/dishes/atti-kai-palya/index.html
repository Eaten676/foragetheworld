Atti kai palya

Atti kai palya is made with wild cluster fig, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make atti kai palya?

Unripe cluster figs boiled and cooked dry with coconut.

Wild ingredients

  • Cluster fig (Ficus racemosa): in season March to August
