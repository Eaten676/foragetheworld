Kiekie fruit

Freycinetia banksii
Also called Kiekie fruit

Kiekie fruit is a wild fruit you can forage from February to April in Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South and West Coast. Climbing plant with long strap leaves; sweet fleshy bracts (tāwhara) in spring and fruit (pirori) in autumn.

When is kiekie fruit in season in New Zealand?

Kiekie fruit is usually in season February to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kiekie fruit grow in New Zealand?

Kiekie fruit grows in Auckland, Bay of Plenty, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Manawatū-Whanganui, Marlborough, Nelson, Northland, Taranaki, Tasman, Waikato, Wellington, West Coast. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast.

How do you recognise kiekie fruit?

Climbing plant with long strap leaves; sweet fleshy bracts (tāwhara) in spring and fruit (pirori) in autumn.

How do you harvest kiekie fruit?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat kiekie fruit?

Eaten fresh, a prized traditional sweet.

How do you store kiekie fruit?

Best fresh.
