Crabapple

Malus
Also called Crabapple, Wild apple

Crabapple is a wild fruit you can forage from September to November across the United States. Small apple trees in hedges and old farms; sour little apples.

When is crabapple in season in the United States?

Crabapple is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does crabapple grow in the United States?

Crabapple grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise crabapple?

Small apple trees in hedges and old farms; sour little apples.

How do you harvest crabapple?

Collect fallen or easily picked apples in autumn; slightly underripe ones have the most pectin.

How do you eat crabapple?

Jelly, cider, crab apple butter.

How do you store crabapple?

Jelly; add to low-pectin jams.

What is crabapple good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with crabapple?

  • Crabapple jelly: Crab apples simmered, strained and set into a pink jelly.
