Kandali saag

Kandali saag is made with wild stinging nettle, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kandali saag?

Blanched nettles cooked with garlic, chilli and a little rice flour. A Kumaoni winter dish.

Wild ingredients

  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season February to November
