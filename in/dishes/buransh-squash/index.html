Buransh squash

Buransh squash is made with wild buransh, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make buransh squash?

Simmer red buransh petals in water, strain, then cook with sugar and lemon into a squash. Dilute to drink.

Wild ingredients

  • Buransh (Rhododendron arboreum): in season February to April
