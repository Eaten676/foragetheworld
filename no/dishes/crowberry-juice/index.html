Crowberry juice (kreklingsaft)

Crowberry juice (kreklingsaft) is made with wild crowberry, a favourite in Northern Norway.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make crowberry juice (kreklingsaft)?

Steam-juiced crowberries, often mixed with bilberry.

Wild ingredients

  • Crowberry (Empetrum nigrum): in season August to September
