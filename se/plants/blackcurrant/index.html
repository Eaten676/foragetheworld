Wild blackcurrant

Ribes nigrum
Also called Svarta vinbär

Wild blackcurrant is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across Sweden. Shrub of damp woods with lobed leaves that smell strongly of blackcurrant; black berries in hanging strings.

When is wild blackcurrant in season in Sweden?

Wild blackcurrant is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild blackcurrant grow in Sweden?

Wild blackcurrant grows across Sweden. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle), Norrland (north).

How do you recognise wild blackcurrant?

Shrub of damp woods with lobed leaves that smell strongly of blackcurrant; black berries in hanging strings.

How do you harvest wild blackcurrant?

Strip ripe strings of berries in midsummer.

How do you eat wild blackcurrant?

Cordial, jam.

How do you store wild blackcurrant?

Freeze or cordial.

What is wild blackcurrant good for?

Vitamin C.
