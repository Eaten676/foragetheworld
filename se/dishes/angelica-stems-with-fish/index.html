Angelica stems with fish (kvanne)

Angelica stems with fish (kvanne) is made with wild garden angelica, a favourite in Norrland (north).

How do you make angelica stems with fish (kvanne)?

Young kvanne stems cooked with char, a Sámi tradition.

Wild ingredients

  • Garden angelica (Angelica archangelica): in season June to July
