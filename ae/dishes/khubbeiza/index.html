Khubbeiza (mallow stew)

Khubbeiza (mallow stew) is made with wild mallow.

Open in Forage United Arab EmiratesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make khubbeiza (mallow stew)?

Mallow leaves sautéed with onion, garlic, olive oil and lemon.

Wild ingredients

  • Mallow (Malva parviflora): in season November to April
