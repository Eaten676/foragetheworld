Neem flowers

Azadirachta indica
Also called Neem, Bevu hoovu, Veppam poo

Neem flowers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from March to April in South India, Northern plains, Central forests, West coast and Deccan, East and Thar and dry northwest. Tiny, fragrant white flowers on the neem tree around Ugadi. Bitter.

Caution. Small amounts only. Neem oil and seeds are not food.

When is neem flowers in season in India?

Neem flowers is usually in season March to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does neem flowers grow in India?

Neem flowers grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, Northern plains, Central forests, West coast and Deccan, East, Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise neem flowers?

Tiny, fragrant white flowers on the neem tree around Ugadi. Bitter.

How do you harvest neem flowers?

Spread a cloth under the tree and shake the branches gently.

How do you eat neem flowers?

Ugadi pachadi, veppam poo rasam, fried with rice.

How do you store neem flowers?

Shade-dry. Keeps a year.

What can you cook with neem flowers?

  • Ugadi pachadi: Neem flowers, raw mango, tamarind, jaggery, salt and chilli: six tastes in one spoonful for the new year.
  • Veppam poo rasam: Fried neem flowers in a tamarind rasam. Bitter, sour and soothing.
