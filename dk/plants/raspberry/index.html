Wild raspberry

Rubus idaeus
Also called Hindbær

Wild raspberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across Denmark. Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

When is wild raspberry in season in Denmark?

Wild raspberry is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild raspberry grow in Denmark?

Wild raspberry grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise wild raspberry?

Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

How do you harvest wild raspberry?

Pick berries that slip off their core with no tug, into a shallow container.

How do you eat wild raspberry?

Fresh, jam, cordial.

How do you store wild raspberry?

Freeze.

What is wild raspberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild raspberry?

  • Raspberry and gooseberry trifle: Summer berries layered with cream and macaroons.
