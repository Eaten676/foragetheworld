Pineapple weed tea

Pineapple weed tea is made with wild pineapple weed.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make pineapple weed tea?

Fresh or dried flower heads steeped like chamomile, with honey.

Wild ingredients

  • Pineapple weed (Matricaria discoidea): in season June to September
