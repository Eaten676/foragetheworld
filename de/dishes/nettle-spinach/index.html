Nettle spinach (Brennnesselspinat)

Nettle spinach (Brennnesselspinat) is made with wild stinging nettle.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nettle spinach (brennnesselspinat)?

Blanched nettles creamed with onion and nutmeg, with fried eggs and potatoes.

Wild ingredients

  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season March to June
