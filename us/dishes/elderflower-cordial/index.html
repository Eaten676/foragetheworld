Elderflower cordial

Elderflower cordial is made with wild elderflower, a favourite in Northeast and Pacific Northwest.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower cordial?

Steep flower heads overnight with lemon, strain and dissolve in sugar syrup. Dilute with sparkling water.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus canadensis): in season May to July
