Indian pennywort

Centella asiatica
Also called Brahmi, Ondelaga, Vallarai, Thankuni

Indian pennywort is a wild green you can forage from June to October in South India, East and Northeast. Kidney-shaped leaves on creeping runners in damp, shady ground. Used more as a tonic than as food.

When is Indian pennywort in season in India?

Indian pennywort is usually in season June to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does Indian pennywort grow in India?

Indian pennywort grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: South India, East, Northeast.

How do you recognise Indian pennywort?

Kidney-shaped leaves on creeping runners in damp, shady ground. Used more as a tonic than as food.

How do you harvest Indian pennywort?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat Indian pennywort?

Chutney, tambuli, thankuni bata with rice.

How do you store Indian pennywort?

Shade-dry and powder.

What can you cook with Indian pennywort?

  • Ondelaga juice: Blend a handful of pennywort leaves with water, jaggery and lime, then strain. Or grind them into buttermilk for tambuli.
  • Thankuni bata / ondelaga chutney: Pennywort ground with coconut, green chilli and a little tamarind.
