Chickweed

Stellaria media
Also called Vogelmiere

Chickweed is a wild green you can forage from March to November across Germany. Soft sprawling mat with tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

When is chickweed in season in Germany?

Chickweed is usually in season March to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chickweed grow in Germany?

Chickweed grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise chickweed?

Soft sprawling mat with tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

How do you harvest chickweed?

Snip the soft top few centimetres with scissors, leaving the roots so it regrows within weeks.

How do you eat chickweed?

Raw in salads and sandwiches.

How do you store chickweed?

Best fresh.

What is chickweed good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with chickweed?

  • Green sauce (Grüne Soße) with wild herbs: Frankfurt-style herb sauce of seven herbs with eggs and potatoes.
