Chestnut gnocchi (Gnocchi di castagne)

Chestnut gnocchi (Gnocchi di castagne) is made with wild sweet chestnut, a favourite in Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you make chestnut gnocchi (gnocchi di castagne)?

Ticino gnocchi of chestnut flour and potato with sage butter.

Wild ingredients

  • Sweet chestnut (Castanea sativa): in season October to November
