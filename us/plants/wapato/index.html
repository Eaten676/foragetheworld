Wapato

Sagittaria latifolia
Also called Wapato, Duck potato

Wapato is a root or shoot you can forage from September to December across the United States. Arrowhead-shaped leaves in marsh shallows; tubers at the end of runners in the mud.

Caution. Only from clean water; always cook.

When is wapato in season in the United States?

Wapato is usually in season September to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wapato grow in the United States?

Wapato grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise wapato?

Arrowhead-shaped leaves in marsh shallows; tubers at the end of runners in the mud.

How do you harvest wapato?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat wapato?

Roast or boil like potatoes.

How do you store wapato?

Store cool.
