Huli soppu chutney

Huli soppu chutney is made with wild wood sorrel, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make huli soppu chutney?

Wood sorrel ground with coconut, chilli and a little jaggery. Small portions only.

Wild ingredients

  • Wood sorrel (Oxalis corniculata): in season June to February
