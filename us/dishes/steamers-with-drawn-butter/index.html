Steamers with drawn butter

Steamers with drawn butter is made with wild clams, a favourite in Northeast.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make steamers with drawn butter?

Soft-shell clams steamed open and dipped in broth then melted butter.

Wild ingredients

  • Clams (Mya arenaria / Leukoma staminea): in season September to May
