Chestnut

Castanea spp.
Also called Chestnut, Châtaignier

Chestnut is a wild nut you can forage from October in British Columbia and Ontario. Large tree with long, saw-toothed leaves; glossy brown nuts inside very spiny green cases.

Caution. Horse chestnuts (conkers) are poisonous: they have one round nut in a case with few, blunt spikes.

When is chestnut in season in Canada?

Chestnut is usually in season October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chestnut grow in Canada?

Chestnut grows in British Columbia, Ontario. Regions: British Columbia, Ontario.

How do you recognise chestnut?

Large tree with long, saw-toothed leaves; glossy brown nuts inside very spiny green cases.

How do you harvest chestnut?

Gather fallen nuts with gloves or by rolling the spiny cases open with your boots; choose plump, shiny nuts.

How do you eat chestnut?

Roasted, chestnut purée, flour, stuffing.

How do you store chestnut?

Freeze peeled, or dry and grind.
