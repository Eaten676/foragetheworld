Juniper-cured game sauce

Juniper-cured game sauce is made with wild juniper berries, lingonberry.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make juniper-cured game sauce?

Crushed juniper and lingonberry in a cream sauce for elk or reindeer.

Wild ingredients

  • Juniper berries (Juniperus communis): in season September to November
  • Lingonberry (Vaccinium vitis-idaea): in season August to October
