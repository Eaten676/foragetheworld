Cranachan with raspberries

Cranachan with raspberries is made with wild raspberry, a favourite in Scotland.

How do you make cranachan with raspberries?

Toasted oats, whisky, honey, cream and wild raspberries.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild raspberry (Rubus idaeus): in season July to August
