Samphire

Tecticornia
Also called Samphire, Glasswort

Samphire is a wild green you can forage from September to March in Tropical north, Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, South-west and Tasmania. Jointed, succulent, leafless stems on salt marshes and saline flats.

When is samphire in season in Australia?

Samphire is usually in season September to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does samphire grow in Australia?

Samphire grows across Australia. Regions: Tropical north, Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, South-west, Tasmania.

How do you recognise samphire?

Jointed, succulent, leafless stems on salt marshes and saline flats.

How do you harvest samphire?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat samphire?

Steamed with fish or pickled.

How do you store samphire?

Pickle.

What is samphire good for?

Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with samphire?

  • Pickled samphire: Samphire tips in spiced vinegar.
