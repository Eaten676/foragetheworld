Karonda pickle

Karonda pickle is made with wild karonda, a favourite in West coast and Deccan, Central forests and Northern plains.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make karonda pickle?

Halved karondas salted, then tossed in mustard oil with fennel and nigella.

Wild ingredients

  • Karonda (Carissa carandas): in season July to September
