Hummaid salad

Hummaid salad is made with wild bladder dock, a favourite in Hajar Mountains and wadis.

Open in Forage United Arab EmiratesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make hummaid salad?

Sour bladder dock leaves with tomato, onion and lemon.

Wild ingredients

  • Bladder dock (Rumex vesicarius): in season December to March
