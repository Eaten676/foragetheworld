Juneberry

Amelanchier lamarckii
Also called Kupfer-Felsenbirne

Juneberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July in North German lowlands and coast. Naturalised shrub of heaths and woodland edges with white star flowers in April and purple-black berries in June.

When is juneberry in season in Germany?

Juneberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does juneberry grow in Germany?

Juneberry grows in Bremen, Hamburg, Lower Saxony, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Schleswig-Holstein. Regions: North German lowlands and coast.

How do you recognise juneberry?

Naturalised shrub of heaths and woodland edges with white star flowers in April and purple-black berries in June.

How do you harvest juneberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat juneberry?

Raw, in muffins, jam and crumbles; tastes of almond and blueberry.

How do you store juneberry?

Freeze or dry.

What is juneberry good for?

Vitamin C.
