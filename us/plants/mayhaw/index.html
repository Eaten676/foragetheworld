Mayhaw

Crataegus aestivalis
Also called Mayhaw

Mayhaw is a wild fruit you can forage from May in Southeast. Hawthorn of southern swamps with small red fruit that drops into the water in May.

Caution. Don't eat the seeds.

When is mayhaw in season in the United States?

Mayhaw is usually in season May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mayhaw grow in the United States?

Mayhaw grows in Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia. Regions: Southeast.

How do you recognise mayhaw?

Hawthorn of southern swamps with small red fruit that drops into the water in May.

How do you harvest mayhaw?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat mayhaw?

Mayhaw jelly, a Gulf South tradition.

How do you store mayhaw?

Jelly.

What is mayhaw good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with mayhaw?

  • Mayhaw jelly: Louisiana and Georgia's spring jelly.
