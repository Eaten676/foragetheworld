Moringa

Moringa oleifera
Also called Sahjan, Nugge, Murungai, Shevga

Moringa is a wild green you can forage all year round in South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Thar and dry northwest and Northeast. Feathery tree with tiny paired leaflets; long drumstick pods in spring.

When is moringa in season in India?

Moringa is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does moringa grow in India?

Moringa grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Thar and dry northwest, Northeast.

How do you recognise moringa?

Feathery tree with tiny paired leaflets; long drumstick pods in spring.

How do you harvest moringa?

Snap off whole leaf sprigs from the branch tips. Pruning like this makes the tree bushier. Pick pods while they still snap cleanly.

How do you eat moringa?

Leaves in dal, palya or thoran. Pods in sambar. Flowers fried with besan.

How do you store moringa?

Shade-dry leaves until crisp, then grind to powder. Keeps 4–6 months airtight.

What is moringa good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium, Vitamin A, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with moringa?

  • Moringa tea: Steep a spoon of dried moringa leaves or powder in hot water for 5 minutes, and add lemon and honey.
  • Drumstick leaf pakoda: Moringa leaves stirred into besan with onion and chilli, fried in small fritters.
  • Shevgyachya shenganchi amti: Maharashtrian drumstick amti: toor dal with goda masala, kokum or tamarind and a touch of jaggery.
  • Nugge soppu palya: Moringa leaves stir-fried with mustard, urad dal, onion and grated coconut.
  • Drumstick sambar: Toor dal and tamarind sambar with drumstick pods simmered until tender.
  • Moringa thepla: Soft flatbreads of wheat and besan with moringa leaves and spices.
