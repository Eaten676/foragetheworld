Water chestnut

Trapa natans
Also called Singhara, Paniphal

Water chestnut is a sap or starch you can forage from October to December in Northern plains, East and Central forests. Floating pond plant with horned dark fruit.

Caution. Only from clean ponds. Always cook.

When is water chestnut in season in India?

Water chestnut is usually in season October to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does water chestnut grow in India?

Water chestnut grows in Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Haryana, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Northern plains, East, Central forests.

How do you recognise water chestnut?

Floating pond plant with horned dark fruit.

How do you harvest water chestnut?

Picked from floating plants by boat. Wear gloves, since the horns are sharp.

How do you eat water chestnut?

Boiled or roasted. Flour for fasting rotis.

How do you store water chestnut?

Dry the kernels and grind into flour.

What is water chestnut good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with water chestnut?

  • Boiled singhara: Fresh water chestnuts boiled in their shells, peeled and sprinkled with salt.
  • Singhara atta puri: Water chestnut flour puris, eaten on fasting days.
