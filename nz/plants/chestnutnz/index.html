Sweet chestnut

Castanea sativa
Also called Sweet chestnut

Sweet chestnut is a wild nut you can forage from March to May in Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Top of the South and Canterbury. Introduced trees with long saw-toothed leaves and very spiny cases.

Caution. Horse chestnuts are poisonous: few, blunt spikes and one round nut.

When is sweet chestnut in season in New Zealand?

Sweet chestnut is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sweet chestnut grow in New Zealand?

Sweet chestnut grows in Bay of Plenty, Canterbury, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Manawatū-Whanganui, Marlborough, Nelson, Taranaki, Tasman, Waikato. Regions: Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Top of the South, Canterbury.

How do you recognise sweet chestnut?

Introduced trees with long saw-toothed leaves and very spiny cases.

How do you harvest sweet chestnut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat sweet chestnut?

Roasted, in stuffing.

How do you store sweet chestnut?

Freeze peeled.

What is sweet chestnut good for?

Calories.
