Wild drumstick tree

Moringa peregrina
Also called Yasar, Shu'

Wild drumstick tree is a wild green you can forage from February to May in Hajar Mountains and wadis. Slender, smooth grey-barked tree of mountain wadis; its feathery leaves fall quickly, leaving bare twigs, white-pink flowers and long drumstick-shaped pods.

Caution. Uncommon in the wild: only pick from planted trees.

When is wild drumstick tree in season in the UAE?

Wild drumstick tree is usually in season February to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild drumstick tree grow in the UAE?

Wild drumstick tree grows in Al Ain, Fujairah, Hatta, Khor Fakkan and Kalba, Ras Al Khaimah. Regions: Hajar Mountains and wadis.

How do you recognise wild drumstick tree?

Slender, smooth grey-barked tree of mountain wadis; its feathery leaves fall quickly, leaving bare twigs, white-pink flowers and long drumstick-shaped pods.

How do you harvest wild drumstick tree?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.

How do you eat wild drumstick tree?

Young leaves and tender pods cooked; seeds roasted, or pressed for ben oil.

How do you store wild drumstick tree?

Dry leaves.

What is wild drumstick tree good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Protein.

What can you cook with wild drumstick tree?

  • Moringa leaf tea: Dried drumstick leaves steeped in hot water.
