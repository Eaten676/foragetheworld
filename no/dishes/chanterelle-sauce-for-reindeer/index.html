Chanterelle sauce for reindeer

Chanterelle sauce for reindeer is made with wild chanterelle, lingonberry, a favourite in Trøndelag and Northern Norway.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make chanterelle sauce for reindeer?

Chanterelles in cream with juniper, served with reindeer and lingonberries.

Wild ingredients

  • Chanterelle (Cantharellus cibarius): in season July to September
  • Lingonberry (Vaccinium vitis-idaea): in season August to October
