Elderberry and plum compote

Elderberry and plum compote is made with wild elderberry, cherry plum, a favourite in North German lowlands and coast.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderberry and plum compote?

Fliederbeeren and plums stewed with cinnamon.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderberry (Sambucus nigra): in season August to September
  • Cherry plum (Prunus cerasifera): in season July to August
