Wild pomegranate

Punica granatum
Also called Daru, Darim, Daruni

Wild pomegranate is a wild fruit you can forage from September to October in Himalaya. Hardy shrub of dry Himalayan slopes. Small, very sour fruit.

When is wild pomegranate in season in India?

Wild pomegranate is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild pomegranate grow in India?

Wild pomegranate grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise wild pomegranate?

Hardy shrub of dry Himalayan slopes. Small, very sour fruit.

How do you harvest wild pomegranate?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat wild pomegranate?

Seeds fresh, chutney.

How do you store wild pomegranate?

Sun-dry the seeds into anardana. Keeps a year or more.

What is wild pomegranate good for?

Vitamin C.
