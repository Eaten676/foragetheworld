Elderflower syrup (Holunderblütensirup)

Elderflower syrup (Holunderblütensirup) is made with wild elderflower.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower syrup (holunderblütensirup)?

Flowers steeped with lemon, strained and boiled with sugar. Mixed with sparkling water as Hugo or Holunderschorle.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
