Sea beet

Beta vulgaris subsp. maritima
Also called Strandbete

Sea beet is a wild green you can forage from May to September in Southern Norway and Western Norway. Glossy, leathery, dark green leaves on shingle beaches, sea walls and salt marsh edges. Wild ancestor of chard.

When is sea beet in season in Norway?

Sea beet is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea beet grow in Norway?

Sea beet grows in Agder, Møre og Romsdal, Rogaland, Telemark, Vestfold, Vestland. Regions: Southern Norway, Western Norway.

How do you recognise sea beet?

Glossy, leathery, dark green leaves on shingle beaches, sea walls and salt marsh edges. Wild ancestor of chard.

How do you harvest sea beet?

Pick the younger leaves from the centre; leave the plant and its root in place.

How do you eat sea beet?

Wilt like spinach with butter.

How do you store sea beet?

Blanch and freeze.

What is sea beet good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium, Vitamin C.
