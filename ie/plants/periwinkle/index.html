Periwinkles

Littorina littorea
Also called Periwinkles, Winkles, Faocha

Periwinkles is a wild protein you can forage from January to December across Ireland. Small dark spiral sea snails on rocks and seaweed at low tide.

Caution. Only from clean shores. Leave small ones to grow.

When is periwinkles in season in Ireland?

Periwinkles is usually in season January to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does periwinkles grow in Ireland?

Periwinkles grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise periwinkles?

Small dark spiral sea snails on rocks and seaweed at low tide.

How do you harvest periwinkles?

Pick winkles from rocks at low tide, leaving the small ones.

How do you eat periwinkles?

Boiled in salted water and picked out with a pin.

How do you store periwinkles?

Eat the same day.

What is periwinkles good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with periwinkles?

  • Periwinkles with a pin: Winkles boiled in seawater and picked out with a pin, the old seaside treat.
