Beechnut

Fagus sylvatica
Also called Buchecker

Beechnut is a wild nut you can forage from September to October across Germany. Smooth grey-barked tree; small triangular nuts in spiny husks, plentiful in mast years.

Caution. Eat roasted and in moderation.

When is beechnut in season in Germany?

Beechnut is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does beechnut grow in Germany?

Beechnut grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise beechnut?

Smooth grey-barked tree; small triangular nuts in spiny husks, plentiful in mast years.

How do you harvest beechnut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat beechnut?

Roasted as a snack, or pressed for oil.

How do you store beechnut?

Dry.

What is beechnut good for?

Fat, Calories.
