Fiddleheads with butter

Fiddleheads with butter is made with wild ostrich fern fiddleheads, a favourite in Atlantic Canada and Quebec.

How do you make fiddleheads with butter?

Boiled fiddleheads sautéed in butter, the New Brunswick spring dish.

Wild ingredients

  • Ostrich fern fiddleheads (Matteuccia struthiopteris): in season May to June
