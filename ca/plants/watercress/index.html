Watercress

Nasturtium officinale
Also called Watercress, Cresson

Watercress is a wild green you can forage from April to November in Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia and Atlantic Canada. Floating mats of glossy, rounded leaflets in clear, cold, flowing streams. Peppery.

Caution. Only from clean water, and cook it: wild cress can carry liver fluke from livestock.

When is watercress in season in Canada?

Watercress is usually in season April to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does watercress grow in Canada?

Watercress grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise watercress?

Floating mats of glossy, rounded leaflets in clear, cold, flowing streams. Peppery.

How do you harvest watercress?

Cut stems above the water line with scissors, only from clean, flowing water away from livestock.

How do you eat watercress?

Soup, or cooked greens.

How do you store watercress?

Best fresh.

What is watercress good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.
