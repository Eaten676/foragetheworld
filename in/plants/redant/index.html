Red ant chutney

Oecophylla smaragdina
Also called Kai pimpudi, Kai chutney, Chaprah

Red ant chutney is a wild protein you can forage all year round in Central forests and East. Weaver ants and their eggs, collected year-round from leaf nests. Similipal kai chutney has a GI tag.

Caution. The ants bite hard. Learn from tribal collectors, and leave most of each nest.

When is red ant chutney in season in India?

Red ant chutney is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does red ant chutney grow in India?

Red ant chutney grows in Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: Central forests, East.

How do you recognise red ant chutney?

Weaver ants and their eggs, collected year-round from leaf nests. Similipal kai chutney has a GI tag.

How do you harvest red ant chutney?

Learn from tribal collectors, who knock part of a leaf nest into a basket and leave the rest.

How do you eat red ant chutney?

Ground with chilli, garlic and salt into chutney.

How do you store red ant chutney?

Dry the chutney paste in the sun.

What is red ant chutney good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with red ant chutney?

  • Kai chutney: Red ants and eggs ground with chilli, garlic, ginger and salt. Similipal's GI-tagged chutney.
