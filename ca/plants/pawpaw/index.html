Pawpaw

Asimina triloba
Also called Pawpaw, Asiminier

Pawpaw is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in Ontario. Understory tree with big drooping leaves; green fruit that taste of banana and mango when soft and fragrant.

Caution. Don't eat the seeds or skin.

When is pawpaw in season in Canada?

Pawpaw is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does pawpaw grow in Canada?

Pawpaw grows in Ontario. Regions: Ontario.

How do you recognise pawpaw?

Understory tree with big drooping leaves; green fruit that taste of banana and mango when soft and fragrant.

How do you harvest pawpaw?

Shake the trunk gently: ripe pawpaws drop, or pick fruit that is soft and fragrant.

How do you eat pawpaw?

Fresh, ice cream, bread.

How do you store pawpaw?

Freeze the pulp.

What is pawpaw good for?

Calories, Vitamin C, Protein.
