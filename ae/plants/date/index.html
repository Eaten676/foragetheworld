Dates

Phoenix dactylifera
Also called Nakhla, Rutab (soft), Tamr (dried)

Dates is a wild fruit you can forage from June to September in Cities, farms and oases, Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Hajar Mountains and wadis and East coast. Tall palm of farms, oases and streets; heavy bunches turn from green to yellow or red, then soften into rutab and dry into tamr.

Caution. Almost every palm belongs to someone: a farm, a family or the municipality. Ask before picking, and skip roadside palms that may be sprayed.

When is dates in season in the UAE?

Dates is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does dates grow in the UAE?

Dates grows across the UAE. Regions: Cities, farms and oases, Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Hajar Mountains and wadis, East coast.

How do you recognise dates?

Tall palm of farms, oases and streets; heavy bunches turn from green to yellow or red, then soften into rutab and dry into tamr.

How do you harvest dates?

Pick only ripe fruit that comes away easily, and only with the owner's permission: most trees and palms belong to a farm, a family or the municipality.

How do you eat dates?

Fresh rutab, dried tamr, date syrup (dibs) and luqaimat.

How do you store dates?

Dry fully, then keep cool in airtight jars.

What is dates good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with dates?

  • Date syrup (dibs): Dates simmered, pressed and reduced to a dark syrup.
  • Luqaimat with date syrup: Crisp fried dough balls drizzled with dibs.
  • Fresh rutab: Soft, half-ripe summer dates eaten straight from the bunch with Arabic coffee.
