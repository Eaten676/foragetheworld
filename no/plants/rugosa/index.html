Beach rose

Rosa rugosa
Also called Rynkerose

Beach rose is a wild fruit you can forage from August to September in Southern Norway, Western Norway and Eastern Norway. Invasive, very prickly rose of dunes and coasts with wrinkled leaves, pink flowers and big tomato-like hips.

Caution. Remove the hairy seeds.

When is beach rose in season in Norway?

Beach rose is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does beach rose grow in Norway?

Beach rose grows in Agder, Akershus, Buskerud, Innlandet, Møre og Romsdal, Oslo, Rogaland, Telemark, Vestfold, Vestland, Østfold. Regions: Southern Norway, Western Norway, Eastern Norway.

How do you recognise beach rose?

Invasive, very prickly rose of dunes and coasts with wrinkled leaves, pink flowers and big tomato-like hips.

How do you harvest beach rose?

Pick firm red hips in late summer; wear gloves against the prickles.

How do you eat beach rose?

Hips for syrup, jam and soup; petals for syrup.

How do you store beach rose?

Freeze or syrup.

What is beach rose good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.
