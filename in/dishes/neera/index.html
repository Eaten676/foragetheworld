Neera (fresh palm sap)

Neera (fresh palm sap) is made with wild date palm, palmyra palm, a favourite in South India, West coast and Deccan and East.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make neera (fresh palm sap)?

Collected at dawn from tapped palms and drunk straight away.

Note. It ferments into toddy within hours. Tapping is licensed in many states, so buy from an official neera outlet.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild date palm (Phoenix sylvestris): in season May to July
  • Palmyra palm (Borassus flabellifer): in season April to January
