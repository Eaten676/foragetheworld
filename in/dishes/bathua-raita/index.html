Bathua raita

Bathua raita is made with wild bathua, a favourite in Northern plains and Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bathua raita?

Boiled, chopped bathua folded into curd with roasted cumin.

Wild ingredients

  • Bathua (Chenopodium album): in season November to March
