Wood sorrel

Oxalis acetosella
Also called Wood sorrel, Seamsóg

Wood sorrel is a wild green you can forage from April to September across Ireland. Small clover-like leaves with three heart-shaped leaflets and white, lilac-veined flowers on shady woodland floors.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; small amounts.

When is wood sorrel in season in Ireland?

Wood sorrel is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wood sorrel grow in Ireland?

Wood sorrel grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise wood sorrel?

Small clover-like leaves with three heart-shaped leaflets and white, lilac-veined flowers on shady woodland floors.

How do you harvest wood sorrel?

Pinch off a few leaves and flowers from large patches; never pull up the creeping roots.

How do you eat wood sorrel?

A lemony garnish.

How do you store wood sorrel?

Best fresh.

What is wood sorrel good for?

Vitamin C.
