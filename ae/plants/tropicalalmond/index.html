Tropical almond

Terminalia catappa
Also called Beydam, Indian almond

Tropical almond is a wild nut you can forage from May to November in Cities, farms and oases, East coast and Gulf coast. Big shade tree of streets, parks and the east coast with huge leaves that turn red before falling and almond-shaped fruits that ripen from green to red-purple.

When is tropical almond in season in the UAE?

Tropical almond is usually in season May to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does tropical almond grow in the UAE?

Tropical almond grows across the UAE. Regions: Cities, farms and oases, East coast, Gulf coast.

How do you recognise tropical almond?

Big shade tree of streets, parks and the east coast with huge leaves that turn red before falling and almond-shaped fruits that ripen from green to red-purple.

How do you harvest tropical almond?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat tropical almond?

Crack the hard shell for the almond-like kernel, raw or toasted.

How do you store tropical almond?

Dry the kernels.

What is tropical almond good for?
