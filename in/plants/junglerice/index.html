Jungle rice

Echinochloa colona
Also called Sawa, Sanwak, Kuthiraivali (wild)

Jungle rice is a wild seed or grain you can forage from September to November in Northern plains, Central forests, East, West coast and Deccan, South India, Thar and dry northwest and Northeast. Wild ancestor of barnyard millet. A common grass of wet field edges, with purple-tinged seed heads in neat rows.

When is jungle rice in season in India?

Jungle rice is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does jungle rice grow in India?

Jungle rice grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Northern plains, Central forests, East, West coast and Deccan, South India, Thar and dry northwest, Northeast.

How do you recognise jungle rice?

Wild ancestor of barnyard millet. A common grass of wet field edges, with purple-tinged seed heads in neat rows.

How do you harvest jungle rice?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat jungle rice?

Cooked like millet or rice, porridge, fasting khichdi.

How do you store jungle rice?

Thresh, winnow and dry. Keeps months airtight.

What is jungle rice good for?

Calories, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with jungle rice?

  • Bhagar / nivara khichdi: Wild rice or jungle rice cooked with peanuts, cumin, green chilli and ghee. The classic fasting meal.
