Kalmi saag

Kalmi saag is made with wild water spinach, a favourite in East.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kalmi saag?

Water spinach stir-fried with nigella, garlic and dry chilli.

Wild ingredients

  • Water spinach (Ipomoea aquatica): in season June to November
