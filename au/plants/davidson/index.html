Davidson's plum

Davidsonia jerseyana
Also called Davidson's plum

Davidson's plum is a wild fruit you can forage from November to February in Subtropical east coast. Small rainforest tree with long hairy leaves; dark purple, very sour plums growing straight from the trunk.

When is Davidson's plum in season in Australia?

Davidson's plum is usually in season November to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does Davidson's plum grow in Australia?

Davidson's plum grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise Davidson's plum?

Small rainforest tree with long hairy leaves; dark purple, very sour plums growing straight from the trunk.

How do you harvest Davidson's plum?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat Davidson's plum?

Jam, sauces for meat, sorbet.

How do you store Davidson's plum?

Freeze or jam.

What is Davidson's plum good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with Davidson's plum?

  • Davidson's plum jam: Sour plums cooked with sugar into a deep red jam.
