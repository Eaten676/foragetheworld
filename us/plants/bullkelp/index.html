Bull kelp

Nereocystis luetkeana
Also called Bull kelp

Bull kelp is a wild green you can forage from May to August in Pacific Northwest, California and Alaska. Giant brown kelp with a long hollow stipe ending in a float and long blades.

Caution. Check state seaweed harvest rules and licences.

When is bull kelp in season in the United States?

Bull kelp is usually in season May to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bull kelp grow in the United States?

Bull kelp grows in Alaska, California, Hawaii, Idaho, Oregon, Washington. Regions: Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise bull kelp?

Giant brown kelp with a long hollow stipe ending in a float and long blades.

How do you harvest bull kelp?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat bull kelp?

Pickled stipe rings, dried blades as seasoning.

How do you store bull kelp?

Pickle or dry.

What is bull kelp good for?

Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with bull kelp?

  • Pickled bull kelp: Sliced kelp stipe rings in sweet dill brine.
