Hairy bittercress

Cardamine hirsuta
Also called Behaartes Schaumkraut, Cardamine hérissée

Hairy bittercress is a wild green you can forage from January to December across Switzerland. Small rosette of round leaflets in garden beds and paths; tiny white flowers and seed pods that spring open. Peppery like cress.

When is hairy bittercress in season in Switzerland?

Hairy bittercress is usually in season January to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does hairy bittercress grow in Switzerland?

Hairy bittercress grows across Switzerland. Regions: Jura, Swiss Plateau, Alps, Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you recognise hairy bittercress?

Small rosette of round leaflets in garden beds and paths; tiny white flowers and seed pods that spring open. Peppery like cress.

How do you harvest hairy bittercress?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat hairy bittercress?

Raw in salads and sandwiches.

How do you store hairy bittercress?

Best fresh.

What is hairy bittercress good for?

Vitamin C.
