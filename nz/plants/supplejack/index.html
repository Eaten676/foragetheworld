Supplejack shoots

Ripogonum scandens
Also called Kareao, Supplejack

Supplejack shoots is a root or shoot you can forage from September to December across New Zealand. Black, wiry vine that tangles the bush; new shoot tips look like asparagus.

When is supplejack shoots in season in New Zealand?

Supplejack shoots is usually in season September to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does supplejack shoots grow in New Zealand?

Supplejack shoots grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise supplejack shoots?

Black, wiry vine that tangles the bush; new shoot tips look like asparagus.

How do you harvest supplejack shoots?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat supplejack shoots?

Steam or eat raw like asparagus.

How do you store supplejack shoots?

Best fresh.
