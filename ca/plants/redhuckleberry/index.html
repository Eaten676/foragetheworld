Red huckleberry

Vaccinium parvifolium
Also called Red huckleberry, Airelle à petites feuilles

Red huckleberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to August in British Columbia. Airy shrub with green, angled twigs, often growing from old stumps in coastal forests, with see-through bright red berries.

When is red huckleberry in season in Canada?

Red huckleberry is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does red huckleberry grow in Canada?

Red huckleberry grows in British Columbia. Regions: British Columbia.

How do you recognise red huckleberry?

Airy shrub with green, angled twigs, often growing from old stumps in coastal forests, with see-through bright red berries.

How do you harvest red huckleberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat red huckleberry?

Fresh, in jam, pancakes and muffins; tart and bright.

How do you store red huckleberry?

Freeze.

What is red huckleberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with red huckleberry?

  • Red huckleberry jam: Tart coastal huckleberries cooked with sugar.
