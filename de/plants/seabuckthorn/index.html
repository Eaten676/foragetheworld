Sea buckthorn

Hippophae rhamnoides
Also called Sanddorn

Sea buckthorn is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in North German lowlands and coast and South and the Alps. Thorny coastal and riverside shrub with narrow silvery leaves and dense clusters of bright orange berries.

When is sea buckthorn in season in Germany?

Sea buckthorn is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea buckthorn grow in Germany?

Sea buckthorn grows in Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, Bremen, Hamburg, Lower Saxony, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Schleswig-Holstein. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise sea buckthorn?

Thorny coastal and riverside shrub with narrow silvery leaves and dense clusters of bright orange berries.

How do you harvest sea buckthorn?

Cut whole small branches with secateurs where allowed, freeze them, then shake or knock off the berries.

How do you eat sea buckthorn?

Juice, syrup, jam; very sour and rich in vitamin C.

How do you store sea buckthorn?

Freeze whole branches, then shake off berries.

What is sea buckthorn good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with sea buckthorn?

  • Sea buckthorn juice (Sanddornsaft): Sour Baltic coast berries juiced and sweetened.
