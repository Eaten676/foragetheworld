Sweet woodruff

Galium odoratum
Also called Lievevrouwebedstro

Sweet woodruff is a edible flower or tea you can forage from April to May in East and South. Low woodland plant with whorls of narrow leaves and white star flowers; smells of hay and vanilla when wilted.

Caution. Contains coumarin; use only a small bunch per litre.

When is sweet woodruff in season in the Netherlands?

Sweet woodruff is usually in season April to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sweet woodruff grow in the Netherlands?

Sweet woodruff grows in Drenthe, Flevoland, Friesland, Gelderland, Groningen, Limburg, North Brabant, Overijssel, Zeeland. Regions: East, South.

How do you recognise sweet woodruff?

Low woodland plant with whorls of narrow leaves and white star flowers; smells of hay and vanilla when wilted.

How do you harvest sweet woodruff?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat sweet woodruff?

Woodruff syrup and May punch; wilt before using.

How do you store sweet woodruff?

Dry.
