Bhurat roti

Bhurat roti is made with wild bhurat.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bhurat roti?

Ground bhurat seeds mixed into bajra flour and patted into thick rotis. Desert famine fare.

Wild ingredients

  • Bhurat (Cenchrus biflorus): in season September to October
