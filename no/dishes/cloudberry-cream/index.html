Cloudberry cream (multekrem)

Cloudberry cream (multekrem) is made with wild cloudberry, a favourite in Northern Norway and Trøndelag.

How do you make cloudberry cream (multekrem)?

Cloudberries folded into whipped cream; a Christmas dessert.

Wild ingredients

  • Cloudberry (Rubus chamaemorus): in season July to August
