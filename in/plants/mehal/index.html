Wild Himalayan pear

Pyrus pashia
Also called Mehal, Batangi, Kainth

Wild Himalayan pear is a wild fruit you can forage from October to December in Himalaya. Small hill tree with hard, speckled brown pears.

When is wild Himalayan pear in season in India?

Wild Himalayan pear is usually in season October to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild Himalayan pear grow in India?

Wild Himalayan pear grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise wild Himalayan pear?

Small hill tree with hard, speckled brown pears.

How do you harvest wild Himalayan pear?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat wild Himalayan pear?

Only once overripe and soft, then fresh.

How do you store wild Himalayan pear?

Let it soften in hay, then eat.

What is wild Himalayan pear good for?

Calories.
