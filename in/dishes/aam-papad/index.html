Aam papad

Aam papad is made with wild mango, a favourite in Northern plains, West coast and Deccan and Thar and dry northwest.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make aam papad?

Mango pulp and jaggery sun-dried in thin layers into fruit leather.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild mango (Mangifera indica): in season April to June
