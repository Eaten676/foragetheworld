Basale bassaru

Basale bassaru is made with wild Malabar spinach, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make basale bassaru?

Malabar spinach and dal stock as a spicy saaru, with the greens as palya. Two dishes from one pot.

Wild ingredients

  • Malabar spinach (Basella alba): in season June to November
