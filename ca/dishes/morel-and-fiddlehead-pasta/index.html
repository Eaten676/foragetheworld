Morel and fiddlehead pasta

Morel and fiddlehead pasta is made with wild morel, ostrich fern fiddleheads, a favourite in Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make morel and fiddlehead pasta?

Spring morels and boiled fiddleheads tossed with butter and pasta.

Wild ingredients

  • Morel (Morchella spp.): in season May to June
  • Ostrich fern fiddleheads (Matteuccia struthiopteris): in season May to June
Seasons and ranges are approximate and shift with weather and altitude. Identify every plant with an experienced local before eating it. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited on each page.
