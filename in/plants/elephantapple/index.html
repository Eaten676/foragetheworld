Elephant apple

Dillenia indica
Also called Ou tenga, Chalta, Uva

Elephant apple is a wild fruit you can forage from October to January in Northeast, East and South India. Forest tree with huge pleated leaves and fist-sized green fruit with thick, sour fleshy layers.

When is elephant apple in season in India?

Elephant apple is usually in season October to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does elephant apple grow in India?

Elephant apple grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: Northeast, East, South India.

How do you recognise elephant apple?

Forest tree with huge pleated leaves and fist-sized green fruit with thick, sour fleshy layers.

How do you harvest elephant apple?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat elephant apple?

Ou tenga dal and fish curry, chutney, pickle.

How do you store elephant apple?

Slice and sun-dry, or pickle.

What is elephant apple good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with elephant apple?

  • Ou tenga chutney: Elephant apple layers boiled and mashed with mustard oil, chilli and salt.
  • Ou tenga dal: Masoor dal soured with elephant apple slices. Assamese summer comfort.
