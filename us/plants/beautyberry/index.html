American beautyberry

Callicarpa americana
Also called American beautyberry, French mulberry

American beautyberry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in Southeast and Southwest. Southern understory shrub with startling clusters of glossy magenta berries hugging the stem at each leaf pair.

Caution. Eat cooked and in moderation; large amounts of raw berries can upset the stomach.

When is American beautyberry in season in the United States?

American beautyberry is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does American beautyberry grow in the United States?

American beautyberry grows in Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, Nevada, New Mexico, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, West Virginia. Regions: Southeast, Southwest.

How do you recognise American beautyberry?

Southern understory shrub with startling clusters of glossy magenta berries hugging the stem at each leaf pair.

How do you harvest American beautyberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat American beautyberry?

Bland raw; cooked into a floral jelly. Crushed leaves are a folk mosquito repellent.

How do you store American beautyberry?

Make jelly.

What can you cook with American beautyberry?

  • Beautyberry jelly: Beautyberries simmered and strained into a floral pink jelly.
