Wild thyme

Thymus serpyllum
Also called Backtimjan

Wild thyme is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to August across Sweden. Low aromatic mat with tiny leaves and pink-purple flowers on dry, sunny grassland and rocks.

When is wild thyme in season in Sweden?

Wild thyme is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild thyme grow in Sweden?

Wild thyme grows across Sweden. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle), Norrland (north).

How do you recognise wild thyme?

Low aromatic mat with tiny leaves and pink-purple flowers on dry, sunny grassland and rocks.

How do you harvest wild thyme?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat wild thyme?

Herb for meat and potatoes; tea.

How do you store wild thyme?

Dry.
