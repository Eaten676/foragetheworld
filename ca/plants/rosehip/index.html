Rose hips

Rosa
Also called Wild rose, Cynorrhodon

Rose hips is a wild fruit you can forage from September to December across Canada. Red-orange hips on wild rose bushes after the flowers fade, best after the first frost.

Caution. Strain out the hairy seeds, which irritate.

When is rose hips in season in Canada?

Rose hips is usually in season September to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does rose hips grow in Canada?

Rose hips grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise rose hips?

Red-orange hips on wild rose bushes after the flowers fade, best after the first frost.

How do you harvest rose hips?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat rose hips?

Syrup, tea, jelly.

How do you store rose hips?

Dry for tea; syrup.

What is rose hips good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.
