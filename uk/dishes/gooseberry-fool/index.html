Gooseberry fool

Gooseberry fool is made with wild gooseberry, elderflower.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make gooseberry fool?

Stewed gooseberries with elderflower folded into cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild gooseberry (Ribes uva-crispa): in season June to July
  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
