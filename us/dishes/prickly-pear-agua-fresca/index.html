Prickly pear agua fresca

Prickly pear agua fresca is made with wild prickly pear, a favourite in Southwest and California.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make prickly pear agua fresca?

Blend peeled tunas with water and lime, strain out the seeds and serve over ice.

Wild ingredients

  • Prickly pear (Opuntia): in season July to October
