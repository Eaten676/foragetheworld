Karibevu podi

Karibevu podi is made with wild curry leaves, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make karibevu podi?

Dry-roasted curry leaves with urad dal, chilli and tamarind, ground to a powder for rice and dosa.

Wild ingredients

  • Curry leaves (Murraya koenigii): in season all year round
