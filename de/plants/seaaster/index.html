Sea aster

Tripolium pannonicum
Also called Strand-Aster

Sea aster is a wild green you can forage from April to July in North German lowlands and coast. Fleshy, lance-shaped leaves on salt marshes, with purple Michaelmas-daisy flowers in late summer.

When is sea aster in season in Germany?

Sea aster is usually in season April to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea aster grow in Germany?

Sea aster grows in Bremen, Hamburg, Lower Saxony, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Schleswig-Holstein. Regions: North German lowlands and coast.

How do you recognise sea aster?

Fleshy, lance-shaped leaves on salt marshes, with purple Michaelmas-daisy flowers in late summer.

How do you harvest sea aster?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat sea aster?

Salty, succulent leaves raw, steamed or in tempura.

How do you store sea aster?

Best fresh.

What is sea aster good for?

Vitamin A.
