Bog myrtle

Myrica gale
Also called Pors

Bog myrtle is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to August in Götaland (south) and Svealand (middle). Low shrub of wet bogs with grey-green, resin-scented leaves.

Caution. Avoid in pregnancy; small amounts.

When is bog myrtle in season in Sweden?

Bog myrtle is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bog myrtle grow in Sweden?

Bog myrtle grows in Blekinge, Dalarna, Gotland, Halland, Jönköping, Kalmar, Kronoberg, Skåne, Stockholm, Södermanland, Uppsala, Värmland, Västmanland, Västra Götaland, Örebro, Östergötland. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle).

How do you recognise bog myrtle?

Low shrub of wet bogs with grey-green, resin-scented leaves.

How do you harvest bog myrtle?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat bog myrtle?

Old beer flavouring (gale); leaves as a herb.

How do you store bog myrtle?

Dry.
