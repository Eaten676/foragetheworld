Bamboo rice

Bambusa bambos
Also called Mulayari, Bidiru akki, Moongil arisi

Bamboo rice is a wild seed or grain you can forage from March to May in South India, Northeast, East and Central forests. Seed from bamboo clumps that flower only once in decades, then die. Rare but abundant when it happens.

When is bamboo rice in season in India?

Bamboo rice is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bamboo rice grow in India?

Bamboo rice grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: South India, Northeast, East, Central forests.

How do you recognise bamboo rice?

Seed from bamboo clumps that flower only once in decades, then die. Rare but abundant when it happens.

How do you harvest bamboo rice?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat bamboo rice?

Cooked like rice, payasam.

How do you store bamboo rice?

Dry well. Keeps like rice.

What is bamboo rice good for?

Calories, Protein.

What can you cook with bamboo rice?

  • Bamboo rice: Cooked like ordinary rice, with a nutty, sticky bite.
  • Bamboo rice payasam: Bamboo rice simmered in coconut milk and jaggery with cardamom.
