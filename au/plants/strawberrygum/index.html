Strawberry gum

Eucalyptus olida
Also called Strawberry gum

Strawberry gum is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round in Subtropical east coast. Gum tree of the New England Tablelands with narrow leaves that smell of strawberries and cinnamon.

Caution. Use sparingly; strong eucalyptus oils.

When is strawberry gum in season in Australia?

Strawberry gum is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does strawberry gum grow in Australia?

Strawberry gum grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise strawberry gum?

Gum tree of the New England Tablelands with narrow leaves that smell of strawberries and cinnamon.

How do you harvest strawberry gum?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat strawberry gum?

Dried, ground leaves flavour desserts, tea and syrups.

How do you store strawberry gum?

Dry and grind.

What can you cook with strawberry gum?

  • Strawberry gum iced tea: Dried strawberry gum leaves steeped and chilled with honey.
