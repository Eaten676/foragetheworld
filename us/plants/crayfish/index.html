Crayfish

Procambarus clarkii
Also called Crawfish, Crawdad

Crayfish is a wild protein you can forage from March to July in Southeast, Midwest, Northeast, California and Pacific Northwest. Small freshwater lobsters under rocks in streams and in ditches and rice fields.

Caution. Check fishing licence rules and water quality.

When is crayfish in season in the United States?

Crayfish is usually in season March to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does crayfish grow in the United States?

Crayfish grows in Alabama, Arkansas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin. Regions: Southeast, Midwest, Northeast, California, Pacific Northwest.

How do you recognise crayfish?

Small freshwater lobsters under rocks in streams and in ditches and rice fields.

How do you harvest crayfish?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat crayfish?

Crawfish boil, étouffée.

How do you store crayfish?

Cook and freeze tail meat.

What is crayfish good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with crayfish?

  • Crawfish étouffée: Crawfish tails in a roux of butter, onion, celery and pepper over rice.
