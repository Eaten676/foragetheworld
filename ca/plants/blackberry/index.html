Blackberry

Rubus allegheniensis
Also called Blackberry, Mûre

Blackberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to September in British Columbia and Ontario. Thorny arching canes with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen from red to glossy black and keep their white core.

When is blackberry in season in Canada?

Blackberry is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blackberry grow in Canada?

Blackberry grows in British Columbia, Ontario. Regions: British Columbia, Ontario.

How do you recognise blackberry?

Thorny arching canes with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen from red to glossy black and keep their white core.

How do you harvest blackberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat blackberry?

Fresh, cobbler, jam, pie.

How do you store blackberry?

Freeze on trays, then bag.

What is blackberry good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.
