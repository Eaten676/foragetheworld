Gorse flowers

Ulex europaeus
Also called Gorse, Furze, Whin

Gorse flowers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from February to May across the UK. Spiny evergreen shrub covered in coconut-scented yellow pea flowers, most of the year.

Caution. Eat only the flowers, in moderation.

When is gorse flowers in season in the UK?

Gorse flowers is usually in season February to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does gorse flowers grow in the UK?

Gorse flowers grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise gorse flowers?

Spiny evergreen shrub covered in coconut-scented yellow pea flowers, most of the year.

How do you harvest gorse flowers?

Pick open flowers carefully with gloves; scent is strongest on sunny days.

How do you eat gorse flowers?

Gorse flower cordial, wine and syrup.

How do you store gorse flowers?

Cordial.
