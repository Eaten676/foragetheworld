Abalone

Haliotis rubra
Also called Abalone

Abalone is a wild protein you can forage all year round in Temperate south-east, Tasmania and South-west. Blacklip abalone clamp onto rocks below the low-tide mark; one shell with a row of holes.

Caution. Strict licences, size limits and season closures apply in every state.

When is abalone in season in Australia?

Abalone is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does abalone grow in Australia?

Abalone grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria, Western Australia. Regions: Temperate south-east, Tasmania, South-west.

How do you recognise abalone?

Blacklip abalone clamp onto rocks below the low-tide mark; one shell with a row of holes.

How do you harvest abalone?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat abalone?

Thinly sliced and seared, or tenderised and fried.

How do you store abalone?

Freeze.

What is abalone good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with abalone?

  • Seared abalone: Thinly sliced abalone seared in butter and garlic.
