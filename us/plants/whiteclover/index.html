White clover

Trifolium repens
Also called White clover

White clover is a edible flower or tea you can forage from May to September across the United States. Creeping clover in lawns with white flower heads.

When is white clover in season in the United States?

White clover is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does white clover grow in the United States?

White clover grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise white clover?

Creeping clover in lawns with white flower heads.

How do you harvest white clover?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat white clover?

Flowers in tea and salads.

How do you store white clover?

Dry.
