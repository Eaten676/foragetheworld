Chia fresca

Chia fresca is made with wild desert chia, a favourite in Southwest and California.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make chia fresca?

Chia seeds soaked in water with lime and a little honey.

Wild ingredients

  • Desert chia (Salvia columbariae): in season May to July
