Steamed alexanders with butter

Steamed alexanders with butter is made with wild alexanders, a favourite in South East England, South West England and Wales.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make steamed alexanders with butter?

Peeled young stems steamed and buttered like asparagus.

Wild ingredients

  • Alexanders (Smyrnium olusatrum): in season January to April
