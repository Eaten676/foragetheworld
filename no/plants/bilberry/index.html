Bilberry

Vaccinium myrtillus
Also called Blåbær

Bilberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to September across Norway. Low shrub of acid woods and heaths with bright green angled stems; dark blue berries that stain purple inside.

When is bilberry in season in Norway?

Bilberry is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bilberry grow in Norway?

Bilberry grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise bilberry?

Low shrub of acid woods and heaths with bright green angled stems; dark blue berries that stain purple inside.

How do you harvest bilberry?

Pick by hand or with a berry comb from the low shrubs; the comb is not allowed everywhere, so check local rules.

How do you eat bilberry?

Fresh, pies, soup, pancakes.

How do you store bilberry?

Freeze or dry.

What can be confused with bilberry?

Bog bilberry (Vaccinium uliginosum), rated mild. Bilberry stems are green and angled; bog bilberry has round brown stems and blue-grey leaves. Bog bilberry flesh is pale, bilberry is purple right through. Bog bilberry is edible but can cause headaches in large amounts.

What is bilberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with bilberry?

  • Bilberry muffins (blåbærmuffins): Muffins packed with forest bilberries.
  • Bilberry soup (blåbærsuppe): Warm bilberries thickened with potato starch.
