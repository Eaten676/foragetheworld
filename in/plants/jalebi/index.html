Jungle jalebi

Pithecellobium dulce
Also called Vilayati imli, Seemai puli, Kodukkapuli, Ganga imli

Jungle jalebi is a wild fruit you can forage from March to May in South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains and Thar and dry northwest. Thorny hedge tree with coiled pink-green pods holding sweet white pulp.

Caution. Eat the pulp only, not the black seeds.

When is jungle jalebi in season in India?

Jungle jalebi is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does jungle jalebi grow in India?

Jungle jalebi grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise jungle jalebi?

Thorny hedge tree with coiled pink-green pods holding sweet white pulp.

How do you harvest jungle jalebi?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat jungle jalebi?

Pulp fresh, straight from the pod.

How do you store jungle jalebi?

Eat fresh.

What is jungle jalebi good for?

Calories, Protein.
