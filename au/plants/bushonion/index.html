Bush onion

Cyperus bulbosus
Also called Bush onion

Bush onion is a root or shoot you can forage from March to September in Arid centre and Tropical north. Grass-like sedge of inland plains and claypans; small onion-like bulbs below the ground.

When is bush onion in season in Australia?

Bush onion is usually in season March to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does bush onion grow in Australia?

Bush onion grows in Northern Territory, Queensland, South Australia, Western Australia. Regions: Arid centre, Tropical north.

How do you recognise bush onion?

Grass-like sedge of inland plains and claypans; small onion-like bulbs below the ground.

How do you harvest bush onion?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat bush onion?

Bulbs roasted in hot sand and ashes, then rubbed to remove the skin; a key desert food (nalgoo).

How do you store bush onion?

Dry roasted bulbs.

What is bush onion good for?

Calories.
