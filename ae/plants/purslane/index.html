Purslane

Portulaca oleracea
Also called Baqla, Rijla

Purslane is a wild green you can forage from March to November in Cities, farms and oases and Gulf coast. Fleshy, paddle-shaped leaves on smooth reddish stems that sprawl over irrigated gardens, farms and pavements in the hot months.

Caution. Prostrate spurge looks similar but has milky sap; purslane has none.

When is purslane in season in the UAE?

Purslane is usually in season March to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does purslane grow in the UAE?

Purslane grows across the UAE. Regions: Cities, farms and oases, Gulf coast.

How do you recognise purslane?

Fleshy, paddle-shaped leaves on smooth reddish stems that sprawl over irrigated gardens, farms and pavements in the hot months.

How do you harvest purslane?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.

How do you eat purslane?

Raw in salads and fattoush, or cooked with tomato and egg.

How do you store purslane?

Best fresh.

What can be confused with purslane?

Prostrate spurge (Euphorbia prostrata and relatives), rated harmful. Purslane has thick, fleshy leaves and clear, watery sap. Spurges are thin and flat with tiny leaves, and bleed white milky sap that burns skin and eyes.

What is purslane good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with purslane?

  • Purslane fattoush: Toasted bread, purslane, tomato, cucumber and sumac dressing.
