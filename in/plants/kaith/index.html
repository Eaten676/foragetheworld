Wood apple

Limonia acidissima
Also called Kaith, Bela, Vilampazham, Kavath

Wood apple is a wild fruit you can forage from October to March in South India, Central forests, West coast and Deccan, East and Northern plains. Thorny tree of dry scrub. The fruit is a hard grey ball holding brown, sweet-sour pulp.

When is wood apple in season in India?

Wood apple is usually in season October to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wood apple grow in India?

Wood apple grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, Central forests, West coast and Deccan, East, Northern plains.

How do you recognise wood apple?

Thorny tree of dry scrub. The fruit is a hard grey ball holding brown, sweet-sour pulp.

How do you harvest wood apple?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat wood apple?

Pulp with jaggery, chutney, or vilampazham pachadi.

How do you store wood apple?

Whole fruit keeps weeks. Dry the pulp with jaggery.

What can be confused with wood apple?

Nux vomica (kuchla) (Strychnos nux-vomica), rated deadly. Nux vomica fruit is smooth and orange with a thin, soft skin. Bael and wood apple have hard, woody shells you have to crack. Its seeds are flat, grey, velvety discs like buttons, and contain strychnine. Bael and wood apple trees have thorns. Nux vomica has glossy leaves with three strong veins.

What is wood apple good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with wood apple?

  • Wood apple sharbat: Scoop out the pulp, mash it in water with jaggery, strain, and add roasted cumin.
  • Kaith chutney: Wood apple pulp ground with jaggery, green chilli and roasted cumin.
