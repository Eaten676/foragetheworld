Steamed periwinkles (wrinkles)

Steamed periwinkles (wrinkles) is made with wild periwinkles, a favourite in Atlantic Canada.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make steamed periwinkles (wrinkles)?

Periwinkles boiled in salted water and picked out with a pin.

Wild ingredients

  • Periwinkles (Littorina littorea): in season all year round
