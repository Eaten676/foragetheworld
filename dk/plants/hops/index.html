Hop shoots

Humulus lupulus
Also called Humle

Hop shoots is a root or shoot you can forage from March to May across Denmark. Climbing vine of hedges and riverbanks; tender spring shoots taste like asparagus.

When is hop shoots in season in Denmark?

Hop shoots is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does hop shoots grow in Denmark?

Hop shoots grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise hop shoots?

Climbing vine of hedges and riverbanks; tender spring shoots taste like asparagus.

How do you harvest hop shoots?

Snap off the top 10–15 cm of new shoots where they bend easily, like asparagus.

How do you eat hop shoots?

Blanched in butter, in omelettes or risotto.

How do you store hop shoots?

Pickle or freeze.

What is hop shoots good for?

Vitamin C.
