Red clover

Trifolium pratense
Also called Red clover

Red clover is a edible flower or tea you can forage from May to September across the UK. Leaves of three with a pale chevron and round pink-purple flower heads.

When is red clover in season in the UK?

Red clover is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does red clover grow in the UK?

Red clover grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise red clover?

Leaves of three with a pale chevron and round pink-purple flower heads.

How do you harvest red clover?

Pick fresh pink heads in full bloom, avoiding brown ones.

How do you eat red clover?

Tea; flowers on salads.

How do you store red clover?

Dry the heads.

What is red clover good for?

Protein.
