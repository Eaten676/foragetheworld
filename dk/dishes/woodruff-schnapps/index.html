Woodruff schnapps (skovmærkesnaps)

Woodruff schnapps (skovmærkesnaps) is made with wild sweet woodruff.

How do you make woodruff schnapps (skovmærkesnaps)?

Wilted woodruff steeped briefly in aquavit.

Wild ingredients

  • Sweet woodruff (Galium odoratum): in season April to May
