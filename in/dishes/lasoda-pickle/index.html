Lasoda pickle

Lasoda pickle is made with wild lasoda, a favourite in Thar and dry northwest and Northern plains.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make lasoda pickle?

Green lasoda berries pickled in mustard oil with fennel and turmeric.

Wild ingredients

  • Lasoda (Cordia myxa): in season April to June
