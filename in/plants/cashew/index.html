Cashew apple & nut

Anacardium occidentale
Also called Kaju, Geru, Mundiri, Kaju bondu

Cashew apple & nut is a wild nut you can forage from March to May in South India, West coast and Deccan and East. Coastal tree with a juicy apple and the nut hanging beneath it. The apple is very high in vitamin C.

Caution. Raw shells hold a caustic oil that burns skin. Roast outdoors and stay out of the smoke.

When is cashew apple & nut in season in India?

Cashew apple & nut is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cashew apple & nut grow in India?

Cashew apple & nut grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East.

How do you recognise cashew apple & nut?

Coastal tree with a juicy apple and the nut hanging beneath it. The apple is very high in vitamin C.

How do you harvest cashew apple & nut?

Gather fallen ripe apples, twist off the nut, and handle raw nuts with gloves.

How do you eat cashew apple & nut?

Apple fresh or in curry. Nuts after proper roasting.

How do you store cashew apple & nut?

Roasted shelled nuts keep for months.

What is cashew apple & nut good for?

Vitamin C, Fat, Protein, Calories.

What can you cook with cashew apple & nut?

  • Cashew apple juice: Squeeze ripe cashew apples, then mix the juice with a little rice kanji or salt to cut the throat-catching astringency.
