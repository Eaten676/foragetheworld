Lasoda

Cordia myxa
Also called Lasoda, Gunda, Lasura

Lasoda is a wild fruit you can forage from April to June in Thar and dry northwest, Northern plains, West coast and Deccan and Central forests. Small tree with clusters of sticky green berries.

When is lasoda in season in India?

Lasoda is usually in season April to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lasoda grow in India?

Lasoda grows in Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Rajasthan, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand. Regions: Thar and dry northwest, Northern plains, West coast and Deccan, Central forests.

How do you recognise lasoda?

Small tree with clusters of sticky green berries.

How do you harvest lasoda?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat lasoda?

Pickle or sabzi when green.

How do you store lasoda?

Pickle in mustard oil.

What can you cook with lasoda?

  • Lasoda pickle: Green lasoda berries pickled in mustard oil with fennel and turmeric.
  • Panchkuta: The Thar's five-ingredient desert curry, cooked dry with spices and oil.
