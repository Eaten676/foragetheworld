Common mallow

Malva sylvestris
Also called Almindelig katost

Common mallow is a wild green you can forage from May to September across Denmark. Sprawling plant of verges and waste ground with round, lobed leaves and pink-purple flowers striped with dark veins.

When is common mallow in season in Denmark?

Common mallow is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does common mallow grow in Denmark?

Common mallow grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise common mallow?

Sprawling plant of verges and waste ground with round, lobed leaves and pink-purple flowers striped with dark veins.

How do you harvest common mallow?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat common mallow?

Young leaves cooked or in soup (they thicken it); flowers in salads; green 'cheese' seed pods raw.

How do you store common mallow?

Dry leaves.

What is common mallow good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C.
