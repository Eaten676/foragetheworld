Sweetfern

Comptonia peregrina
Also called Sweetfern, Comptonie voyageuse

Sweetfern is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to September in Ontario, Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Not a fern: a low, aromatic shrub of sandy clearings with long, fern-like scalloped leaves.

When is sweetfern in season in Canada?

Sweetfern is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sweetfern grow in Canada?

Sweetfern grows in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise sweetfern?

Not a fern: a low, aromatic shrub of sandy clearings with long, fern-like scalloped leaves.

How do you harvest sweetfern?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat sweetfern?

Leaves for tea and as a seasoning for fish.

How do you store sweetfern?

Dry leaves.
