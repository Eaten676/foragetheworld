Kinpira gobo

Kinpira gobo is made with wild burdock root.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kinpira gobo?

Matchstick burdock root stir-fried with carrot, soy, mirin and sesame.

Wild ingredients

  • Burdock root (Arctium minus): in season April to October
