Bael sharbat

Bael sharbat is made with wild bael, a favourite in Northern plains, East and Central forests.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bael sharbat?

Crack the shell, soak the pulp in water, mash, and strain out the seeds and fibres. Sweeten with jaggery and add a pinch of black salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Bael (Aegle marmelos): in season March to June
