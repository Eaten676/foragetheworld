Mahua laddoo

Mahua laddoo is made with wild mahua, a favourite in Central forests and East.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make mahua laddoo?

Dried mahua flowers roasted and pounded with roasted grains and sesame.

Wild ingredients

  • Mahua (Madhuca longifolia): in season March to June
