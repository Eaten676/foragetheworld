Mussels

Mytilus galloprovincialis
Also called Blue mussel

Mussels is a wild protein you can forage from April to October in Temperate south-east, Tasmania and South-west. Blue-black shells on rocks and pylons.

Caution. Check state bag limits and shellfish closures before collecting; many areas are closed or protected.

When is mussels in season in Australia?

Mussels is usually in season April to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mussels grow in Australia?

Mussels grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria, Western Australia. Regions: Temperate south-east, Tasmania, South-west.

How do you recognise mussels?

Blue-black shells on rocks and pylons.

How do you harvest mussels?

Twist mussels off rocks at low tide, taking only large ones, and check local toxin warnings first.

How do you eat mussels?

Steamed with garlic and wine.

How do you store mussels?

Eat the same day.

What is mussels good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with mussels?

  • Steamed mussels with river mint: Mussels steamed with garlic, white wine and native mint.
