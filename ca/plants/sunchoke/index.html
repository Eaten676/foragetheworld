Jerusalem artichoke

Helianthus tuberosus
Also called Sunchoke, Topinambour

Jerusalem artichoke is a root or shoot you can forage from November to April in Ontario, Quebec and Prairies. Tall sunflower with small yellow flowers; knobbly tubers under the plant, sweetest after frost.

Caution. Rich in inulin; may cause gas.

When is Jerusalem artichoke in season in Canada?

Jerusalem artichoke is usually in season November to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does Jerusalem artichoke grow in Canada?

Jerusalem artichoke grows in Alberta, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, Saskatchewan. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Prairies.

How do you recognise Jerusalem artichoke?

Tall sunflower with small yellow flowers; knobbly tubers under the plant, sweetest after frost.

How do you harvest Jerusalem artichoke?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat Jerusalem artichoke?

Roasted, soup, chips.

How do you store Jerusalem artichoke?

Store in damp sand.
