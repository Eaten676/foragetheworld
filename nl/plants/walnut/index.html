Wild nut · Sep–Oct

Walnut

Juglans regia
Also called Walnoot

Walnut is a wild nut you can forage from September to October across the Netherlands. Tree with leaves of 5–9 smooth leaflets; green husks split to drop wrinkled nuts.

When is walnut in season in the Netherlands?

Walnut is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does walnut grow in the Netherlands?

Walnut grows across the Netherlands. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden, West, East, South.

How do you recognise walnut?

Tree with leaves of 5–9 smooth leaflets; green husks split to drop wrinkled nuts.

How do you harvest walnut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat walnut?

Raw, in cakes, Engadine nut tart; green walnuts for liqueur in June.

How do you store walnut?

Dry in the shell.

What is walnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.
