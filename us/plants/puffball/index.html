Giant puffball

Calvatia gigantea
Also called Giant puffball

Giant puffball is a wild mushroom you can forage from August to October in Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains and Rocky Mountains. Smooth white ball the size of a football or bigger, lying in meadows and field edges with no stem or gills. Pure white and firm like tofu inside when young.

Caution. Always slice in half top to bottom: it must be pure white all through. Any outline of a cap or gills inside means a deadly Amanita egg.

When is giant puffball in season in the United States?

Giant puffball is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does giant puffball grow in the United States?

Giant puffball grows in Alabama, Arkansas, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Rocky Mountains.

How do you recognise giant puffball?

Smooth white ball the size of a football or bigger, lying in meadows and field edges with no stem or gills. Pure white and firm like tofu inside when young.

How do you harvest giant puffball?

Cut only young, pure-white, firm puffballs; slice each in half to check it's white all through.

How do you eat giant puffball?

Sliced thick and fried like steak, or breaded.

How do you store giant puffball?

Cook fresh; fried slices freeze.

What can be confused with giant puffball?

Death cap (young 'egg') (Amanita phalloides), rated deadly. Cut every puffball in half: a puffball is pure white and uniform inside. A young Amanita shows the outline of a cap, gills and stem inside the 'egg'. Death caps kill; there is no safe amount.

What is giant puffball good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with giant puffball?

  • Puffball parmesan: Breaded puffball slices baked with tomato and cheese.
