Wild strawberry

Fragaria virginiana
Also called Wild strawberry, Fraise des champs

Wild strawberry is a wild fruit you can forage from May to July across Canada. Low plant with leaves in threes and white five-petalled flowers; tiny intensely sweet berries.

When is wild strawberry in season in Canada?

Wild strawberry is usually in season May to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild strawberry grow in Canada?

Wild strawberry grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise wild strawberry?

Low plant with leaves in threes and white five-petalled flowers; tiny intensely sweet berries.

How do you harvest wild strawberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat wild strawberry?

Fresh, or folded into cream.

How do you store wild strawberry?

Freeze or jam.

What is wild strawberry good for?

Vitamin C.
