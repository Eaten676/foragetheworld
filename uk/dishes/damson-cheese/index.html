Damson cheese

Damson cheese is made with wild damson and bullace, a favourite in Midlands and East and Northern England.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make damson cheese?

Damson pulp boiled down into a firm fruit paste for cheese boards.

Wild ingredients

  • Damson and bullace (Prunus domestica subsp. insititia): in season August to October
