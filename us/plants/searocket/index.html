Sea rocket

Cakile edentula
Also called Sea rocket, American sea rocket

Sea rocket is a wild green you can forage from June to September in Northeast, Midwest, California and Pacific Northwest. Fleshy, lobed leaves on sandy beaches above the tide line, with lilac flowers.

When is sea rocket in season in the United States?

Sea rocket is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea rocket grow in the United States?

Sea rocket grows in California, Connecticut, Delaware, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, Washington, West Virginia, Wisconsin. Regions: Northeast, Midwest, California, Pacific Northwest.

How do you recognise sea rocket?

Fleshy, lobed leaves on sandy beaches above the tide line, with lilac flowers.

How do you harvest sea rocket?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat sea rocket?

Peppery leaves and pods raw or quickly cooked.

How do you store sea rocket?

Best fresh.

What is sea rocket good for?

Vitamin C.
