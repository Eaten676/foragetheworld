Wild chives

Allium schoenoprasum
Also called Purløg

Wild chives is a wild green you can forage from May to August in Bornholm. Clumps of thin, hollow, onion-scented leaves with round purple flower heads on rocky shores, river gravels and alpine meadows.

When is wild chives in season in Denmark?

Wild chives is usually in season May to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild chives grow in Denmark?

Wild chives grows in Capital Region (Hovedstaden). Regions: Bornholm.

How do you recognise wild chives?

Clumps of thin, hollow, onion-scented leaves with round purple flower heads on rocky shores, river gravels and alpine meadows.

How do you harvest wild chives?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat wild chives?

Snip over eggs, potatoes and fish.

How do you store wild chives?

Freeze chopped.

What is wild chives good for?

Vitamin C.
