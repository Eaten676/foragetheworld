Panang kizhangu

Panang kizhangu is made with wild palm tuber, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make panang kizhangu?

Palm tubers boiled with turmeric and salt, peeled and eaten warm.

Wild ingredients

  • Palm tuber (Borassus flabellifer): in season December to January
