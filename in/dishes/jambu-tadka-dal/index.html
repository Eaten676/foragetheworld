Jambu tadka dal

Jambu tadka dal is made with wild jambu (himalayan wild onion), a favourite in Himalaya.

How do you make jambu tadka dal?

Dal finished with jambu leaves sizzled in ghee. The Garhwali signature.

Wild ingredients

  • Jambu (Himalayan wild onion) (Allium stracheyi): in season June to August
