Arctic bramble

Rubus arcticus
Also called Arctic bramble, Ronce arctique

Arctic bramble is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August in The North, Prairies and Quebec. Tiny, thornless northern plant with pink flowers and dark red, intensely fragrant berries.

When is arctic bramble in season in Canada?

Arctic bramble is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does arctic bramble grow in Canada?

Arctic bramble grows in Alberta, Manitoba, Newfoundland and Labrador, Northwest Territories, Nunavut, Quebec, Saskatchewan, Yukon. Regions: The North, Prairies, Quebec.

How do you recognise arctic bramble?

Tiny, thornless northern plant with pink flowers and dark red, intensely fragrant berries.

How do you harvest arctic bramble?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat arctic bramble?

Fresh, liqueur, cream.

How do you store arctic bramble?

Freeze.

What is arctic bramble good for?

Vitamin C.
