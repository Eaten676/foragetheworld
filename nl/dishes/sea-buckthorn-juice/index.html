Sea buckthorn juice (duindoornsap)

Sea buckthorn juice (duindoornsap) is made with wild sea buckthorn, a favourite in Coast, dunes and Wadden.

How do you make sea buckthorn juice (duindoornsap)?

Tart orange berries from the dunes, juiced and sweetened.

Wild ingredients

  • Sea buckthorn (Hippophae rhamnoides): in season August to October
