Mulberry sharbat

Mulberry sharbat is made with wild mulberry, a favourite in Northern plains and Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make mulberry sharbat?

Mash ripe mulberries, strain, cook with sugar into a syrup, and dilute with chilled water and lime.

Wild ingredients

  • Mulberry (Morus alba): in season March to May
