Wild mango

Mangifera indica
Also called Aam, Appe midi, Kaadu maavu

Wild mango is a wild fruit you can forage from April to June in South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests and Northeast. Old trees on village edges and in forests. Small, fibrous, very aromatic fruit.

When is wild mango in season in India?

Wild mango is usually in season April to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild mango grow in India?

Wild mango grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests, Northeast.

How do you recognise wild mango?

Old trees on village edges and in forests. Small, fibrous, very aromatic fruit.

How do you harvest wild mango?

Use a pole with a net (a mango picker) so the fruit doesn't bruise. Ask the owner.

How do you eat wild mango?

Ripe fresh, raw in pickles, panna, appe midi uppinakai.

How do you store wild mango?

Aam papad (fruit leather), amchur, brined whole raw mangoes.

What is wild mango good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Calories.

What can you cook with wild mango?

  • Aam panna: Boil or roast raw mangoes, scoop out the pulp and blend it with jaggery, roasted cumin, black salt and mint. Dilute with cold water.
  • Appe midi uppinakai: Whole tender wild mangoes brined with mustard, chilli and turmeric. Malnad's prized pickle.
  • Ugadi pachadi: Neem flowers, raw mango, tamarind, jaggery, salt and chilli: six tastes in one spoonful for the new year.
  • Aam papad: Mango pulp and jaggery sun-dried in thin layers into fruit leather.
