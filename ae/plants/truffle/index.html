Desert truffle

Terfezia and Tirmania species
Also called Faqa', Kama, Fagaa

Desert truffle is a wild mushroom you can forage from January to April in Desert, dunes and gravel plains and Hajar Mountains and wadis. Potato-like lumps a few centimetres under the sand near the small yellow-flowered rock rose (Helianthemum). Look for cracked, swollen mounds about two weeks after heavy winter rain and thunder.

Caution. Only eat firm truffles you've had confirmed by an experienced hunter, and always cook them.

When is desert truffle in season in the UAE?

Desert truffle is usually in season January to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does desert truffle grow in the UAE?

Desert truffle grows across the UAE. Regions: Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Hajar Mountains and wadis.

How do you recognise desert truffle?

Potato-like lumps a few centimetres under the sand near the small yellow-flowered rock rose (Helianthemum). Look for cracked, swollen mounds about two weeks after heavy winter rain and thunder.

How do you harvest desert truffle?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat desert truffle?

Peel and wash well; stew in saloona, or grill with olive oil.

How do you store desert truffle?

Keep cool in a basket, never plastic; freezes well.

What is desert truffle good for?

Protein, Calories.

What can you cook with desert truffle?

  • Grilled desert truffles: Peeled faqa' sliced, brushed with olive oil and grilled, finished with salt and coriander.
  • Truffle saloona: Emirati tomato-based meat stew with chunks of desert truffle.
