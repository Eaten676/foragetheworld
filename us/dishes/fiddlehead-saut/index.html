Fiddlehead sauté

Fiddlehead sauté is made with wild ostrich fern fiddleheads, a favourite in Northeast and Alaska.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make fiddlehead sauté?

Boiled fiddleheads sautéed in butter with garlic and lemon.

Note. Boil 15 minutes first and discard the water.

Wild ingredients

  • Ostrich fern fiddleheads (Matteuccia struthiopteris): in season April to May
