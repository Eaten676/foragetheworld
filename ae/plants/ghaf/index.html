Ghaf leaves and pods

Prosopis cineraria
Also called Ghaf, Khejri

Ghaf leaves and pods is a wild green you can forage from March to June in Desert, dunes and gravel plains and Cities, farms and oases. The UAE's national tree: a round, droopy, grey-green tree of sand plains with tiny paired leaflets and long, thin pods that ripen yellow to reddish brown.

Caution. Protected national tree: never break branches. Only take a few leaves or pods by hand from trees on your own or permitted land.
