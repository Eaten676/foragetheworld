Wattleseed

Acacia victoriae
Also called Wattleseed, Elegant wattle

Wattleseed is a wild seed or grain you can forage from November to January in Arid centre, Temperate south-east and South-west. Spiny wattle shrub with yellow puffball flowers and flat pods; roasted seed tastes of coffee and hazelnut.

Caution. Only some wattles are edible; learn the exact species.

When is wattleseed in season in Australia?

Wattleseed is usually in season November to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wattleseed grow in Australia?

Wattleseed grows across Australia. Regions: Arid centre, Temperate south-east, South-west.

How do you recognise wattleseed?

Spiny wattle shrub with yellow puffball flowers and flat pods; roasted seed tastes of coffee and hazelnut.

How do you harvest wattleseed?

Shake or strip ripe seed heads into a bag when they rattle and fall easily. Winnow out the chaff in a breeze.

How do you eat wattleseed?

Roasted and ground in damper, ice cream, coffee.

How do you store wattleseed?

Roast, grind and store dry.

What is wattleseed good for?

Protein, Calories.

What can you cook with wattleseed?

  • Wattleseed damper: Campfire bread made with roasted ground wattleseed.
  • Macadamia and wattleseed ice cream: Coffee-like wattleseed custard with toasted macadamias.
