Silverweed

Argentina anserina
Also called Silverweed, Potentille ansérine

Silverweed is a root or shoot you can forage from September to November across Canada. Creeping plant of wet sands and lake shores with feathery leaves that are silver underneath and single yellow flowers.

When is silverweed in season in Canada?

Silverweed is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does silverweed grow in Canada?

Silverweed grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise silverweed?

Creeping plant of wet sands and lake shores with feathery leaves that are silver underneath and single yellow flowers.

How do you harvest silverweed?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat silverweed?

Roots roasted or boiled; taste like parsnip or sweet potato.

How do you store silverweed?

Store roots.
