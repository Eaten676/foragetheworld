Saltbush lamb

Saltbush lamb is made with wild old man saltbush, a favourite in Arid centre and Temperate south-east.

Open in Forage AustraliaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make saltbush lamb?

Lamb rubbed with dried saltbush and pepperberry, roasted.

Wild ingredients

  • Old man saltbush (Atriplex nummularia): in season all year round
