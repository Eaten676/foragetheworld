Dulse

Palmaria palmata
Also called Søl

Dulse is a wild green you can forage from April to September in Jutland. Leathery, dark red seaweed fronds on rocks and kelp stems at low tide.

Caution. Cut from attached plants with scissors, leaving the base; avoid polluted shores.

When is dulse in season in Denmark?

Dulse is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does dulse grow in Denmark?

Dulse grows in Central Jutland (Midtjylland), North Jutland (Nordjylland), Southern Denmark (Syddanmark). Regions: Jutland.

How do you recognise dulse?

Leathery, dark red seaweed fronds on rocks and kelp stems at low tide.

How do you harvest dulse?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat dulse?

Dried as a snack, crisped in a pan, in soups.

How do you store dulse?

Dry.

What is dulse good for?

Iron & calcium, Protein.
