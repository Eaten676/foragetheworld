Fat hen

Chenopodium album
Also called Melganzenvoet

Fat hen is a wild green you can forage from May to September across the Netherlands. Goose-foot leaves with a white mealy bloom on young tips; weed of gardens and fields.

Caution. Contains oxalates; cook and eat in moderation.

When is fat hen in season in the Netherlands?

Fat hen is usually in season May to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does fat hen grow in the Netherlands?

Fat hen grows across the Netherlands. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden, West, East, South.

How do you recognise fat hen?

Goose-foot leaves with a white mealy bloom on young tips; weed of gardens and fields.

How do you harvest fat hen?

Pick the top 10–15 cm of young plants; strip seeds in autumn by running a hand up the dry spikes.

How do you eat fat hen?

Cook like spinach.

How do you store fat hen?

Blanch and freeze.

What is fat hen good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.
