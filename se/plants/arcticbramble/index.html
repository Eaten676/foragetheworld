Arctic bramble

Rubus arcticus
Also called Åkerbär

Arctic bramble is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August in Norrland (north) and Svealand (middle). Tiny, thornless northern plant with pink flowers and dark red, intensely fragrant berries.

When is arctic bramble in season in Sweden?

Arctic bramble is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does arctic bramble grow in Sweden?

Arctic bramble grows in Dalarna, Gävleborg, Jämtland, Norrbotten, Stockholm, Södermanland, Uppsala, Värmland, Västerbotten, Västernorrland, Västmanland, Örebro. Regions: Norrland (north), Svealand (middle).

How do you recognise arctic bramble?

Tiny, thornless northern plant with pink flowers and dark red, intensely fragrant berries.

How do you harvest arctic bramble?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat arctic bramble?

Fresh, liqueur, cream.

How do you store arctic bramble?

Freeze.

What is arctic bramble good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with arctic bramble?

  • Arctic bramble liqueur cream: Åkerbär folded into cream with a splash of liqueur.
