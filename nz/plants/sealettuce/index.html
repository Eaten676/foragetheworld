Sea lettuce

Ulva
Also called Sea lettuce

Sea lettuce is a wild green you can forage from September to April across New Zealand. Bright green, thin seaweed sheets in rock pools.

Caution. Only from clean coasts; respect rāhui.

When is sea lettuce in season in New Zealand?

Sea lettuce is usually in season September to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea lettuce grow in New Zealand?

Sea lettuce grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise sea lettuce?

Bright green, thin seaweed sheets in rock pools.

How do you harvest sea lettuce?

Pick fresh green sheets in rock pools, leaving the holdfast.

How do you eat sea lettuce?

Dried and crumbled, in fritters.

How do you store sea lettuce?

Dry.

What is sea lettuce good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.
