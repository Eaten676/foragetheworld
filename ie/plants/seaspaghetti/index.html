Sea spaghetti

Himanthalia elongata
Also called Sea spaghetti, Thongweed, Ríseach

Sea spaghetti is a wild green you can forage from April to July in Connacht, Munster and Ulster (Republic). Long olive-brown straps growing from little button bases on the lower shore.

Caution. Cut above the button at spring low tides.

When is sea spaghetti in season in Ireland?

Sea spaghetti is usually in season April to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea spaghetti grow in Ireland?

Sea spaghetti grows in Cavan, Clare, Cork, Donegal, Galway, Kerry, Leitrim, Limerick, Mayo, Monaghan, Roscommon, Sligo, Tipperary, Waterford. Regions: Connacht, Munster, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise sea spaghetti?

Long olive-brown straps growing from little button bases on the lower shore.

How do you harvest sea spaghetti?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat sea spaghetti?

Blanched as 'pasta', in salads, or dried.

How do you store sea spaghetti?

Dry.

What is sea spaghetti good for?

Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with sea spaghetti?

  • Sea spaghetti carbonara: Blanched sea spaghetti tossed with bacon, egg and cheese.
