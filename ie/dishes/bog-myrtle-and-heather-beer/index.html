Bog myrtle and heather beer

Bog myrtle and heather beer is made with wild bog myrtle, heather flowers, a favourite in Connacht and Ulster (Republic).

Open in Forage IrelandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bog myrtle and heather beer?

Old-style gruit ale brewed with bog myrtle and heather instead of hops.

Wild ingredients

  • Bog myrtle (Myrica gale): in season June to September
  • Heather flowers (Calluna vulgaris): in season August to September
