Green amaranth

Amaranthus viridis
Also called Amaranth

Green amaranth is a wild green you can forage from April to November in Cities, farms and oases. Upright weed of gardens and farms with oval, notched-tip leaves and slender green flower spikes, thriving in the summer heat with irrigation.

When is green amaranth in season in the UAE?

Green amaranth is usually in season April to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does green amaranth grow in the UAE?

Green amaranth grows in Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Al Ain, Dubai, Fujairah, Hatta, Ras Al Khaimah, Sharjah. Regions: Cities, farms and oases.

How do you recognise green amaranth?

Upright weed of gardens and farms with oval, notched-tip leaves and slender green flower spikes, thriving in the summer heat with irrigation.

How do you harvest green amaranth?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.

How do you eat green amaranth?

Young leaves cooked like spinach, in stews and with dal.

How do you store green amaranth?

Blanch and freeze.

What is green amaranth good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with green amaranth?

  • Wild greens with eggs: Wilted wild greens cooked with onion and spices, then eggs scrambled in.
