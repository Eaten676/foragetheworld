Nettle soup

Nettle soup is made with wild stinging nettle.

Open in Forage IrelandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nettle soup?

Nettle tops simmered with potato, onion and stock, blended with cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season March to June
