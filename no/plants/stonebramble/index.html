Stone bramble

Rubus saxatilis
Also called Teiebær

Stone bramble is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across Norway. Low, weakly prickly plant of rocky woods; a few glossy red drupelets per berry.

When is stone bramble in season in Norway?

Stone bramble is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does stone bramble grow in Norway?

Stone bramble grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise stone bramble?

Low, weakly prickly plant of rocky woods; a few glossy red drupelets per berry.

How do you harvest stone bramble?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat stone bramble?

Fresh, jelly.

How do you store stone bramble?

Freeze.

What is stone bramble good for?

Vitamin C.
