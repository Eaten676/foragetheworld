Lotus stem & seeds

Nelumbo nucifera
Also called Kamal kakdi, Nadru, Bhein, Makhana

Lotus stem & seeds is a root or shoot you can forage from October to February in East, Northern plains and Himalaya. Kashmir's lakes and the eastern plains' ponds. Stems are starchy, and the seeds carry protein.

Caution. Only from clean water. Cook thoroughly.

When is lotus stem & seeds in season in India?

Lotus stem & seeds is usually in season October to February. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lotus stem & seeds grow in India?

Lotus stem & seeds grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Delhi, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Ladakh, Odisha, Punjab, Sikkim, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: East, Northern plains, Himalaya.

How do you recognise lotus stem & seeds?

Kashmir's lakes and the eastern plains' ponds. Stems are starchy, and the seeds carry protein.

How do you harvest lotus stem & seeds?

Stems are pulled from pond mud by skilled harvesters. Buy unless you know the pond is clean.

How do you eat lotus stem & seeds?

Nadru yakhni, chips, fresh lotus seeds.

How do you store lotus stem & seeds?

Dry seeds keep for months. Pop them like makhana.

What is lotus stem & seeds good for?

Calories, Protein.

What can you cook with lotus stem & seeds?

  • Kamal kakdi chips: Thinly sliced lotus stem, fried crisp with chilli and salt.
  • Nadru yakhni: Lotus stem in a fennel and dry-ginger yoghurt gravy. Kashmiri.
