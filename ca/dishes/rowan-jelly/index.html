Rowan jelly

Rowan jelly is made with wild rowan berries, a favourite in Atlantic Canada and Quebec.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make rowan jelly?

Frost-sweetened mountain ash berries cooked with apples and strained.

Wild ingredients

  • Rowan berries (Sorbus americana / S. aucuparia): in season August to November
