Kathal biryani

Kathal biryani is made with wild jackfruit, a favourite in Northern plains and East.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kathal biryani?

Raw jackfruit chunks layered with rice and whole spices. It eats like meat.

Wild ingredients

  • Jackfruit (Artocarpus heterophyllus): in season March to July
