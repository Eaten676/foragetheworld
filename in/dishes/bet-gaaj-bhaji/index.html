Bet gaaj bhaji

Bet gaaj bhaji is made with wild cane shoots, a favourite in Northeast.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bet gaaj bhaji?

Bitter cane shoot core stir-fried with potato and mustard oil.

Wild ingredients

  • Cane shoots (Calamus tenuis): in season July to September
