Barramundi in native ginger leaf

Barramundi in native ginger leaf is made with wild native ginger, lemon myrtle, a favourite in Tropical north.

Open in Forage AustraliaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make barramundi in native ginger leaf?

Fish wrapped in native ginger leaves with lemon myrtle and grilled.

Wild ingredients

  • Native ginger (Alpinia caerulea): in season January to May
  • Lemon myrtle (Backhousia citriodora): in season all year round
