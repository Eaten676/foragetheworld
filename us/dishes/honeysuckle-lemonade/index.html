Honeysuckle lemonade

Honeysuckle lemonade is made with wild Japanese honeysuckle flowers, a favourite in Southeast.

How do you make honeysuckle lemonade?

Honeysuckle flower syrup stirred into fresh lemonade.

Wild ingredients

  • Japanese honeysuckle flowers (Lonicera japonica): in season May to July
