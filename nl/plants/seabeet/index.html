Sea beet

Beta vulgaris subsp. maritima
Also called Strandbiet

Sea beet is a wild green you can forage from March to October in Coast, dunes and Wadden. Glossy, leathery, dark green leaves on shingle beaches, sea walls and salt marsh edges. Wild ancestor of chard.

When is sea beet in season in the Netherlands?

Sea beet is usually in season March to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea beet grow in the Netherlands?

Sea beet grows in Friesland, Groningen, North Holland, South Holland, Zeeland. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden.

How do you recognise sea beet?

Glossy, leathery, dark green leaves on shingle beaches, sea walls and salt marsh edges. Wild ancestor of chard.

How do you harvest sea beet?

Pick the younger leaves from the centre; leave the plant and its root in place.

How do you eat sea beet?

Wilt like spinach with butter.

How do you store sea beet?

Blanch and freeze.

What is sea beet good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with sea beet?

  • Sea beet stamppot: Mashed potatoes stirred with wilted sea beet and smoked sausage.
