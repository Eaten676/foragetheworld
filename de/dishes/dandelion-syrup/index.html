Dandelion syrup (Löwenzahnhonig)

Dandelion syrup (Löwenzahnhonig) is made with wild dandelion.

Open in Forage GermanyShare on WhatsApp

How do you make dandelion syrup (löwenzahnhonig)?

Flower heads simmered with lemon and sugar into a vegan 'honey'.

Wild ingredients

  • Dandelion (Taraxacum officinale): in season March to October
