Chickweed

Stellaria media
Also called Chickweed

Chickweed is a wild green you can forage from April to November across New Zealand. Soft sprawling mat with tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

When is chickweed in season in New Zealand?

Chickweed is usually in season April to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chickweed grow in New Zealand?

Chickweed grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise chickweed?

Soft sprawling mat with tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

How do you harvest chickweed?

Snip the soft top few centimetres with scissors, leaving the roots so it regrows within weeks.

How do you eat chickweed?

Raw in salads and sandwiches.

How do you store chickweed?

Best fresh.

What is chickweed good for?

Vitamin C.
