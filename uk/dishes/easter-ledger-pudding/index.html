Easter-ledger pudding

Easter-ledger pudding is made with wild bistort, stinging nettle, a favourite in Northern England.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make easter-ledger pudding?

Cumbrian pudding of bistort, nettles, oats and egg, served with bacon.

Wild ingredients

  • Bistort (Bistorta officinalis): in season March to May
  • Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica): in season March to June
