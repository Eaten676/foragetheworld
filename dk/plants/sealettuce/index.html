Sea lettuce

Ulva lactuca
Also called Søsalat

Sea lettuce is a wild green you can forage from April to September across Denmark. Bright green, thin translucent sheets of seaweed in rock pools and on the lower shore.

Caution. Only from clean shores; avoid near outfalls.

When is sea lettuce in season in Denmark?

Sea lettuce is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sea lettuce grow in Denmark?

Sea lettuce grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise sea lettuce?

Bright green, thin translucent sheets of seaweed in rock pools and on the lower shore.

How do you harvest sea lettuce?

Pick fresh green sheets in rock pools, leaving the holdfast.

How do you eat sea lettuce?

Dried and crumbled, fried crisp, in soups.

How do you store sea lettuce?

Dry.

What is sea lettuce good for?

Iron & calcium, Vitamin A.
