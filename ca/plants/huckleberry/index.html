Huckleberry

Vaccinium membranaceum
Also called Black huckleberry, Airelle

Huckleberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to September in British Columbia. Mountain shrub with thin oval leaves and dark purple berries, often on old burns and ski slopes.

When is huckleberry in season in Canada?

Huckleberry is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does huckleberry grow in Canada?

Huckleberry grows in British Columbia. Regions: British Columbia.

How do you recognise huckleberry?

Mountain shrub with thin oval leaves and dark purple berries, often on old burns and ski slopes.

How do you harvest huckleberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat huckleberry?

Fresh, pie, jam, pancakes.

How do you store huckleberry?

Freeze.

What is huckleberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with huckleberry?

  • Huckleberry crumble: Mountain huckleberries under an oat crumble.
