Hazelnut

Corylus avellana
Also called Haselnuss, Noisette, Nocciola

Hazelnut is a wild nut you can forage from August to October in Swiss Plateau, Jura and Ticino and southern valleys. Multi-stemmed shrub with round, toothed leaves; nuts in frilly husks, ripe when they turn brown.

When is hazelnut in season in Switzerland?

Hazelnut is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does hazelnut grow in Switzerland?

Hazelnut grows in Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. Regions: Swiss Plateau, Jura, Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you recognise hazelnut?

Multi-stemmed shrub with round, toothed leaves; nuts in frilly husks, ripe when they turn brown.

How do you harvest hazelnut?

Pick nuts as husks turn brown and the nut slips out, before squirrels get them; or gather fallen nuts.

How do you eat hazelnut?

Raw, roasted, in baking.

How do you store hazelnut?

Dry in the shell.

What is hazelnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.
