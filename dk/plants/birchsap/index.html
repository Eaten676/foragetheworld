Birch sap

Betula pendula
Also called Birkesaft

Birch sap is a sap or starch you can forage from March to April across Denmark. White-barked birch; for a few weeks before the leaves open, sap rises and drips from a small tap hole.

Caution. Only tap with the landowner's permission, use a small hole, and plug it afterwards.

When is birch sap in season in Denmark?

Birch sap is usually in season March to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does birch sap grow in Denmark?

Birch sap grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise birch sap?

White-barked birch; for a few weeks before the leaves open, sap rises and drips from a small tap hole.

How do you harvest birch sap?

Drill a small hole a few centimetres deep, collect for a few days only, then plug the hole with a twig.

How do you eat birch sap?

Drink fresh, ferment lightly, or boil down to syrup.

How do you store birch sap?

Freeze sap; it spoils in days.

What is birch sap good for?

Calories.
