Kānuka tea

Kunzea robusta
Also called Kānuka tea

Kānuka tea is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round across New Zealand. Tall, soft-leaved tea tree with tiny, fragrant white flowers; softer to touch than prickly mānuka.

When is kānuka tea in season in New Zealand?

Kānuka tea is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kānuka tea grow in New Zealand?

Kānuka tea grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise kānuka tea?

Tall, soft-leaved tea tree with tiny, fragrant white flowers; softer to touch than prickly mānuka.

How do you harvest kānuka tea?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat kānuka tea?

Leaves and flowers steeped as tea.

How do you store kānuka tea?

Dry.

What can you cook with kānuka tea?

  • Kānuka tea: Fresh kānuka leaves steeped in hot water.
