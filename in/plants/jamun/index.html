Jamun

Syzygium cumini
Also called Jamun, Nerale, Naaval, Kalojam

Jamun is a wild fruit you can forage from May to July in South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains and Northeast. Tall avenue tree dropping deep purple oval fruit in early monsoon.

When is jamun in season in India?

Jamun is usually in season May to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does jamun grow in India?

Jamun grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, Central forests, East, Northern plains, Northeast.

How do you recognise jamun?

Tall avenue tree dropping deep purple oval fruit in early monsoon.

How do you harvest jamun?

Spread a sheet under the tree and gently shake the branches. Ripe fruit falls easily.

How do you eat jamun?

Fresh with salt, sorbet, jamun vinegar.

How do you store jamun?

Short-lived fresh. Dry the seeds for powder, or make syrup or vinegar.

What can be confused with jamun?

Lantana (Lantana camara), rated toxic. Lantana berries are small, glossy and black, in tight round clusters on a scrambling shrub. Karonda and jamun fruits are larger. Lantana leaves are rough, toothed and strong-smelling, and its flowers come in pink, yellow and orange clusters. Unripe green lantana berries are the most poisonous, especially for children.

What is jamun good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with jamun?

  • Jamun juice: Deseed ripe jamun, blend with water, lime, black salt and a little sugar. Strain and drink chilled.
  • Jamun sorbet: Jamun pulp blended with sugar, lime and black salt, then frozen.
