Jambu (Himalayan wild onion)

Allium stracheyi
Also called Jambu, Jimbu, Faran

Jambu (Himalayan wild onion) is a root or shoot you can forage from June to August in Himalaya. Grass-like Himalayan onion leaves, dried and fried in ghee as a tempering for dal.

Caution. Threatened in the wild. Buy it from village growers rather than picking.

When is jambu (himalayan wild onion) in season in India?

Jambu (Himalayan wild onion) is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does jambu (himalayan wild onion) grow in India?

Jambu (Himalayan wild onion) grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise jambu (himalayan wild onion)?

Grass-like Himalayan onion leaves, dried and fried in ghee as a tempering for dal.

How do you harvest jambu (himalayan wild onion)?

Buy from village growers. The wild plant is threatened.

How do you eat jambu (himalayan wild onion)?

Tempering for dal, aloo and raita.

How do you store jambu (himalayan wild onion)?

Sun-dry the leaves. Keeps a year.

What can you cook with jambu (himalayan wild onion)?

  • Jambu tadka dal: Dal finished with jambu leaves sizzled in ghee. The Garhwali signature.
