Curly dock

Rumex crispus
Also called Curly dock, Patience crépue

Curly dock is a wild green you can forage from April to July across Canada. Long, narrow leaves with wavy, curled edges; tall rust-brown seed stalks in summer.

Caution. High in oxalates; cook and eat in moderation.

When is curly dock in season in Canada?

Curly dock is usually in season April to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does curly dock grow in Canada?

Curly dock grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise curly dock?

Long, narrow leaves with wavy, curled edges; tall rust-brown seed stalks in summer.

How do you harvest curly dock?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat curly dock?

Young leaves cooked like spinach; lemony.

How do you store curly dock?

Blanch and freeze.

What is curly dock good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.
