Pineapple weed

Matricaria discoidea
Also called Pineapple weed, Wild chamomile

Pineapple weed is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to September across the United States. Low plant of paths and driveways with feathery leaves and green-yellow cone flowers without petals; smells of pineapple.

Caution. Avoid if allergic to ragweed or chamomile.

When is pineapple weed in season in the United States?

Pineapple weed is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does pineapple weed grow in the United States?

Pineapple weed grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise pineapple weed?

Low plant of paths and driveways with feathery leaves and green-yellow cone flowers without petals; smells of pineapple.

How do you harvest pineapple weed?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat pineapple weed?

Flower heads as a chamomile-like tea, or raw in salads.

How do you store pineapple weed?

Dry flower heads.

What can you cook with pineapple weed?

  • Pineapple weed tea: Fresh or dried flower heads steeped like chamomile, with honey.
