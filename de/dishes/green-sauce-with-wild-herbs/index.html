Green sauce (Grüne Soße) with wild herbs

Green sauce (Grüne Soße) with wild herbs is made with wild common sorrel, ground elder, chickweed, watercress, a favourite in West.

How do you make green sauce (grüne soße) with wild herbs?

Frankfurt-style herb sauce of seven herbs with eggs and potatoes.

Wild ingredients

  • Common sorrel (Rumex acetosa): in season April to August
  • Ground elder (Aegopodium podagraria): in season March to June
  • Chickweed (Stellaria media): in season March to November
  • Watercress (Nasturtium officinale): in season March to October
