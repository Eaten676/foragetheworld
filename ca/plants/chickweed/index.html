Chickweed

Stellaria media
Also called Chickweed, Mouron des oiseaux

Chickweed is a wild green you can forage from April to December across Canada. Sprawling soft mat in lawns and gardens; tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

When is chickweed in season in Canada?

Chickweed is usually in season April to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chickweed grow in Canada?

Chickweed grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise chickweed?

Sprawling soft mat in lawns and gardens; tiny white star flowers and a single line of hairs down the stem.

How do you harvest chickweed?

Snip the soft top few centimetres with scissors, leaving the roots so it regrows within weeks.

How do you eat chickweed?

Raw in salads and sandwiches like a mild sprout.

How do you store chickweed?

Best fresh; keeps a few days wrapped in the fridge.

What can be confused with chickweed?

Scarlet pimpernel (Lysimachia arvensis), rated harmful. Chickweed has a single line of hairs down its stem and white flowers. Scarlet pimpernel has square stems, black dots under the leaves and orange flowers.

What is chickweed good for?

Vitamin C.
