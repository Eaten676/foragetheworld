Wild nori furikake

Wild nori furikake is made with wild laver (wild nori), a favourite in Pacific Northwest and California.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make wild nori furikake?

Toasted, crumbled wild nori mixed with sesame and salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Laver (wild nori) (Porphyra / Pyropia spp.): in season March to June
