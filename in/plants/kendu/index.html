Kendu

Diospyros melanoxylon
Also called Tendu, Kendu, Beedi patta

Kendu is a wild fruit you can forage from April to June in Central forests, East and West coast and Deccan. The beedi-leaf tree of central India's forests. Yellowish fruit with sweet, sticky pulp.

When is kendu in season in India?

Kendu is usually in season April to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kendu grow in India?

Kendu grows in Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: Central forests, East, West coast and Deccan.

How do you recognise kendu?

The beedi-leaf tree of central India's forests. Yellowish fruit with sweet, sticky pulp.

How do you harvest kendu?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat kendu?

Ripe fruit fresh.

How do you store kendu?

Short-lived. Eat fresh.

What is kendu good for?

Calories, Vitamin A.
