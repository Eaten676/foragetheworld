Witchetty grubs on coals

Witchetty grubs on coals is made with wild witchetty grub, a favourite in Arid centre.

How do you make witchetty grubs on coals?

Grubs cooked briefly on hot coals until golden.

Wild ingredients

  • Witchetty grub (Endoxyla leucomochla): in season all year round
