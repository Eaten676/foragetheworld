Bhagar / nivara khichdi

Bhagar / nivara khichdi is made with wild rice, jungle rice.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bhagar / nivara khichdi?

Wild rice or jungle rice cooked with peanuts, cumin, green chilli and ghee. The classic fasting meal.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild rice (Oryza rufipogon): in season October to December
  • Jungle rice (Echinochloa colona): in season September to November
