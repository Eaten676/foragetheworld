Seaweed chowder

Seaweed chowder is made with wild sea lettuce, dulse, blue mussels, a favourite in Connacht and Munster.

How do you make seaweed chowder?

Creamy West Coast chowder with mussels and seaweed.

Wild ingredients

  • Sea lettuce (Ulva lactuca): in season April to September
  • Dulse (Palmaria palmata): in season April to September
  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
