Green-lipped mussels

Perna canaliculus
Also called Green-lipped mussels

Green-lipped mussels is a wild protein you can forage all year round across New Zealand. Large mussels with green-edged shells on rocky coasts.

Caution. Check toxic shellfish warnings and MPI limits.

When is green-lipped mussels in season in New Zealand?

Green-lipped mussels is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does green-lipped mussels grow in New Zealand?

Green-lipped mussels grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise green-lipped mussels?

Large mussels with green-edged shells on rocky coasts.

How do you harvest green-lipped mussels?

Twist mussels off rocks at low tide, taking only large ones, and check local toxin warnings first.

How do you eat green-lipped mussels?

Steamed, in chowder or fritters.

How do you store green-lipped mussels?

Eat the same day.

What is green-lipped mussels good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with green-lipped mussels?

  • Green-lipped mussel chowder: Creamy chowder with green-lipped mussels and native celery.
