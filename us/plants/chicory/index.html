Chicory

Cichorium intybus
Also called Chicory, Blue sailors

Chicory is a root or shoot you can forage from March to November across the United States. Roadside plant with sky-blue flowers on stiff stems; dandelion-like basal leaves.

When is chicory in season in the United States?

Chicory is usually in season March to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chicory grow in the United States?

Chicory grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise chicory?

Roadside plant with sky-blue flowers on stiff stems; dandelion-like basal leaves.

How do you harvest chicory?

Only dig roots where you have the landowner's permission, since uprooting wild plants is illegal in many places. Take a few from a large patch and backfill the hole.

How do you eat chicory?

Young leaves as bitter greens; roasted root as coffee.

How do you store chicory?

Dry roasted root.

What is chicory good for?

Vitamin A.
