Native raspberry

Rubus rosifolius
Also called Rose-leaf bramble, Native raspberry

Native raspberry is a wild fruit you can forage from September to December in Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east and Tropical north. Prickly rainforest-edge shrub with soft leaves and bright red, mild raspberries.

When is native raspberry in season in Australia?

Native raspberry is usually in season September to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does native raspberry grow in Australia?

Native raspberry grows across Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east, Tropical north.

How do you recognise native raspberry?

Prickly rainforest-edge shrub with soft leaves and bright red, mild raspberries.

How do you harvest native raspberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat native raspberry?

Fresh, jam.

How do you store native raspberry?

Freeze.

What is native raspberry good for?

Vitamin C.
