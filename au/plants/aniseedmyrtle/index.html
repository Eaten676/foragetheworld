Aniseed myrtle

Syzygium anisatum
Also called Aniseed myrtle

Aniseed myrtle is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round in Subtropical east coast. Rainforest tree with wavy leaves that smell strongly of aniseed.

When is aniseed myrtle in season in Australia?

Aniseed myrtle is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does aniseed myrtle grow in Australia?

Aniseed myrtle grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise aniseed myrtle?

Rainforest tree with wavy leaves that smell strongly of aniseed.

How do you harvest aniseed myrtle?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat aniseed myrtle?

Tea, baking, with fish.

How do you store aniseed myrtle?

Dry leaves.

What can you cook with aniseed myrtle?

  • Aniseed myrtle tea: Dried leaves steeped as a liquorice-scented tea.
