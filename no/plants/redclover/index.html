Red clover

Trifolium pratense
Also called Rødkløver

Red clover is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to August across Norway. Leaves of three with a pale chevron and round pink-purple flower heads.

When is red clover in season in Norway?

Red clover is usually in season June to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does red clover grow in Norway?

Red clover grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise red clover?

Leaves of three with a pale chevron and round pink-purple flower heads.

How do you harvest red clover?

Pick fresh pink heads in full bloom, avoiding brown ones.

How do you eat red clover?

Tea; flowers on salads.

How do you store red clover?

Dry the heads.

What is red clover good for?

Protein.
