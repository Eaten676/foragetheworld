Wild marjoram

Origanum vulgare
Also called Kungsmynta

Wild marjoram is a edible flower or tea you can forage from July to August in Götaland (south) and Svealand (middle). Bushy aromatic herb on dry chalky slopes with purple-pink flower clusters; the wild oregano.

When is wild marjoram in season in Sweden?

Wild marjoram is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild marjoram grow in Sweden?

Wild marjoram grows in Blekinge, Dalarna, Gotland, Halland, Jönköping, Kalmar, Kronoberg, Skåne, Stockholm, Södermanland, Uppsala, Värmland, Västmanland, Västra Götaland, Örebro, Östergötland. Regions: Götaland (south), Svealand (middle).

How do you recognise wild marjoram?

Bushy aromatic herb on dry chalky slopes with purple-pink flower clusters; the wild oregano.

How do you harvest wild marjoram?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat wild marjoram?

Herb for pizza, tomatoes, lamb; tea.

How do you store wild marjoram?

Dry.
