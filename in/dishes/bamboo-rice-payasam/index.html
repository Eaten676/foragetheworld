Bamboo rice payasam

Bamboo rice payasam is made with wild bamboo rice, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make bamboo rice payasam?

Bamboo rice simmered in coconut milk and jaggery with cardamom.

Wild ingredients

  • Bamboo rice (Bambusa bambos): in season March to May
