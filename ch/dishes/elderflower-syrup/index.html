Elderflower syrup (Holunderblütensirup)

Elderflower syrup (Holunderblütensirup) is made with wild elderflower, a favourite in Swiss Plateau and Jura.

Open in Forage SwitzerlandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower syrup (holunderblütensirup)?

Flower heads steeped with lemon for two days, strained and boiled with sugar. Topped up with water or sparkling water.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
