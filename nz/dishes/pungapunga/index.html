Pungapunga (raupō pollen cake)

Pungapunga (raupō pollen cake) is made with wild raupō.

Open in Forage New ZealandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make pungapunga (raupō pollen cake)?

Golden raupō pollen mixed with water and steamed or baked.

Wild ingredients

  • Raupō (Typha orientalis): in season November to January
