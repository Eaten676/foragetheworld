Iceland moss

Cetraria islandica
Also called Iceland moss, Lichen d'Islande

Iceland moss is a wild green you can forage all year round in The North, Quebec and Atlantic Canada. Brown-green, curly lichen on heaths and mountain tundra (not a moss).

Caution. Bitter and mildly toxic raw: always soak and boil in changed water. Lichens absorb pollution, so gather far from roads.

When is iceland moss in season in Canada?

Iceland moss is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does iceland moss grow in Canada?

Iceland moss grows in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Northwest Territories, Nova Scotia, Nunavut, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Yukon. Regions: The North, Quebec, Atlantic Canada.

How do you recognise iceland moss?

Brown-green, curly lichen on heaths and mountain tundra (not a moss).

How do you harvest iceland moss?

Gather small amounts by hand from clean upland areas, leaving most of the patch.

How do you eat iceland moss?

Soaked and boiled in changes of water, then used in porridge, bread or as a soup thickener.

How do you store iceland moss?

Dry.
