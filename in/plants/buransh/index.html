Buransh

Rhododendron arboreum
Also called Buransh, Lali gurans

Buransh is a edible flower or tea you can forage from February to April in Himalaya and Northeast. State tree of Uttarakhand, covered in red blossoms in spring.

Caution. Only the red tree species. Other rhododendrons, especially high-altitude shrubs, are toxic.

When is buransh in season in India?

Buransh is usually in season February to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does buransh grow in India?

Buransh grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya, Northeast.

How do you recognise buransh?

State tree of Uttarakhand, covered in red blossoms in spring.

How do you harvest buransh?

Gather fresh fallen flowers, or pick a few within reach. Never climb the trees.

How do you eat buransh?

Petals in juice, chutney and squash.

How do you store buransh?

Make squash or dry the petals.

What is buransh good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with buransh?

  • Buransh squash: Simmer red buransh petals in water, strain, then cook with sugar and lemon into a squash. Dilute to drink.
