Paddy snails & small fish

Pila globosa
Also called Gugli, Shamuk, Nathai

Paddy snails & small fish is a wild protein you can forage from July to November in East, Northeast, South India and Central forests. A traditional protein in flooded rice fields across the east and northeast.

Caution. Only from clean, pesticide-free fields. Boil thoroughly, since snails can carry parasites.

When is paddy snails & small fish in season in India?

Paddy snails & small fish is usually in season July to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does paddy snails & small fish grow in India?

Paddy snails & small fish grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Goa, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal. Regions: East, Northeast, South India, Central forests.

How do you recognise paddy snails & small fish?

A traditional protein in flooded rice fields across the east and northeast.

How do you harvest paddy snails & small fish?

Pick them by hand from pesticide-free flooded fields. Leave the small ones.

How do you eat paddy snails & small fish?

Boiled, then curried.

How do you store paddy snails & small fish?

Cook the same day. Small fish can be salted and sun-dried.

What is paddy snails & small fish good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with paddy snails & small fish?

  • Gugli curry: Boiled, cleaned snails in a spicy onion-garlic gravy. Odisha and Bengal.
