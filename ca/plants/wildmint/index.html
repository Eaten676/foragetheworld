Wild mint

Mentha canadensis
Also called Wild mint, Menthe du Canada

Wild mint is a edible flower or tea you can forage from June to September across Canada. Square-stemmed mint of wet meadows and stream banks with pale purple flowers in rings where leaves meet the stem.

Caution. Every mint has a square stem and mint smell; anything without the smell isn't mint.

When is wild mint in season in Canada?

Wild mint is usually in season June to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild mint grow in Canada?

Wild mint grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise wild mint?

Square-stemmed mint of wet meadows and stream banks with pale purple flowers in rings where leaves meet the stem.

How do you harvest wild mint?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat wild mint?

Tea, sauces, salads and drinks.

How do you store wild mint?

Dry.

What can you cook with wild mint?

  • Wild mint tea: Fresh wild mint steeped in hot water.
