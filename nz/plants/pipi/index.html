Pipi and tuatua

Paphies australis
Also called Pipi, Tuatua

Pipi and tuatua is a wild protein you can forage all year round across New Zealand. Smooth, wedge-shaped shellfish buried in sand on surf beaches and harbours.

Caution. Follow MPI daily limits, check toxic shellfish warnings, and respect rāhui.

When is pipi and tuatua in season in New Zealand?

Pipi and tuatua is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does pipi and tuatua grow in New Zealand?

Pipi and tuatua grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise pipi and tuatua?

Smooth, wedge-shaped shellfish buried in sand on surf beaches and harbours.

How do you harvest pipi and tuatua?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat pipi and tuatua?

Steamed, or in fritters.

How do you store pipi and tuatua?

Eat the same day.

What is pipi and tuatua good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with pipi and tuatua?

  • Pipi fritters: Minced pipi in an egg and flour batter, fried golden.
