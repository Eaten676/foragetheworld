Illawarra plum

Podocarpus elatus
Also called Illawarra plum

Illawarra plum is a wild fruit you can forage from March to July in Subtropical east coast. Large rainforest conifer; dark purple, grape-like fleshy stalks with a hard seed attached.

Caution. Eat only the purple fleshy part, never the seed.

When is illawarra plum in season in Australia?

Illawarra plum is usually in season March to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does illawarra plum grow in Australia?

Illawarra plum grows in New South Wales, Queensland. Regions: Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise illawarra plum?

Large rainforest conifer; dark purple, grape-like fleshy stalks with a hard seed attached.

How do you harvest illawarra plum?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat illawarra plum?

Jam, sauces with meat, chutney.

How do you store illawarra plum?

Jam.

What is illawarra plum good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with illawarra plum?

  • Illawarra plum sauce: Plums cooked with ginger and vinegar for duck and pork.
