Nasturtium

Tropaeolum majus
Also called Nasturtium

Nasturtium is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round in Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū and Top of the South. Weedy, round-leaved climber with orange and yellow flowers; peppery.

When is nasturtium in season in New Zealand?

Nasturtium is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does nasturtium grow in New Zealand?

Nasturtium grows in Auckland, Bay of Plenty, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Manawatū-Whanganui, Marlborough, Nelson, Northland, Taranaki, Tasman, Waikato, Wellington. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South.

How do you recognise nasturtium?

Weedy, round-leaved climber with orange and yellow flowers; peppery.

How do you harvest nasturtium?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat nasturtium?

Leaves and flowers in salads; seeds pickled as capers.

How do you store nasturtium?

Pickle seeds.

What is nasturtium good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with nasturtium?

  • Pickled nasturtium capers: Green nasturtium seeds pickled like capers.
