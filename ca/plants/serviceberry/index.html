Serviceberry

Amelanchier
Also called Serviceberry, Amélanchier

Serviceberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July across Canada. Shrub or small tree with white spring blossom and purple berries with a little crown at the tip, like a blueberry.

When is serviceberry in season in Canada?

Serviceberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does serviceberry grow in Canada?

Serviceberry grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise serviceberry?

Shrub or small tree with white spring blossom and purple berries with a little crown at the tip, like a blueberry.

How do you harvest serviceberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat serviceberry?

Fresh, pie, jam, pemmican.

How do you store serviceberry?

Freeze or dry.

What is serviceberry good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with serviceberry?

  • Bannock with saskatoons: Pan bread studded with saskatoon berries.
  • Saskatoon berry pie: The Prairies' signature pie.
