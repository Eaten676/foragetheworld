Tree bean

Parkia timoriana
Also called Yongchak, Zawngtah, Kharial

Tree bean is a wild protein you can forage from November to March in Northeast. Tall tree with long twisted green pods hanging in bunches. Prized in Manipur and Mizoram.

When is tree bean in season in India?

Tree bean is usually in season November to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does tree bean grow in India?

Tree bean grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura. Regions: Northeast.

How do you recognise tree bean?

Tall tree with long twisted green pods hanging in bunches. Prized in Manipur and Mizoram.

How do you harvest tree bean?

Follow local practice and seasonal bans. Take only large adults and leave the young.

How do you eat tree bean?

Young pods in eromba and singju. Seeds cooked.

How do you store tree bean?

Slice and sun-dry the seeds.

What is tree bean good for?

Protein.

What can you cook with tree bean?

  • Singju: Manipuri salad of sliced tree bean, wild herbs and cabbage, dressed with roasted chilli and perilla.
  • Eromba with yongchak: Manipuri mash of boiled tree bean, potato, fermented fish and fiery chilli.
