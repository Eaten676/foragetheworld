Sea rocket and beach greens salad

Sea rocket and beach greens salad is made with wild sea rocket, scurvy grass, sea plantain, a favourite in Jutland.

Open in Forage DenmarkShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sea rocket and beach greens salad?

Peppery beach greens with apple and buttermilk dressing.

Wild ingredients

  • Sea rocket (Cakile maritima): in season June to September
  • Scurvy grass (Cochlearia officinalis): in season March to July
  • Sea plantain (Plantago maritima): in season April to August
