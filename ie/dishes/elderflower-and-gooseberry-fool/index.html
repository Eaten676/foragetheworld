Elderflower and gooseberry fool

Elderflower and gooseberry fool is made with wild gooseberry, elderflower.

Open in Forage IrelandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower and gooseberry fool?

Stewed gooseberries with elderflower folded into whipped cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild gooseberry (Ribes uva-crispa): in season June to July
  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
