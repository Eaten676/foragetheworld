Rosebay willowherb

Chamerion angustifolium
Also called Schmalblättriges Weidenröschen

Rosebay willowherb is a wild green you can forage from April to August across Switzerland. Tall plant of clearings and burns with spikes of magenta flowers; young shoots in spring.

When is rosebay willowherb in season in Switzerland?

Rosebay willowherb is usually in season April to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does rosebay willowherb grow in Switzerland?

Rosebay willowherb grows across Switzerland. Regions: Jura, Swiss Plateau, Alps, Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you recognise rosebay willowherb?

Tall plant of clearings and burns with spikes of magenta flowers; young shoots in spring.

How do you harvest rosebay willowherb?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat rosebay willowherb?

Shoots like asparagus; leaves fermented into Ivan chai tea.

How do you store rosebay willowherb?

Dry fermented leaves.

What is rosebay willowherb good for?

Vitamin A, Vitamin C.
