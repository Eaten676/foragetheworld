Loquat jam

Loquat jam is made with wild loquat, a favourite in Southeast and California.

How do you make loquat jam?

Peeled, seeded loquats cooked into a golden jam.

Wild ingredients

  • Loquat (Eriobotrya japonica): in season March to May
