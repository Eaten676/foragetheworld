Moules marinière

Moules marinière is made with wild blue mussels, a favourite in South West England, Wales and Scotland.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make moules marinière?

Mussels steamed with wine, shallots and parsley.

Wild ingredients

  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
