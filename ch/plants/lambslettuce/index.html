Lamb's lettuce

Valerianella locusta
Also called Feldsalat, Nüsslisalat, Mâche, Doucette, Valerianella

Lamb's lettuce is a wild green you can forage from January to December in Swiss Plateau, Jura and Ticino and southern valleys. Small rosettes of spoon-shaped soft leaves in fields and vineyards in late winter.

When is lamb's lettuce in season in Switzerland?

Lamb's lettuce is usually in season January to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lamb's lettuce grow in Switzerland?

Lamb's lettuce grows in Aargau, Appenzell Ausserrhoden, Basel-Landschaft, Basel-Stadt, Bern, Fribourg, Geneva, Graubünden, Jura, Lucerne, Neuchâtel, Schaffhausen, Schwyz, Solothurn, St. Gallen, Thurgau, Ticino, Vaud, Zug, Zurich. Regions: Swiss Plateau, Jura, Ticino and southern valleys.

How do you recognise lamb's lettuce?

Small rosettes of spoon-shaped soft leaves in fields and vineyards in late winter.

How do you harvest lamb's lettuce?

Cut whole small rosettes at ground level in late winter.

How do you eat lamb's lettuce?

Salad, the Swiss and German Nüsslisalat.

How do you store lamb's lettuce?

Best fresh.

What is lamb's lettuce good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with lamb's lettuce?

  • Lamb's lettuce with egg (Nüsslisalat): Nüsslisalat with chopped egg, bacon and croutons.
