Shapla khoi

Shapla khoi is made with wild water lily seeds.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make shapla khoi?

Water lily seeds popped in a hot pan like tiny popcorn, then salted.

Wild ingredients

  • Water lily seeds (Nymphaea nouchali): in season October to January
