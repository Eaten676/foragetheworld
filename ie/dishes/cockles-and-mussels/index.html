Cockles and mussels

Cockles and mussels is made with wild cockles, blue mussels, a favourite in Leinster and Munster.

Open in Forage IrelandShare on WhatsApp

How do you make cockles and mussels?

Steamed cockles and mussels with cream, garlic and cider, as in Molly Malone.

Wild ingredients

  • Cockles (Cerastoderma edule): in season September to April
  • Blue mussels (Mytilus edulis): in season January to December
