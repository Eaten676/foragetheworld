Black trumpet butter

Black trumpet butter is made with wild horn of plenty, a favourite in Götaland (south) and Svealand (middle).

Open in Forage SwedenShare on WhatsApp

How do you make black trumpet butter?

Dried trumpets powdered into butter.

Wild ingredients

  • Horn of plenty (Craterellus cornucopioides): in season August to October
