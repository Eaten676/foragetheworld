Mulberry

Morus alba
Also called Shahtoot, Hippunerale, Tuti

Mulberry is a wild fruit you can forage from March to May in Northern plains, Himalaya, South India, East and Central forests. Feral trees around old silk farms and canals. Blackberry-like fruit.

When is mulberry in season in India?

Mulberry is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does mulberry grow in India?

Mulberry grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Goa, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: Northern plains, Himalaya, South India, East, Central forests.

How do you recognise mulberry?

Feral trees around old silk farms and canals. Blackberry-like fruit.

How do you harvest mulberry?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat mulberry?

Fresh, jam, sharbat.

How do you store mulberry?

Sun-dry, or make jam.

What is mulberry good for?

Vitamin C, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with mulberry?

  • Mulberry sharbat: Mash ripe mulberries, strain, cook with sugar into a syrup, and dilute with chilled water and lime.
  • Mulberry jam: Mulberries cooked with sugar and lemon until thick.
