Juniper and lingonberry game stew

Juniper and lingonberry game stew is made with wild juniper berries, lingonberry, a favourite in Norrland (north).

Open in Forage SwedenShare on WhatsApp

How do you make juniper and lingonberry game stew?

Moose or deer braised with juniper, served with lingonberries.

Wild ingredients

  • Juniper berries (Juniperus communis): in season September to November
  • Lingonberry (Vaccinium vitis-idaea): in season August to October
