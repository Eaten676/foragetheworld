Elderflower buttermilk (koldskål)

Elderflower buttermilk (koldskål) is made with wild elderflower.

Open in Forage DenmarkShare on WhatsApp

How do you make elderflower buttermilk (koldskål)?

Chilled buttermilk sweetened with elderflower, with kammerjunker biscuits.

Wild ingredients

  • Elderflower (Sambucus nigra): in season May to June
