Kachri chutney

Kachri chutney is made with wild kachri, a favourite in Thar and dry northwest.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kachri chutney?

Dried kachri ground with garlic, red chilli and salt. Fiery Rajasthani chutney.

Wild ingredients

  • Kachri (Cucumis callosus): in season October to December
