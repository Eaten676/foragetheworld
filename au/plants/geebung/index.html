Geebung

Persoonia linearis
Also called Geebung

Geebung is a wild fruit you can forage from January to April in Subtropical east coast and Temperate south-east. Shrub with soft papery bark and narrow leaves; green fruit that fall when ripe.

When is geebung in season in Australia?

Geebung is usually in season January to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does geebung grow in Australia?

Geebung grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Victoria. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east.

How do you recognise geebung?

Shrub with soft papery bark and narrow leaves; green fruit that fall when ripe.

How do you harvest geebung?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat geebung?

Soft ripe flesh sucked off the seed.

How do you store geebung?

Best fresh.
