Roasted jackfruit seeds

Roasted jackfruit seeds is made with wild jackfruit, a favourite in South India, West coast and Deccan and East.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make roasted jackfruit seeds?

Seeds roasted in hot sand or a pan, then peeled and salted. Nutty and filling.

Wild ingredients

  • Jackfruit (Artocarpus heterophyllus): in season March to July
