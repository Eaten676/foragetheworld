Blue mussels

Mytilus edulis
Also called Blue mussels, Moule bleue

Blue mussels is a wild protein you can forage from October to April in Atlantic Canada, British Columbia and Quebec. Blue-black shells in clusters on rocks and piers at low tide.

Caution. Paralytic shellfish poisoning can kill and cooking doesn't destroy it. Check your state or provincial shellfish closure map before every harvest, and get a licence where required.

When is blue mussels in season in Canada?

Blue mussels is usually in season October to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blue mussels grow in Canada?

Blue mussels grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Atlantic Canada, British Columbia, Quebec.

How do you recognise blue mussels?

Blue-black shells in clusters on rocks and piers at low tide.

How do you harvest blue mussels?

Twist mussels off rocks at low tide, taking only large ones, and check local toxin warnings first.

How do you eat blue mussels?

Steamed with wine, garlic and herbs.

How do you store blue mussels?

Eat the same day.

What is blue mussels good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with blue mussels?

  • PEI mussels: Mussels steamed with white wine, garlic and herbs.
