Bilberry tart (Heidelbeerwähe)

Bilberry tart (Heidelbeerwähe) is made with wild bilberry, a favourite in Alps and Swiss Plateau.

How do you make bilberry tart (heidelbeerwähe)?

Flat Swiss tart of bilberries in a custard of cream and egg.

Wild ingredients

  • Bilberry (Vaccinium myrtillus): in season July to August
