Sugar kelp salad (sukkertare)

Sugar kelp salad (sukkertare) is made with wild sugar kelp, a favourite in Western Norway and Northern Norway.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sugar kelp salad (sukkertare)?

Blanched kelp ribbons dressed with sesame and vinegar.

Wild ingredients

  • Sugar kelp (Saccharina latissima): in season March to June
