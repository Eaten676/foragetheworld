Bird cherry juice (heggesaft)

Bird cherry juice (heggesaft) is made with wild bird cherry, a favourite in Northern Norway.

How do you make bird cherry juice (heggesaft)?

Cooked bird cherries strained into a dark, almondy juice.

Wild ingredients

  • Bird cherry (Prunus padus): in season August
