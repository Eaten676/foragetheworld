Cashew apple juice

Cashew apple juice is made with wild cashew apple & nut, a favourite in West coast and Deccan and South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make cashew apple juice?

Squeeze ripe cashew apples, then mix the juice with a little rice kanji or salt to cut the throat-catching astringency.

Note. Fermented, it becomes feni, which is alcohol.

Wild ingredients

  • Cashew apple & nut (Anacardium occidentale): in season March to May
