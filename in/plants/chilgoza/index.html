Chilgoza pine nuts

Pinus gerardiana
Also called Chilgoza, Neoza, Ri

Chilgoza pine nuts is a wild nut you can forage from September to October in Himalaya. Kinnaur's pine with large resinous cones holding long nuts.

Caution. Cone harvest belongs to local communities. Buy, don't collect.

When is chilgoza pine nuts in season in India?

Chilgoza pine nuts is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does chilgoza pine nuts grow in India?

Chilgoza pine nuts grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise chilgoza pine nuts?

Kinnaur's pine with large resinous cones holding long nuts.

How do you harvest chilgoza pine nuts?

Cones are harvested under community rights in Kinnaur. Buy, don't collect.

How do you eat chilgoza pine nuts?

Roasted, or raw.

How do you store chilgoza pine nuts?

Keep in the shell. Lasts months.
