Gooseberry and sweet cicely fool

Gooseberry and sweet cicely fool is made with wild sweet cicely, gooseberry, a favourite in Northern England and Scotland.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make gooseberry and sweet cicely fool?

Gooseberries stewed with sweet cicely and folded into cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Sweet cicely (Myrrhis odorata): in season April to July
  • Wild gooseberry (Ribes uva-crispa): in season June to July
