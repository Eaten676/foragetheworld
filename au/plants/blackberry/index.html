Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus
Also called Blackberry

Blackberry is a wild fruit you can forage from January to March in Temperate south-east, Tasmania, South-west and Subtropical east coast. Invasive bramble along creeks and paddock edges.

Caution. Widely sprayed with herbicide as a declared weed; ask landholders and avoid browned, dying canes.

When is blackberry in season in Australia?

Blackberry is usually in season January to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blackberry grow in Australia?

Blackberry grows across Australia. Regions: Temperate south-east, Tasmania, South-west, Subtropical east coast.

How do you recognise blackberry?

Invasive bramble along creeks and paddock edges.

How do you harvest blackberry?

Pick glossy, fully black berries that come away easily; wear long sleeves and avoid low berries by dog-walking paths.

How do you eat blackberry?

Fresh, jam, crumble.

How do you store blackberry?

Freeze.

What is blackberry good for?

Vitamin C.
