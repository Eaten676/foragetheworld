Wild gooseberry

Ribes uva-crispa
Also called Gooseberry

Wild gooseberry is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July across the UK. Spiny shrub with small lobed leaves; hairy green to red berries.

When is wild gooseberry in season in the UK?

Wild gooseberry is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild gooseberry grow in the UK?

Wild gooseberry grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise wild gooseberry?

Spiny shrub with small lobed leaves; hairy green to red berries.

How do you harvest wild gooseberry?

Pick when berries soften and colour; wear gloves against the spines.

How do you eat wild gooseberry?

Fool, crumble, jam.

How do you store wild gooseberry?

Freeze.

What is wild gooseberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild gooseberry?

  • Gooseberry fool: Stewed gooseberries with elderflower folded into cream.
  • Gooseberry and sweet cicely fool: Gooseberries stewed with sweet cicely and folded into cream.
