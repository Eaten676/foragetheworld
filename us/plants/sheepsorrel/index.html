Sheep sorrel

Rumex acetosella
Also called Sheep sorrel, Sourgrass

Sheep sorrel is a wild green you can forage from April to September across the United States. Small arrow-shaped leaves with two flaring lobes at the base, in poor sandy soil; reddish flower spikes.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; small amounts.

When is sheep sorrel in season in the United States?

Sheep sorrel is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sheep sorrel grow in the United States?

Sheep sorrel grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise sheep sorrel?

Small arrow-shaped leaves with two flaring lobes at the base, in poor sandy soil; reddish flower spikes.

How do you harvest sheep sorrel?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat sheep sorrel?

Lemony garnish, or in soups and sauces for fish.

How do you store sheep sorrel?

Best fresh.

What is sheep sorrel good for?
