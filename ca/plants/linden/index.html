Linden flowers

Tilia americana
Also called Basswood, Tilleul

Linden flowers is a edible flower or tea you can forage from July to August in Ontario and Quebec. Big tree with heart-shaped leaves; dangling clusters of pale yellow honey-scented flowers under a leafy wing.

When is linden flowers in season in Canada?

Linden flowers is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does linden flowers grow in Canada?

Linden flowers grows in Ontario, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec.

How do you recognise linden flowers?

Big tree with heart-shaped leaves; dangling clusters of pale yellow honey-scented flowers under a leafy wing.

How do you harvest linden flowers?

Pick flower clusters with their leafy bract when the scent is strongest, early in flowering.

How do you eat linden flowers?

Calming tea.

How do you store linden flowers?

Dry flowers with their bract.
