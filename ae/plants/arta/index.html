Arta fruit

Calligonum comosum
Also called Arta, Abal

Arta fruit is a wild fruit you can forage from February to April in Desert, dunes and gravel plains. Leafless, broom-like shrub of high dunes with jointed green twigs, tiny scented spring flowers and bristly, ball-shaped fruits.

When is arta fruit in season in the UAE?

Arta fruit is usually in season February to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does arta fruit grow in the UAE?

Arta fruit grows in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Al Dhafra, Dubai, Sharjah, Umm Al Quwain. Regions: Desert, dunes and gravel plains.

How do you recognise arta fruit?

Leafless, broom-like shrub of high dunes with jointed green twigs, tiny scented spring flowers and bristly, ball-shaped fruits.

How do you harvest arta fruit?

Pick only ripe fruit that comes away easily, and only with the owner's permission: most trees and palms belong to a farm, a family or the municipality.

How do you eat arta fruit?

Young fruits eaten fresh for their tart taste; young twigs traditionally pounded into milk or rice.

How do you store arta fruit?

Best fresh.

What is arta fruit good for?

Vitamin C.
