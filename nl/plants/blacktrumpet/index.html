Horn of plenty

Craterellus cornucopioides
Also called Hoorn des overvloeds

Horn of plenty is a wild mushroom you can forage from August to November in East and South. Thin, hollow, black-grey trumpets with smooth undersides, hidden in leaf litter under beech and oak.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: learn it in person with an experienced forager first, and always cook it.

When is horn of plenty in season in the Netherlands?

Horn of plenty is usually in season August to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does horn of plenty grow in the Netherlands?

Horn of plenty grows in Drenthe, Flevoland, Friesland, Gelderland, Groningen, Limburg, North Brabant, Overijssel, Zeeland. Regions: East, South.

How do you recognise horn of plenty?

Thin, hollow, black-grey trumpets with smooth undersides, hidden in leaf litter under beech and oak.

How do you harvest horn of plenty?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat horn of plenty?

Sautéed, in risotto, or dried and powdered.

How do you store horn of plenty?

Dry.

What is horn of plenty good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.
