Nopales salad

Nopales salad is made with wild prickly pear, a favourite in Southwest and California.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nopales salad?

Diced, de-spined pads boiled and tossed with tomato, onion, coriander and lime.

Wild ingredients

  • Prickly pear (Opuntia): in season July to October
