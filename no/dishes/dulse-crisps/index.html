Dulse crisps (søl)

Dulse crisps (søl) is made with wild dulse, a favourite in Western Norway and Northern Norway.

Open in Forage NorwayShare on WhatsApp

How do you make dulse crisps (søl)?

Dried dulse toasted in a dry pan until crisp.

Wild ingredients

  • Dulse (Palmaria palmata): in season April to September
