Porcini pasta

Porcini pasta is made with wild porcini, a favourite in Rocky Mountains and Pacific Northwest.

How do you make porcini pasta?

Porcini sautéed with garlic, tossed with pasta and parmesan.

Wild ingredients

  • Porcini (Boletus edulis): in season August to October
