Cloudberry jam with waffles (hjortronsylt)

Cloudberry jam with waffles (hjortronsylt) is made with wild cloudberry, a favourite in Norrland (north).

How do you make cloudberry jam with waffles (hjortronsylt)?

Warm cloudberry jam over cream and waffles or ice cream.

Wild ingredients

  • Cloudberry (Rubus chamaemorus): in season July to August
