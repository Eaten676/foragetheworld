Himalayan berry juice

Himalayan berry juice is made with wild hisalu, kafal, kilmora, ghain, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make himalayan berry juice?

Crush ripe wild berries with a little water, strain, then add honey and a pinch of salt.

Wild ingredients

  • Hisalu (Rubus ellipticus): in season April to June
  • Kafal (Myrica esculenta): in season April to June
  • Kilmora (Berberis asiatica): in season May to July
  • Ghain (Elaeagnus umbellata): in season July to August
