Watercress

Nasturtium officinale
Also called Watercress, Wātakirihi

Watercress is a wild green you can forage from August to December across New Zealand. Glossy rounded leaflets in clean, cold, flowing streams.

Caution. Only from clean water, and cook it.

When is watercress in season in New Zealand?

Watercress is usually in season August to December. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does watercress grow in New Zealand?

Watercress grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise watercress?

Glossy rounded leaflets in clean, cold, flowing streams.

How do you harvest watercress?

Cut stems above the water line with scissors, only from clean, flowing water away from livestock.

How do you eat watercress?

Boil-up, soup.

How do you store watercress?

Best fresh.

What is watercress good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with watercress?

  • Pork and pūhā boil-up: Pork bones, potatoes, kūmara and pūhā or watercress simmered with dumplings.
  • Watercress and pork boil-up: Pork bones, potatoes and watercress, the Waikato way.
