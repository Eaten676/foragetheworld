Kumat

Senegalia senegal
Also called Kumat, Kumtha

Kumat is a wild seed or grain you can forage from November to January in Thar and dry northwest. Thorny desert acacia. Its seeds go into Rajasthan's famous panchkuta.

When is kumat in season in India?

Kumat is usually in season November to January. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kumat grow in India?

Kumat grows in Gujarat, Rajasthan. Regions: Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise kumat?

Thorny desert acacia. Its seeds go into Rajasthan's famous panchkuta.

How do you harvest kumat?

Wait until seed heads or pods are dry and ripe, then strip or shake them into a bag or onto a cloth before they shatter. Thresh, winnow and sun-dry the seeds well.

How do you eat kumat?

Panchkuta with ker, sangri, kachri and lasoda.

How do you store kumat?

Boil and sun-dry the seeds.

What is kumat good for?

Protein, Calories.

What can you cook with kumat?

  • Panchkuta: The Thar's five-ingredient desert curry, cooked dry with spices and oil.
