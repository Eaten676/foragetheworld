Saguaro fruit

Carnegiea gigantea
Also called Saguaro fruit

Saguaro fruit is a wild fruit you can forage from June to July in Southwest. The giant branching cactus of the Sonoran Desert; red-pulped fruit split open atop the arms in early summer.

Caution. Only gather fallen fruit or use a long pole; saguaros are protected in Arizona, so never damage the plant.

When is saguaro fruit in season in the United States?

Saguaro fruit is usually in season June to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does saguaro fruit grow in the United States?

Saguaro fruit grows in Arizona, Nevada, New Mexico, Texas, Utah. Regions: Southwest.

How do you recognise saguaro fruit?

The giant branching cactus of the Sonoran Desert; red-pulped fruit split open atop the arms in early summer.

How do you harvest saguaro fruit?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat saguaro fruit?

Fresh pulp, or boiled down into syrup and jam, a Tohono O'odham harvest tradition.

How do you store saguaro fruit?

Dry pulp or make syrup.

What is saguaro fruit good for?

Calories, Vitamin C.

What can you cook with saguaro fruit?

  • Saguaro fruit syrup: Saguaro pulp boiled down into a dark, fig-like syrup.
