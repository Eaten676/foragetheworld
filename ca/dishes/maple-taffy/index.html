Maple taffy (tire d'érable)

Maple taffy (tire d'érable) is made with wild maple sap, a favourite in Quebec.

Open in Forage CanadaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make maple taffy (tire d'érable)?

Hot maple syrup poured on snow and rolled onto a stick.

Wild ingredients

  • Maple sap (Acer saccharum): in season February to March
