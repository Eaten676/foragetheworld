Panchkuta

Panchkuta is made with wild ker, khejri (sangri), kachri, kumat, lasoda, a favourite in Thar and dry northwest.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make panchkuta?

The Thar's five-ingredient desert curry, cooked dry with spices and oil.

Wild ingredients

  • Ker (Capparis decidua): in season April to June
  • Khejri (sangri) (Prosopis cineraria): in season March to May
  • Kachri (Cucumis callosus): in season October to December
  • Kumat (Senegalia senegal): in season November to January
  • Lasoda (Cordia myxa): in season April to June
