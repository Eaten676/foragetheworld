Cane shoots

Calamus tenuis
Also called Bet gaaj, Chural

Cane shoots is a root or shoot you can forage from July to September in Northeast and South India. Spiny climbing rattan palm. The soft white core of young shoots is a bitter Assamese delicacy.

Caution. Heavy spines. Peel carefully with a knife.

When is cane shoots in season in India?

Cane shoots is usually in season July to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cane shoots grow in India?

Cane shoots grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Goa, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Puducherry, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura. Regions: Northeast, South India.

How do you recognise cane shoots?

Spiny climbing rattan palm. The soft white core of young shoots is a bitter Assamese delicacy.

How do you harvest cane shoots?

Cut young shoots with a sharp knife, wearing gloves against the spines.

How do you eat cane shoots?

Bet gaaj bhaji, or roasted in ashes and mashed into pitika.

How do you store cane shoots?

Use fresh.

What can you cook with cane shoots?

  • Bet gaaj bhaji: Bitter cane shoot core stir-fried with potato and mustard oil.
