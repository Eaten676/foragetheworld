Wood sorrel

Oxalis stricta
Also called Yellow wood sorrel, Oxalide

Wood sorrel is a wild green you can forage from May to November in Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada and British Columbia. Small clover-like plant with heart-shaped leaflets that fold at night and five-petalled yellow flowers. Lemony.

Caution. High in oxalic acid; enjoy in small amounts.

When is wood sorrel in season in Canada?

Wood sorrel is usually in season May to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wood sorrel grow in Canada?

Wood sorrel grows in British Columbia, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec. Regions: Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, British Columbia.

How do you recognise wood sorrel?

Small clover-like plant with heart-shaped leaflets that fold at night and five-petalled yellow flowers. Lemony.

How do you harvest wood sorrel?

Pinch off a few leaves and flowers from large patches; never pull up the creeping roots.

How do you eat wood sorrel?

A sour garnish on salads and fish, or a lemonade-like tea.

How do you store wood sorrel?

Best fresh.

What is wood sorrel good for?

Vitamin C.
