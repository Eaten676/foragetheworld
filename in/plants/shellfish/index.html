Clams & shellfish

Meretrix casta
Also called Marvai, Tisrya, Kakka

Clams & shellfish is a wild protein you can forage from October to March in South India, West coast and Deccan and East. Clams on estuary mudflats and coastal sand.

Caution. Skip during the monsoon ban and near towns or drains. Discard any that don't open after cooking.

When is clams & shellfish in season in India?

Clams & shellfish is usually in season October to March. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does clams & shellfish grow in India?

Clams & shellfish grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Goa, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East.

How do you recognise clams & shellfish?

Clams on estuary mudflats and coastal sand.

How do you harvest clams & shellfish?

Dig at low tide in clean estuary mudflats, taking only large clams, and never during the monsoon ban.

How do you eat clams & shellfish?

Sukka, curry, tisryo masala.

How do you store clams & shellfish?

Cook the same day.

What is clams & shellfish good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with clams & shellfish?

  • Tisryo masala: Clams on the half shell cooked dry with coconut and Goan spices.
