Lemon-scented tea tree

Leptospermum petersonii
Also called Lemon-scented tea tree

Lemon-scented tea tree is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round in Subtropical east coast and Temperate south-east. Shrub or small tree with narrow, strongly lemon-scented leaves and white flowers.

When is lemon-scented tea tree in season in Australia?

Lemon-scented tea tree is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does lemon-scented tea tree grow in Australia?

Lemon-scented tea tree grows in Australian Capital Territory, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia, Victoria. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Temperate south-east.

How do you recognise lemon-scented tea tree?

Shrub or small tree with narrow, strongly lemon-scented leaves and white flowers.

How do you harvest lemon-scented tea tree?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat lemon-scented tea tree?

Leaves as a lemony tea or in cooking.

How do you store lemon-scented tea tree?

Dry leaves.

What can you cook with lemon-scented tea tree?

  • Lemon tea tree tea: Fresh leaves steeped in hot water.
