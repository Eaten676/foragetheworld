Shaggy inkcap

Coprinus comatus
Also called Shaggy inkcap, Lawyer's wig

Shaggy inkcap is a wild mushroom you can forage from May to November across the UK. Tall white cylinder with shaggy scales on lawns and verges; gills turn to black ink within hours.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: learn it in person first and always cook it. The related common inkcap is poisonous with alcohol.

When is shaggy inkcap in season in the UK?

Shaggy inkcap is usually in season May to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does shaggy inkcap grow in the UK?

Shaggy inkcap grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise shaggy inkcap?

Tall white cylinder with shaggy scales on lawns and verges; gills turn to black ink within hours.

How do you harvest shaggy inkcap?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat shaggy inkcap?

Cook the same day while gills are white: in butter or soup.

How do you store shaggy inkcap?

Doesn't keep.

What can be confused with shaggy inkcap?

Common inkcap (Coprinopsis atramentaria), rated harmful. Shaggy inkcap is tall, white and covered in shaggy scales. The common inkcap is smaller, smooth and grey-brown, in clusters; with alcohol, even days later, it causes violent sickness.

What is shaggy inkcap good for?

Protein.
