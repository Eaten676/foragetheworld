Black walnut

Juglans nigra
Also called Black walnut, Noyer noir

Black walnut is a wild nut you can forage from September to October in Ontario. Tall tree with long leaves of many leaflets and tennis-ball-sized green husks that stain hands black.

When is black walnut in season in Canada?

Black walnut is usually in season September to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does black walnut grow in Canada?

Black walnut grows in Ontario. Regions: Ontario.

How do you recognise black walnut?

Tall tree with long leaves of many leaflets and tennis-ball-sized green husks that stain hands black.

How do you harvest black walnut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat black walnut?

Baking, ice cream, toasted.

How do you store black walnut?

Hull, dry in shell, crack as needed.

What is black walnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.
