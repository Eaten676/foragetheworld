Appe midi uppinakai

Appe midi uppinakai is made with wild mango, a favourite in South India.

How do you make appe midi uppinakai?

Whole tender wild mangoes brined with mustard, chilli and turmeric. Malnad's prized pickle.

Wild ingredients

  • Wild mango (Mangifera indica): in season April to June
