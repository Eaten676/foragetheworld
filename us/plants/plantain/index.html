Broadleaf plantain

Plantago major
Also called Plantain, White man's foot

Broadleaf plantain is a wild green you can forage from April to September across the United States. Rosette of broad oval leaves with strong parallel veins and a stringy stem, in lawns and paths.

When is broadleaf plantain in season in the United States?

Broadleaf plantain is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does broadleaf plantain grow in the United States?

Broadleaf plantain grows across the United States. Regions: Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Great Plains, Southwest, Rocky Mountains, Pacific Northwest, California, Alaska.

How do you recognise broadleaf plantain?

Rosette of broad oval leaves with strong parallel veins and a stringy stem, in lawns and paths.

How do you harvest broadleaf plantain?

Pick young, soft leaves from the centre of the rosette, and green flower buds before they open.

How do you eat broadleaf plantain?

Very young leaves raw or steamed; seeds on the spikes can be eaten.

How do you store broadleaf plantain?

Dry leaves.

What is broadleaf plantain good for?

Vitamin A, Iron & calcium.
