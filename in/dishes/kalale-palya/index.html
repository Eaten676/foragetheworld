Kalale palya

Kalale palya is made with wild bamboo shoots, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make kalale palya?

Boiled bamboo shoots cooked dry with coconut and chilli. A Malnad monsoon dish.

Note. Boil and discard the water first.

Wild ingredients

  • Bamboo shoots (Bambusa): in season June to September
