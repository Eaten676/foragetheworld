Shepherd's purse

Capsella bursa-pastoris
Also called Hyrdetaske

Shepherd's purse is a wild green you can forage from February to October across Denmark. Small weed of paths and gardens with a dandelion-like rosette, white flowers and heart-shaped seed pods.

When is shepherd's purse in season in Denmark?

Shepherd's purse is usually in season February to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does shepherd's purse grow in Denmark?

Shepherd's purse grows across Denmark. Regions: Jutland, Funen and southern islands, Zealand, Bornholm.

How do you recognise shepherd's purse?

Small weed of paths and gardens with a dandelion-like rosette, white flowers and heart-shaped seed pods.

How do you harvest shepherd's purse?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat shepherd's purse?

Peppery young leaves raw or cooked; used in Chinese dumplings.

How do you store shepherd's purse?

Best fresh.

What is shepherd's purse good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.
