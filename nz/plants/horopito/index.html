Horopito

Pseudowintera colorata
Also called Horopito, Pepper tree

Horopito is a edible flower or tea you can forage all year round across New Zealand. Shrub of cool bush with leathery, red-blotched yellow-green leaves that burn the tongue like pepper.

When is horopito in season in New Zealand?

Horopito is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does horopito grow in New Zealand?

Horopito grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise horopito?

Shrub of cool bush with leathery, red-blotched yellow-green leaves that burn the tongue like pepper.

How do you harvest horopito?

Pick a few leaves from each shrub and dry them.

How do you eat horopito?

Dried and ground as bush pepper.

How do you store horopito?

Dry.

What can you cook with horopito?

  • Horopito salt: Dried horopito ground with sea salt for meat and eggs.
  • Horopito fry bread (parāoa parai): Fried bread dough with a pinch of ground horopito.
