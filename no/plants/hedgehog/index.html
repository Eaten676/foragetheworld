Hedgehog mushroom

Hydnum repandum
Also called Piggsopp

Hedgehog mushroom is a wild mushroom you can forage from August to October across Norway. Buff to pale orange cap with soft spines underneath instead of gills or pores; grows on the woodland floor.

Caution. The spines make it easy to recognise; no dangerous lookalike has spines. Cook it.

When is hedgehog mushroom in season in Norway?

Hedgehog mushroom is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does hedgehog mushroom grow in Norway?

Hedgehog mushroom grows across Norway. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway, Western Norway, Trøndelag, Northern Norway.

How do you recognise hedgehog mushroom?

Buff to pale orange cap with soft spines underneath instead of gills or pores; grows on the woodland floor.

How do you harvest hedgehog mushroom?

Cut at the base and brush off the spines' debris; leave small ones to spread spores.

How do you eat hedgehog mushroom?

Fried in butter, in sauces and pies.

How do you store hedgehog mushroom?

Dry-fry and freeze.

What is hedgehog mushroom good for?

Protein.
