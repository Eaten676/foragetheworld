Makhana kheer

Makhana kheer is made with wild makhana, chironji, a favourite in East and Northern plains.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make makhana kheer?

Roasted fox nuts simmered in milk with sugar, cardamom and chironji.

Wild ingredients

  • Makhana (Euryale ferox): in season August to October
  • Chironji (Buchanania lanzan): in season April to May
