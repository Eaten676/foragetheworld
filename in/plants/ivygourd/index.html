Ivy gourd

Coccinia grandis
Also called Kundru, Tondli, Thonde, Kovakkai

Ivy gourd is a wild green you can forage from March to November in South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests and Northern plains. Climber smothering fences, with five-lobed leaves and small striped green fruit. Leaves are edible too.

When is ivy gourd in season in India?

Ivy gourd is usually in season March to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does ivy gourd grow in India?

Ivy gourd grows in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Lakshadweep, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, West Bengal. Regions: South India, West coast and Deccan, East, Central forests, Northern plains.

How do you recognise ivy gourd?

Climber smothering fences, with five-lobed leaves and small striped green fruit. Leaves are edible too.

How do you harvest ivy gourd?

Pinch off the young tips and top leaves with your fingers or scissors. Leave the lower leaves and roots so the plant regrows. Pick in the morning, away from roads and drains.

How do you eat ivy gourd?

Young green fruit as sabzi or poriyal. Leaves in dal.

How do you store ivy gourd?

Slice and sun-dry.

What can be confused with ivy gourd?

Lollipop climber (Diplocyclos palmatus), rated toxic. Lollipop climber leaves are cut deeply into five narrow lobes. Ivy gourd leaves are only shallowly lobed. Its fruit is round, bright red with white stripes, and grows in clusters. Ivy gourd fruit is oval, single, and green with pale stripes. Ivy gourd has large white bell-shaped flowers. Lollipop climber has tiny yellowish ones.

What is ivy gourd good for?

Vitamin A.

What can you cook with ivy gourd?

  • Tondli bhaji: Ivy gourd slices cooked with peanuts, coconut and goda masala.
