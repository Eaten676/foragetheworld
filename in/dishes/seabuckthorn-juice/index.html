Seabuckthorn juice

Seabuckthorn juice is made with wild seabuckthorn, a favourite in Himalaya.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make seabuckthorn juice?

Crush berries, strain through cloth, and sweeten with honey. Very sour and full of vitamin C.

Wild ingredients

  • Seabuckthorn (Hippophae rhamnoides): in season August to October
