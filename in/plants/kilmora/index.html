Kilmora

Berberis asiatica
Also called Kilmora, Kingor, Chutro

Kilmora is a wild fruit you can forage from May to July in Himalaya. Spiny Himalayan shrub with yellow wood and blue-black berries.

When is kilmora in season in India?

Kilmora is usually in season May to July. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does kilmora grow in India?

Kilmora grows in Arunachal Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Uttarakhand. Regions: Himalaya.

How do you recognise kilmora?

Spiny Himalayan shrub with yellow wood and blue-black berries.

How do you harvest kilmora?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat kilmora?

Ripe berries fresh, or jam.

How do you store kilmora?

Sun-dry like raisins.

What is kilmora good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with kilmora?

  • Himalayan berry juice: Crush ripe wild berries with a little water, strain, then add honey and a pinch of salt.
  • Himalayan berry jam: Wild hill berries cooked with sugar into jam.
