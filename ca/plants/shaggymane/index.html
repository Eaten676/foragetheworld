Shaggy mane

Coprinus comatus
Also called Shaggy mane, Coprin chevelu

Shaggy mane is a wild mushroom you can forage from June to October across Canada. Tall white cylinder with shaggy scales on lawns, trails and disturbed ground; gills turn to black ink within hours.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: learn it in person with an experienced forager before eating, and always cook it. The related common ink cap is poisonous with alcohol.

When is shaggy mane in season in Canada?

Shaggy mane is usually in season June to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does shaggy mane grow in Canada?

Shaggy mane grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise shaggy mane?

Tall white cylinder with shaggy scales on lawns, trails and disturbed ground; gills turn to black ink within hours.

How do you harvest shaggy mane?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat shaggy mane?

Cook the same day it's picked, while gills are still white: sautéed or in soup.

How do you store shaggy mane?

Cook immediately; doesn't keep.

What can be confused with shaggy mane?

Common ink cap (Coprinopsis atramentaria), rated harmful. Shaggy mane is tall, white and covered in shaggy scales. The ink cap is smaller, smooth and grey-brown, in clusters. Ink cap with alcohol, even days later, causes violent sickness.

What is shaggy mane good for?

Protein.
