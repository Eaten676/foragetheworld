Sweet woodruff

Galium odoratum
Also called Myske

Sweet woodruff is a edible flower or tea you can forage from May to June in Eastern Norway and Southern Norway. Low woodland plant with whorls of narrow leaves and white star flowers; smells of hay and vanilla when wilted.

Caution. Contains coumarin; use only a small bunch per litre.

When is sweet woodruff in season in Norway?

Sweet woodruff is usually in season May to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does sweet woodruff grow in Norway?

Sweet woodruff grows in Agder, Akershus, Buskerud, Innlandet, Oslo, Rogaland, Telemark, Vestfold, Østfold. Regions: Eastern Norway, Southern Norway.

How do you recognise sweet woodruff?

Low woodland plant with whorls of narrow leaves and white star flowers; smells of hay and vanilla when wilted.

How do you harvest sweet woodruff?

Snip stems just before flowering and let them wilt for a few hours to release the vanilla scent.

How do you eat sweet woodruff?

Woodruff syrup and May punch; wilt before using.

How do you store sweet woodruff?

Dry.
