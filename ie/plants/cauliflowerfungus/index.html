Cauliflower fungus

Sparassis crispa
Also called Cauliflower fungus

Cauliflower fungus is a wild mushroom you can forage from August to November across Ireland. A cream-coloured 'brain' or sea sponge of flat, curly lobes at the base of pine trees in autumn.

Caution. Intermediate mushroom: learn it in person first.

When is cauliflower fungus in season in Ireland?

Cauliflower fungus is usually in season August to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cauliflower fungus grow in Ireland?

Cauliflower fungus grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise cauliflower fungus?

A cream-coloured 'brain' or sea sponge of flat, curly lobes at the base of pine trees in autumn.

How do you harvest cauliflower fungus?

Twist or cut mushrooms at the base, brush off soil in the woods and carry them in a basket, never a plastic bag. Only take ones you're 100% sure of, leave old and very young ones, and follow local limits.

How do you eat cauliflower fungus?

Cleaned well and sliced into soups, stir-fries or fried like noodles.

How do you store cauliflower fungus?

Cook and freeze.

What is cauliflower fungus good for?

Protein.
