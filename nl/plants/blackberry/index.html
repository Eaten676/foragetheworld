Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus
Also called Braam

Blackberry is a wild fruit you can forage from August to September across the Netherlands. Thorny arching brambles with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen red to black.

When is blackberry in season in the Netherlands?

Blackberry is usually in season August to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does blackberry grow in the Netherlands?

Blackberry grows across the Netherlands. Regions: Coast, dunes and Wadden, West, East, South.

How do you recognise blackberry?

Thorny arching brambles with leaves in threes or fives; berries ripen red to black.

How do you harvest blackberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat blackberry?

Fresh, crumble, jam, jelly.

How do you store blackberry?

Freeze on trays, then bag.

What is blackberry good for?

Vitamin C, Vitamin A.

What can you cook with blackberry?

  • Blackberry jam (bramenjam): Blackberries cooked with sugar and lemon.
  • Blackberry crumble (bramencrumble): Blackberries under an oat crumble.
