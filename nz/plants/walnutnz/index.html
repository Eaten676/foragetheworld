Walnut

Juglans regia
Also called Walnut

Walnut is a wild nut you can forage from March to May in Canterbury, Otago and Southland, Top of the South and East Coast and Hawke's Bay. Old farm and roadside trees left by settlers; green husks split to drop nuts.

When is walnut in season in New Zealand?

Walnut is usually in season March to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does walnut grow in New Zealand?

Walnut grows in Canterbury, Gisborne, Hawke's Bay, Marlborough, Nelson, Otago, Southland, Tasman. Regions: Canterbury, Otago and Southland, Top of the South, East Coast and Hawke's Bay.

How do you recognise walnut?

Old farm and roadside trees left by settlers; green husks split to drop nuts.

How do you harvest walnut?

Gather nuts from the ground once they fall, discarding any with holes or mould. Leave plenty for wildlife, which depends on them for winter.

How do you eat walnut?

Raw, in baking.

How do you store walnut?

Dry in the shell.

What is walnut good for?

Fat, Protein, Calories.
