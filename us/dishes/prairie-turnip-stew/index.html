Prairie turnip stew

Prairie turnip stew is made with wild prairie turnip, a favourite in Great Plains.

Open in Forage United StatesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make prairie turnip stew?

Dried prairie turnips simmered with bison or beef, after Lakota tradition.

Wild ingredients

  • Prairie turnip (Pediomelum esculentum): in season June to July
