Tuangi cockles

Austrovenus stutchburyi
Also called Tuangi cockles

Tuangi cockles is a wild protein you can forage all year round across New Zealand. Ridged round cockles just under the sand of harbour flats.

Caution. Many harbours are closed or polluted: check MPI warnings and limits.

When is tuangi cockles in season in New Zealand?

Tuangi cockles is usually in season all year round. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does tuangi cockles grow in New Zealand?

Tuangi cockles grows across New Zealand. Regions: Northland and Auckland, Central North Island, East Coast and Hawke's Bay, Wellington and Manawatū, Top of the South, West Coast, Canterbury, Otago and Southland.

How do you recognise tuangi cockles?

Ridged round cockles just under the sand of harbour flats.

How do you harvest tuangi cockles?

Check local rules, licences, size limits and water-quality warnings before taking any shellfish or fish.

How do you eat tuangi cockles?

Steamed open, or in chowder.

How do you store tuangi cockles?

Eat the same day.

What is tuangi cockles good for?

Protein, Iron & calcium.

What can you cook with tuangi cockles?

  • Steamed tuangi with kawakawa: Cockles steamed with kawakawa and butter.
