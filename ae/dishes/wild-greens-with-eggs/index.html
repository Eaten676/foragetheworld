Wild greens with eggs

Wild greens with eggs is made with wild green amaranth, sow thistle, nettle-leaved goosefoot.

Open in Forage United Arab EmiratesShare on WhatsApp

How do you make wild greens with eggs?

Wilted wild greens cooked with onion and spices, then eggs scrambled in.

Wild ingredients

  • Green amaranth (Amaranthus viridis): in season April to November
  • Sow thistle (Sonchus oleraceus): in season November to April
  • Nettle-leaved goosefoot (Chenopodium murale): in season November to April
Seasons and ranges are approximate and shift with weather and altitude. Identify every plant with an experienced local before eating it. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, credited on each page.
