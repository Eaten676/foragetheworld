Wild raspberry

Rubus idaeus
Also called Wild raspberry

Wild raspberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across the UK. Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

When is wild raspberry in season in the UK?

Wild raspberry is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild raspberry grow in the UK?

Wild raspberry grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise wild raspberry?

Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

How do you harvest wild raspberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat wild raspberry?

Fresh, jam, cordial.

How do you store wild raspberry?

Freeze.

What is wild raspberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with wild raspberry?

  • Cranachan with raspberries: Toasted oats, whisky, honey, cream and wild raspberries.
