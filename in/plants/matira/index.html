Matira

Citrullus lanatus
Also called Matira, Kalinga

Matira is a wild fruit you can forage from August to October in Thar and dry northwest. Small wild watermelon of the desert. Water source and food in one.

When is matira in season in India?

Matira is usually in season August to October. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does matira grow in India?

Matira grows in Gujarat, Rajasthan. Regions: Thar and dry northwest.

How do you recognise matira?

Small wild watermelon of the desert. Water source and food in one.

How do you harvest matira?

Pick fruit that comes away easily, or gather freshly fallen fruit. Don't break branches, and leave plenty for birds and animals.

How do you eat matira?

Flesh fresh. Seeds dried and ground into flour for sogra rotis.

How do you store matira?

Dry and grind the seeds.

What can be confused with matira?

Bitter apple (Citrullus colocynthis), rated toxic. Bitter apple is fiercely bitter and a strong purgative. Even desert herders avoid it. Its fruits are tennis-ball sized, smooth, green with yellow mottling, and turn yellow as they dry. Its vines and leaves look very like matira, so only pick melons from vines a local has confirmed.

What is matira good for?

Calories.

What can you cook with matira?

  • Matira juice: Blend the flesh with a pinch of black salt and mint, and drink straight away.
  • Sogra: Thick bajra rotis made with ground matira seeds. Desert winter fare.
