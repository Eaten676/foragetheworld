Wild raspberry

Rubus idaeus
Also called Wild raspberry, Sú craobh

Wild raspberry is a wild fruit you can forage from July to August across Ireland. Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

When is wild raspberry in season in Ireland?

Wild raspberry is usually in season July to August. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does wild raspberry grow in Ireland?

Wild raspberry grows across Ireland. Regions: Munster, Leinster, Connacht, Ulster (Republic).

How do you recognise wild raspberry?

Upright canes in clearings with leaves white underneath; red berries pull off hollow.

How do you harvest wild raspberry?

Pick berries that slip off their core with no tug, into a shallow container.

How do you eat wild raspberry?

Fresh, jam, cordial.

How do you store wild raspberry?

Freeze.

What is wild raspberry good for?

Vitamin C.
