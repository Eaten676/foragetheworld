Ribwort plantain

Plantago lanceolata
Also called Spitzwegerich

Ribwort plantain is a wild green you can forage from April to September across Germany. Narrow ribbed leaves in a rosette and dark flower heads on long stalks; young flower buds taste of mushroom.

When is ribwort plantain in season in Germany?

Ribwort plantain is usually in season April to September. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does ribwort plantain grow in Germany?

Ribwort plantain grows across Germany. Regions: North German lowlands and coast, West, East, South and the Alps.

How do you recognise ribwort plantain?

Narrow ribbed leaves in a rosette and dark flower heads on long stalks; young flower buds taste of mushroom.

How do you harvest ribwort plantain?

Pick young, soft leaves from the centre of the rosette, and green flower buds before they open.

How do you eat ribwort plantain?

Young leaves cooked; buds sautéed.

How do you store ribwort plantain?

Dry leaves for tea.

What is ribwort plantain good for?

Vitamin A.
