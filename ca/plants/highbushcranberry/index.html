Highbush cranberry

Viburnum trilobum
Also called Highbush cranberry, Viorne trilobée, Pimbina

Highbush cranberry is a wild fruit you can forage from September to November across Canada. Shrub with three-lobed maple-like leaves and clusters of translucent red berries with one flat seed.

Caution. Cook it; raw berries can upset the stomach.

When is highbush cranberry in season in Canada?

Highbush cranberry is usually in season September to November. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does highbush cranberry grow in Canada?

Highbush cranberry grows across Canada. Regions: British Columbia, Prairies, Ontario, Quebec, Atlantic Canada, The North.

How do you recognise highbush cranberry?

Shrub with three-lobed maple-like leaves and clusters of translucent red berries with one flat seed.

How do you harvest highbush cranberry?

Pick only fully ripe fruit that comes away easily, into a shallow container so it doesn't crush. Leave plenty on the plant for birds and wildlife.

How do you eat highbush cranberry?

Jelly and sauce; better after frost.

How do you store highbush cranberry?

Jelly.

What is highbush cranberry good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with highbush cranberry?

  • Pimbina jelly: Highbush cranberry jelly, a Prairie and Manitoba favourite.
