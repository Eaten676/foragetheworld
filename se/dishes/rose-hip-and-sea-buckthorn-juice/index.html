How do you make rose hip and sea buckthorn juice?

Vitamin-packed autumn juice, sweetened with honey.

Wild ingredients

  • Rose hips (Rosa canina): in season August to October
  • Sea buckthorn (Hippophae rhamnoides): in season August to October
