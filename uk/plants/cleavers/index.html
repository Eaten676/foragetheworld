Cleavers

Galium aparine
Also called Cleavers, Goosegrass

Cleavers is a wild green you can forage from February to May across the UK. Sticky, scrambling plant with whorls of narrow leaves along square stems; clings to clothes.

When is cleavers in season in the UK?

Cleavers is usually in season February to May. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does cleavers grow in the UK?

Cleavers grows across the UK. Regions: South East England, South West England, Midlands and East, Northern England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland.

How do you recognise cleavers?

Sticky, scrambling plant with whorls of narrow leaves along square stems; clings to clothes.

How do you harvest cleavers?

Pick the soft tops in early spring before they get bristly.

How do you eat cleavers?

Young tips steamed or blended into green juice and water.

How do you store cleavers?

Best fresh.

What is cleavers good for?

Vitamin C.
