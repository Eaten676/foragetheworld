Nungu drink

Nungu drink is made with wild palmyra palm, a favourite in South India.

Open in Forage IndiaShare on WhatsApp

How do you make nungu drink?

Peel and chop tender nungu, then mix with its own juice, chilled milk or water, and a little rose syrup or sugar.

Wild ingredients

  • Palmyra palm (Borassus flabellifer): in season April to January
