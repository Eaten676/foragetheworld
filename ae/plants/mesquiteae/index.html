Mesquite pods

Prosopis juliflora
Also called Mesquite, Ghaf bahri

Mesquite pods is a wild seed or grain you can forage from May to August in Desert, dunes and gravel plains, Cities, farms and oases and Gulf coast. Invasive, wide-spreading thorny tree from the Americas, now everywhere on sand plains and roadsides, with paired leaflets and flat pods that ripen yellow.

Caution. Only use ripe, dry, mould-free pods; mouldy pods can carry toxins. Picking is fine, but don't spread the seeds.

How do you harvest mesquite pods?

Shake or strip ripe seed heads into a bag when they rattle and fall easily. Winnow out the chaff in a breeze.

How do you eat mesquite pods?

Dry ripe pods and grind into a sweet flour for breads and drinks.

How do you store mesquite pods?

Store dry flour airtight.
