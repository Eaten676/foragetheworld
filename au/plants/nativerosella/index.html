Native rosella

Hibiscus heterophyllus
Also called Native rosella

Native rosella is a edible flower or tea you can forage from October to April in Subtropical east coast and Tropical north. Rainforest-edge shrub with large white to yellow hibiscus flowers with a dark centre.

When is native rosella in season in Australia?

Native rosella is usually in season October to April. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does native rosella grow in Australia?

Native rosella grows in New South Wales, Northern Territory, Queensland, Western Australia. Regions: Subtropical east coast, Tropical north.

How do you recognise native rosella?

Rainforest-edge shrub with large white to yellow hibiscus flowers with a dark centre.

How do you harvest native rosella?

Pick flowers on a dry, sunny morning once the dew has gone, when the scent is strongest. Take a few heads from each plant so it can still set fruit.

How do you eat native rosella?

Flowers and buds in salads, jams and cordials; young leaves cooked.

How do you store native rosella?

Jam.

What is native rosella good for?

Vitamin C.

What can you cook with native rosella?

  • Native rosella jam: Hibiscus flowers cooked into a bright red jam.
