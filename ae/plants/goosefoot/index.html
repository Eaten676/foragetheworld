Nettle-leaved goosefoot

Chenopodium murale
Also called Goosefoot

Nettle-leaved goosefoot is a wild green you can forage from November to April in Cities, farms and oases and Gulf coast. Weedy plant of farms and gardens with shiny, toothed, diamond-shaped leaves and clusters of tiny green flowers; no sting.

Caution. Contains oxalates and nitrates: cook it, eat in moderation, and skip plants from heavily fertilised fields.

How do you harvest nettle-leaved goosefoot?

Pick a few young leaves by hand, only from plants on farms, in gardens or on land where you have permission. Federal law protects wild plants, so never uproot anything.
