Sloe gin

Sloe gin is made with wild sloe.

Open in Forage United KingdomShare on WhatsApp

How do you make sloe gin?

Pricked sloes steeped in gin with sugar from October until Christmas.

Wild ingredients

  • Sloe (Prunus spinosa): in season September to November
