Field garlic

Allium vineale
Also called Crow garlic, Ail des champs

Field garlic is a wild green you can forage from March to June in Ontario. Thin, hollow, chive-like leaves smelling strongly of garlic, in lawns and fields.

When is field garlic in season in Canada?

Field garlic is usually in season March to June. Timing shifts with weather, latitude and altitude.

Where does field garlic grow in Canada?

Field garlic grows in Ontario. Regions: Ontario.

How do you recognise field garlic?

Thin, hollow, chive-like leaves smelling strongly of garlic, in lawns and fields.

How do you harvest field garlic?

Pinch off young tips and top leaves before the plant flowers, when they are tender and mild. Leave the roots and lower leaves so it regrows. Pick away from roads, sprayed verges and dog-walking paths.

How do you eat field garlic?

Use like chives.

How do you store field garlic?

Freeze chopped.

What can be confused with field garlic?

Lily of the valley (Convallaria majalis), rated deadly. Ramps and wild onions smell of onion or garlic. Lily of the valley smells of nothing when crushed. Lily of the valley has nodding white bell flowers in a row.

What is field garlic good for?

Vitamin C.
